🌗第 1 课 · 9.1 月相Lesson 1 · 9.1 Phases of the Moon

🤔 你有没有想过…… Have you ever wondered…
为什么中秋节的月亮总是又圆又亮，有些晚上却完全看不到月亮？
Why is the Moon always round and bright at Mid-Autumn Festival, yet some nights you can't see it at all?
月球绕地球公转，我们每天看到它被太阳照亮的部分都不同。中秋节是农历十五，正是满月；农历初一是新月，看不到月亮。
As the Moon orbits Earth, we see a different amount of its sunlit side each day. Mid-Autumn is on lunar day 15 – a full moon; lunar day 1 is a new moon, which we can't see.
步骤 1 Step 1

月亮自己不会发光 The Moon makes no light of its own

月球不是光源。我们看到月光，是因为月球反射太阳的光。就像汽车的灯照到路边的反光板，反光板看起来很亮。
The Moon is not a light source. We see moonlight because it reflects sunlight – like a road reflector glowing when car headlights shine on it.
🔁 打个比方 Think of it like this
手电筒 = 太阳，镜子 = 月球：手电筒照到镜子，镜子把光反射到地球仪上。
Torch = Sun, mirror = Moon: the torch shines on the mirror, which reflects the light onto a globe.
🚫 别误会！ Don't be fooled!
❌ 月亮和太阳一样会发光。The Moon gives out light like the Sun.
✅ 月亮只是反射太阳的光；太阳才是光源。The Moon only reflects sunlight; the Sun is the light source.
❓ 想一想 · Check
我们为什么能看到月光？
Why can we see moonlight?
月球像一面大镜子，反射太阳光。
The Moon acts like a big mirror for sunlight.
S6 p.188-189
步骤 2 Step 2

月球怎样转？ How does the Moon move?

月球一边绕着自己的轴自转，一边沿着轨道绕地球公转，两者都是由西向东，各需 27⅓ 天。因为方向相同、时间一样，我们在地球上永远看到月球的同一面。
The Moon spins (rotates) on its axis while orbiting (revolving) Earth, both west to east, each taking 27⅓ days. Because direction and time match, we always see the same face of the Moon.
🏠 自己试试 Try it yourself
和家人玩一玩：你当月球，绕着当“地球”的家人走一圈，而且一直面向他。走完一圈，你自己是不是也转了一圈？
Play it out: you are the Moon. Walk once round a family member ("Earth") while always facing them. Did you also turn round once yourself?
🧠 为什么？ · Why?
为什么我们总是看到月球的同一面？
Why do we always see the same side of the Moon?
月球自转和公转的方向相同、所需的时间也一样（27⅓ 天），所以总是同一面朝向地球。
Its rotation and revolution go the same way and take the same time (27⅓ days), so the same side always faces Earth.
S6 p.190-191
步骤 3 Step 3

月相：月亮的“变身” Moon phases: the Moon's changing shapes

月球绕地球公转时，月、地、日的相对位置改变，我们看到被照亮的部分发生周期性变化，这就是月相。顺序：新月（初一/三十）→ 娥眉月 → 上弦月（初八至初九）→ 凸月 → 满月（十五至十六）→ 凸月 → 下弦月（二十二至二十三）→ 娥眉月 → 新月。
As the Moon orbits, the positions of Moon, Earth and Sun change, so the lit part we see changes in a regular cycle – the phases. Order: new (day 1/30) → crescent → first quarter (day 8–9) → gibbous → full (day 15–16) → gibbous → last quarter (day 22–23) → crescent → new.

🚫 别误会！ Don't be fooled!
❌ 月相是地球的影子挡住了月亮。Moon phases are Earth's shadow covering the Moon.
✅ 月相是因为我们看到月球被太阳照亮的部分不同；地球影子挡住月亮是月食。Phases happen because we see different amounts of the sunlit half; Earth's shadow on the Moon is a lunar eclipse.
❓ 想一想 · Check
农历十五晚上看到的是什么月相？
Which phase do we see on lunar day 15?
满月出现在农历十五至十六。
Full moon is on lunar days 15–16.
❓ 想一想 · Check
上弦月出现在农历哪几天？
On which lunar days is the first quarter?
课本：上弦月初八至初九；下弦月二十二至二十三。
Textbook: first quarter 8–9; last quarter 22–23.
S6 p.192-193
步骤 4 Step 4

月相和我们的生活 Moon phases and our lives

古人根据月相制定历法。华人的农历同时考虑太阳和月球，要用闰月调节；回历完全依据月相，1 号一定是新月，一年只有 354 天。元宵节、中秋节在满月；开斋节在看到新月的次日。月球的引力还影响潮汐，例如满月时涨潮。
People made calendars from the phases. The Chinese lunar calendar follows Sun and Moon and needs leap months; the Hijri calendar follows the Moon only – the 1st is always a new moon and a year has 354 days. Chap Goh Mei and Mid-Autumn fall at full moon; Hari Raya comes the day after the new moon is sighted. The Moon's pull also causes tides, e.g. high tide at full moon.
🏠 自己试试 Try it yourself
用一个月的时间，每晚在同一个地方看月亮，把形状画在月历上，写下农历日期。
For one month, look at the Moon from the same spot each night, draw it on a calendar and note the lunar date.
🧠 为什么？ · Why?
为什么中秋节每年都是满月？
Why is Mid-Autumn always at full moon?
中秋节在农历八月十五；农历是根据月相定的，十五月亮正好是满月。
Mid-Autumn is on the 15th of the 8th lunar month; the lunar calendar follows the Moon, and the 15th is full moon.
S6 p.194-195
⭐ 大道理 Big idea
月亮不发光，只反射阳光；它绕地球转，我们看到的亮面每天不同，形成有规律的月相。
The Moon only reflects sunlight; as it orbits, the lit part we see changes daily in a regular cycle of phases.

✨第 2 课 · 9.2 星座Lesson 2 · 9.2 Constellations

🤔 你有没有想过…… Have you ever wondered…
古时候没有指南针和 GPS，航海的人晚上怎样知道往哪里走？
Long ago, with no compass or GPS, how did sailors find their way at night?
他们看星座：北斗七星指向北极星（北方）；南十字座的长竖指向南方。
They used constellations: the Big Dipper points to the Pole Star (north); the Southern Cross's long bar points south.
步骤 1 Step 1

什么是星座？ What is a constellation?

夜空中的恒星聚集在一起，被人类想象划分成特定的图案，这些图案就是星座。现在天空被划分为 88 个星座；我们熟悉的十二星座位于黄道上。
Stars in the night sky are grouped by imagination into patterns – constellations. The sky is now divided into 88; the familiar twelve zodiac signs lie along the ecliptic.
🔁 打个比方 Think of it like this
像“连连看”：把一颗颗星连起来，就画出勺子、猎人或蝎子。
Like dot-to-dot: join the stars and you get a spoon, a hunter or a scorpion.
❓ 想一想 · Check
星座是什么？
What is a constellation?
星座是人们想象中由恒星连成的图案。
A constellation is an imagined pattern of stars.
S6 p.196-197, p.199
步骤 2 Step 2

四个重要的星座 Four important constellations

北斗七星：像大勺子，是大熊座的一部分，在北半球上空。猎户座：像拿着猎物的猎人，拥有最多明亮的恒星，在赤道上空。天蝎座：像蝎子，是黄道 12 星座之一，在南半球天空。南十字座：像小十字架，是最小的星座，在南方低空。
Big Dipper: a big spoon, part of the Great Bear, in the northern sky. Orion: a hunter, with the most bright stars, above the equator. Scorpius: a scorpion, one of the 12 zodiac constellations, in the southern sky. Southern Cross: a small cross, the smallest constellation, low in the south.
❓ 想一想 · Check
哪一个是最小的星座？
Which is the smallest constellation?
南十字座像一个小小的十字架。
The Southern Cross looks like a tiny cross.
S6 p.197
步骤 3 Step 3

用星座辨别方向 Finding directions with stars

沿着北斗七星斗口的方向延长约五倍，就是北极星，它几乎在正北方。从猎户座腰带中间的星连到头顶再延伸上去，也能找到北极星。南十字座较长的一竖向下划，就是南方。
Extend the Big Dipper's pointer stars about five times and you reach the Pole Star, almost due north. A line from the middle of Orion's belt up through his head also leads to it. The Southern Cross's long bar extended downwards points south.
🏠 自己试试 Try it yourself
（请大人陪同）晴朗的晚上用星空应用程序（如 Star Walk）找找猎户座的三颗“腰带”星。
(With an adult) On a clear night, use a star app such as Star Walk to find Orion's three belt stars.
🧠 为什么？ · Why?
为什么北极星能帮助人们辨别方向？
Why does the Pole Star help people find their way?
北极星几乎在正北方，找到它就知道哪里是北，其他方向也就知道了。
It is almost due north, so once you find it you know north – and therefore the other directions.
S6 p.198-199
步骤 4 Step 4

用星座确认季节 Knowing the seasons from the stars

有些星座在某个季节特别明亮。古时候的人根据特定星座在天空出现的时间，计划春耕、秋收，或准备过冬。马来西亚位于赤道附近，是能看见最多星座的地区之一。
Some constellations are brightest in certain seasons. People planned planting and harvest, or prepared for winter, by when particular constellations appeared. Malaysia, near the equator, can see more constellations than most places.
❓ 想一想 · Check
古时候的农夫观察星座，主要是为了？
Ancient farmers watched constellations mainly to…
星座的第二个用途是确认季节。
The second use of constellations is telling the season.
S6 p.200-201
⭐ 大道理 Big idea
星座是恒星组成的图案，有两个用途：辨别方向和确认季节。
Constellations are star patterns with two uses: finding direction and telling the season.
🌏 那又怎样？科学与生活 So what? Science in my life
  • 中秋节、元宵节和开斋节的日期都跟着月相走——科学就在我们的节日里。
    Mid-Autumn, Chap Goh Mei and Hari Raya all follow the Moon – science is in our festivals.
  • 渔民看月相预测潮汐，决定什么时候出海。
    Fishermen use the Moon's phases to predict tides and decide when to go out.
  • 如果在野外迷路又没有手机信号，认识星座可能救你一命。
    If you're lost outdoors with no signal, knowing the stars could save you.
  • 马来西亚在赤道附近，是观星的好地方，南北天空的星座都能看到。
    Near the equator, Malaysia is a great place for stargazing – you can see both northern and southern constellations.

🌗 9.1 月相✨ 9.2 星座

🌗9.1 月相9.1 Phases of the MoonS6 p.188-195, p.202

  • 月球本身不会发光，它不是光源。
    The Moon does not give out light by itself – it is not a light source.
  • 我们能看到月光，是因为月球反射太阳的光（就像反光板反射车灯的光）。
    We see moonlight because the Moon reflects sunlight (like a road reflector reflecting car headlights).
  • 月球的自转：月球绕着自己的轴，由西向东自转，转一周需 27⅓ 天。
    Rotation of the Moon: the Moon spins on its own axis from west to east, taking 27⅓ days for one turn.
  • 月球的公转：月球绕着地球的轨道，由西向东公转，转一周也需 27⅓ 天。
    Revolution of the Moon: the Moon travels around the Earth in an orbit from west to east, also taking 27⅓ days for one turn.
  • 月球自转和公转的方向相同，所需的时间也一样，所以在地球上我们永远看到月球的同一面。
    The Moon's rotation and revolution are in the same direction and take the same time, so from Earth we always see the same side of the Moon.
  • 月相：月球绕地球公转时，月球、地球和太阳的相对位置改变，月球被太阳照亮的部分在我们看来发生周期性变化。
    Phases of the Moon: as the Moon revolves around the Earth, the positions of the Moon, Earth and Sun change, so the lit part we see changes in a repeating cycle.
  • 从地球上，我们只能看到月球被太阳照到的那一部分。
    From Earth we can only see the part of the Moon that is lit by the Sun.
  • 新月：看不到月亮（月球黑暗的一面朝向地球），出现在农历初一或三十。
    New moon: we cannot see the Moon (its dark side faces Earth); it appears on the 1st or 30th of the lunar month.
  • 娥眉月：只看到细细弯弯的一小部分。
    Crescent moon: only a thin curved part is seen.
  • 上弦月：看到半个月亮；出现在农历初八至初九。
    First quarter moon: half of the Moon's disc is seen; it appears on the 8th to 9th of the lunar month.
  • 凸月：看到超过一半，但还未满。
    Gibbous moon: more than half is seen, but not yet full.
  • 满月：看到整个明亮的圆面；出现在农历十五至十六。
    Full moon: the whole bright disc is seen; it appears on the 15th to 16th of the lunar month.
  • 下弦月：看到半个月亮；出现在农历二十二至二十三。
    Last quarter moon: half of the Moon's disc is seen; it appears on the 22nd to 23rd of the lunar month.
  • 月相变化的顺序：新月 → 娥眉月 → 上弦月 → 凸月 → 满月 → 凸月 → 下弦月 → 娥眉月 → 新月。
    Order of the phases: new moon → crescent → first quarter → gibbous → full moon → gibbous → last quarter → crescent → new moon.
  • 月相从一个满月到下一个满月约需 29½ 天（一个农历月）。 extra 补充
    From one full moon to the next takes about 29½ days (one lunar month).
  • 许多传统节日与月相有关，例如元宵节和中秋节都在满月（农历十五）；伊斯兰教的回历完全依据月相制定，一年只有 354 天。
    Many festivals are linked to the Moon's phases, e.g. the Lantern Festival and Mid-Autumn Festival fall on a full moon (15th of the lunar month); the Islamic calendar follows the Moon's phases exactly and has only 354 days.
  • 月球的引力会影响海洋的潮汐，例如满月时涨潮。
    The Moon's pull affects the ocean tides, e.g. high tide at full moon.
  • 活动 1 借用你的光：手电筒的光照到镜子，镜子再把光反射到地球仪上。手电筒代表太阳（光源），镜子代表月球（反射太阳的光）。 📗 课本 S6 p.188
    Activity 1 – borrow some light: a torch shines on a mirror, which reflects the light onto a globe. The torch stands for the Sun (the light source) and the mirror stands for the Moon (reflecting sunlight).
  • 活动 2 月亮怎么转：同学分别扮演地球和月球，扮演月球的同学一边自转一边绕着地球公转，会发现月球的同一面始终朝着地球——自转一周和公转一周所需的时间相同。 📗 课本 S6 p.190-191
    Activity 2 – how does the Moon move?: students act as the Earth and the Moon; the "Moon" rotates while revolving round the Earth and finds that the same face always points to the Earth – one rotation takes as long as one revolution.
  • 活动 3 月相日记：在一个月内每天在相同的地点观察月相，记录当天的农历和阳历日期，把月相画下来，再讨论月相变化的规律。（注意观察地点的安全） 📗 课本 S6 p.194
    Activity 3 – Moon diary: observe the Moon at the same place every day for a month, record the lunar and Gregorian dates, draw the Moon's shape, then discuss the pattern of the changes. (Choose a safe place.)
  • 活动 4 准时的月相：不少传统节日当天的月相每年都一样，例如开斋节、元宵节、中秋节。（师注）伊斯兰教徒在斋戒月第 29 日傍晚观月，如看到新月，次日就是开斋节。 📗 课本 S6 p.194
    Activity 4 – the Moon on time: on many traditional festivals the Moon's phase is the same every year, e.g. Hari Raya Aidilfitri, the Lantern Festival and the Mid-Autumn Festival. (Teacher's note) Muslims look for the Moon on the evening of the 29th day of Ramadan; if the new moon is seen, the next day is Hari Raya Aidilfitri.
  • 农历与回历：华人的农历考虑了太阳和月球的运行，日期能与季节和月相对应，但需要用闰月来调节误差；回历完全依据月相，1 号一定是新月。几乎所有古老民族都根据月相制定历法，用来了解季节气候变化、安排农事。 📗 课本 S6 p.195
    Lunar calendar vs Hijri calendar: the Chinese lunar calendar takes both the Sun and Moon into account so dates match the seasons and Moon phases, but it needs a leap month to adjust errors; the Hijri calendar follows the Moon only, and the 1st is always a new moon. Almost all ancient peoples made calendars from the Moon's phases to follow the seasons and plan farming.
  • 月亮的周期变化会改变地球上的引力，影响海洋和大气（例：海水在满月时涨潮）。 📗 课本 S6 p.195
    The Moon's regular changes alter the pull of gravity on Earth and affect the oceans and atmosphere (e.g. high tide at full moon).
  • 科学实践王：制作一个月相板，了解在地球上看到的月相是如何形成的。 📗 课本 S6 p.204
    Science practice king: make a Moon-phase board to see how the phases seen from Earth are formed.

回历 1 号一定是新月；农历用闰月调节误差。
In the Hijri calendar the 1st is always a new moon; the lunar calendar uses a leap month to adjust.
💡 你知道吗？ Did you know? extra 补充
月球离地球大约 384,000 公里。月球上没有风，所以宇航员留下的脚印可以保存很久。
The Moon is about 384,000 km away. There is no wind on the Moon, so astronauts' footprints last a very long time.

✨9.2 星座9.2 ConstellationsS6 p.196-201, p.203

  • 夜空中闪闪发亮的恒星聚集在一起，被人类想象划分成特定的图案，这些图案就是星座。
    The shining stars in the night sky form groups that people imagine as particular patterns – these patterns are constellations.
  • 北斗七星：形如大勺子，是大熊座的一部分，出现在北半球上空。
    Big Dipper (Plough): shaped like a big ladle, part of Ursa Major, seen in the northern hemisphere sky.
  • 猎户座：形如拿着猎物的猎人，是拥有最多明亮恒星的星座，出现在赤道上空。
    Orion: looks like a hunter holding prey, the constellation with the most bright stars, seen over the equator.
  • 天蝎座：形似蝎子，是黄道 12 星座之一，出现在南半球天空。
    Scorpius: shaped like a scorpion, one of the 12 zodiac constellations, seen in the southern hemisphere sky.
  • 南十字座：像小小的十字架，是最小的星座，出现在南方低空。
    Southern Cross (Crux): looks like a small cross, the smallest constellation, seen low in the southern sky.
  • 星座的用途一：辨别方向。在指南针和 GPS 发明以前，人们靠星座辨别方向。
    Use 1: finding directions. Before compasses and GPS, people used constellations to find directions.
  • 北斗七星斗口方向延长约五倍，就是北极星；北极星几乎在正北方，是辨别方向的可靠标志。
    Extend the line from the Big Dipper's pointer stars about five times and you reach the Pole Star (Polaris), which lies almost due north – a reliable direction marker.
  • 猎户座在赤道上空，南北半球都能看到；从腰带中间的星连到头顶，一直延伸上去，就可以找到北极星。
    Orion is over the equator and seen from both hemispheres; extend a line from the middle of its belt through its head to find the Pole Star.
  • 南十字座较长的一竖直直向下划，就是南方。
    From the Southern Cross, extend its longer vertical bar downwards to find south.
  • 星座的用途二：确认季节。古时候的人根据特定星座在天空出现的时间，计划春耕、秋收，或准备过冬。
    Use 2: identifying seasons. In the past, people used when certain constellations appeared to plan planting and harvesting, or to prepare for winter.
  • 早期星座的名字和划分各异，现代国际天文学联合会统一整理，把天空划分为 88 个星座。
    Early names and divisions varied; the International Astronomical Union later standardised them and divided the sky into 88 constellations.
  • 以太阳为中心，地球随太阳运行的轨道称为黄道；我们熟悉的十二星座就在黄道上。 extra 补充
    The path that the Sun seems to follow across the sky is the ecliptic; the twelve zodiac constellations lie along it.
  • 我国位于赤道附近，是能看见最多星座的地区之一。
    Our country is near the equator, so we can see many constellations.
  • 活动 5 找找我是谁：用星座卡想象星群图形与名称的关系，配对各自的星座卡；活动 6 找星星：观察星空，记录看到的星座并搜索资料。（师注）可使用 Star Walk、Skyportal、Star Tracker Lite 等星空应用程序。 📗 课本 S6 p.196, p.201
    Activity 5 – who am I?: match constellation cards by shape and name. Activity 6 – find the stars: observe the sky, record the constellations you see and look up information. (Teacher's note) Apps such as Star Walk, Skyportal and Star Tracker Lite can help.
  • 星座来帮忙（情境）：在山上手机收不到信号、又没有指南针时，可以通过北斗七星找到北方，再辨认下山的方向。 📗 课本 S6 p.198
    Constellations to the rescue (scenario): on a hill with no phone signal (no GPS) and no compass, you can use the Big Dipper to find north and work out the way down.
📌 记住！ Remember!
北斗七星 → 北极星 → 北方；南十字座 → 南方。
Big Dipper → Pole Star → north; Southern Cross → south.

夜空里的星座一共有 88 个。
There are 88 constellations in the night sky.
⚠️ 常见错误 Common mistakes
  1. 概念混淆 Concept mix-up
    月球不会发光，我们看到的是它反射的太阳光。
    The Moon gives off no light; we see sunlight it reflects.
  2. 概念混淆 Concept mix-up
    月相变化是因为月球绕地球公转，我们看到被照亮的部分不同——不是地球的影子挡住（那是月食）。
    Phases happen because the Moon orbits the Earth and we see different lit parts — not Earth's shadow (that is an eclipse).
  3. 概念混淆 Concept mix-up
    新月看不到月亮（农历初一）；满月是整个圆面（十五至十六）。
    At new moon you can't see it (lunar day 1); full moon is a whole disc (days 15–16).
  4. 概念混淆 Concept mix-up
    上弦月在农历初八至初九，下弦月在二十二至二十三（根据课本）。
    First quarter is on lunar days 8–9, last quarter on 22–23 (per the textbook).
  5. 答题错误 Exam-answer error
    月相顺序：新月 → 娥眉月 → 上弦月 → 凸月 → 满月 → 凸月 → 下弦月 → 娥眉月。
    Order: new → crescent → first quarter → gibbous → full → gibbous → last quarter → crescent.
  6. 概念混淆 Concept mix-up
    北斗七星找北极星（北方）；南十字座的长竖向下指南方。
    The Big Dipper points to Polaris (north); the long bar of the Southern Cross points south.
  7. 用词陷阱 Wording trap
    ❌ 把“自转”和“公转”弄混。✅ 自转：绕着自己的轴转；公转：沿着轨道绕地球转。
    ❌ Mixing up rotation and revolution. ✅ Rotation: spinning on its own axis; revolution: travelling round Earth.
  8. 概念混淆 Concept mix-up
    ❌ 月球自转一周要一天。✅ 月球自转一周和公转一周都要 27⅓ 天。
    ❌ The Moon spins once a day. ✅ One rotation and one revolution both take 27⅓ days.
  9. 概念混淆 Concept mix-up
    ❌ 星座是由行星组成的。✅ 星座是由恒星组成的图案。
    ❌ Constellations are made of planets. ✅ They are patterns of stars.
  10. 概念混淆 Concept mix-up
    ❌ 北斗七星就是北极星。✅ 沿着北斗七星斗口延长约五倍，才找到北极星。
    ❌ The Big Dipper is the Pole Star. ✅ Extend its pointer stars about five times to reach the Pole Star.
  11. 看图/读表错误 Diagram/table reading
    ❌ 看月相图时，以为月球在地球和太阳之间是满月。✅ 月球在地球和太阳之间是新月；在地球的另一边（背着太阳）才是满月。
    ❌ Thinking the Moon between Earth and Sun is a full moon. ✅ Between them is a new moon; on the far side of Earth is a full moon.
  12. 看图/读表错误 Diagram/table reading
    ❌ 从太阳的角度看月相图。✅ 要想象自己站在地球上，看月球被照亮的那部分有多少朝向你。
    ❌ Reading the phase diagram from the Sun's point of view. ✅ Imagine standing on Earth and ask how much of the lit side faces you.
  13. 答题错误 Exam-answer error
    ❌ 解释月相只写“月亮会变”。✅ 写：“月球绕地球公转，月、地、日的相对位置改变，我们看到被太阳照亮的部分不同。”
    ❌ "The Moon changes." ✅ "As the Moon orbits Earth, the positions of Moon, Earth and Sun change, so we see different amounts of the sunlit part."
  14. 答题错误 Exam-answer error
    ❌ 写月相顺序时漏了凸月或娥眉月。✅ 完整顺序有 8 个：新月、娥眉月、上弦月、凸月、满月、凸月、下弦月、娥眉月。
    ❌ Leaving out gibbous or crescent phases. ✅ The full order has 8: new, crescent, first quarter, gibbous, full, gibbous, last quarter, crescent.

    Clouds only hide it briefly; they don't cause phases.

    Key words: orbits, sunlit part.

❓

(a) The phases change in a regular pattern – a new main phase about every 7–8 days, a full cycle in about one lunar month. (b) New moon (or a thin crescent).
💬 评分理由 Mark by mark

解释（给理由） Explaining with reasons 示范 Example 7

❓

Explain why.
