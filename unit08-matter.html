🧱8.1 物质的形态8.1 States of MatterS6 p.160-170, p.184

  • 世界万物几乎都是由物质组成的。物质有质量，也会占据空间。
    Almost everything in the world is made of matter. Matter has mass and occupies space.
  • 物质的形态有三种：固体、液体和气体。
    Matter exists in three states: solid, liquid and gas.
  • 固体例子：土壤、文具盒；液体例子：河水、咖啡；气体例子：氧气、二氧化碳。
    Solids: soil, pencil case. Liquids: river water, coffee. Gases: oxygen, carbon dioxide.
  • 彩虹是光的折射和反射形成的自然现象，不是物质。
    A rainbow is a natural effect of light bending and reflecting – it is not matter.
  • 质量：用衣架天平比较，物质有质量。装了空气的气球也有质量。
    Mass: using a hanger balance shows that matter has mass. A balloon filled with air also has mass.
  • 体积：物质所占据的空间。可以用量筒测量液体的体积。
    Volume: the space occupied by matter. A measuring cylinder measures the volume of a liquid.
  • 固体：有固定的形状和体积，会占据空间，也有质量。
    Solid: has a fixed shape and volume, occupies space and has mass.
  • 液体：会流动，没有固定的形状，但有固定的体积，会占据空间，也有质量。
    Liquid: flows, has no fixed shape but has a fixed volume, occupies space and has mass.
  • 气体：没有固定的形状和体积，但会占据空间，也有质量。
    Gas: has no fixed shape or volume, but occupies space and has mass.
  • 气体中的粒子之间距离很大，所以气体可以被压缩（例：注射器里的空气）。
    The particles in a gas are far apart, so a gas can be compressed (e.g. air in a syringe).
  • 所有的物质都是由粒子组成的。不同形态的物质，粒子的排列方式不同。
    All matter is made of particles. The particles are arranged differently in each state.
  • 固体的粒子紧密地排列在一起，只会在固定的位置上振动（像排队的学生）。
    In a solid, particles are packed closely together and only vibrate in fixed positions (like students standing in lines).
  • 液体的粒子之间有距离，可以振动和短距离移动（像到处走动的学生）。
    In a liquid, particles have gaps between them and can vibrate and move short distances (like students walking about).
  • 气体的粒子之间有很大的距离，可以振动和移动得很快（像踢足球的学生）。
    In a gas, particles are far apart and vibrate and move very fast (like students playing football).
  • 水有三种形态：水蒸气（气体）、水（液体）和冰块（固体）。
    Water has three states: water vapour (gas), water (liquid) and ice (solid).

Matter has mass and takes up space.

You can see a rainbow, but it has no mass and takes up no space, so it is not matter.

💧8.2 水的形态变化和水循环8.2 Changes in the State of Water and the Water CycleS6 p.171-186

  • 融化：固体变成液体，需要受热（冰块融化成水）。
    Melting: solid becomes liquid; it gains heat (ice melts into water).
  • 凝固：液体变成固体，需要散热（水结成冰块）。
    Freezing: liquid becomes solid; it loses heat (water turns into ice).
  • 蒸发：液体变成气体，需要受热（水吸收热量后蒸发成水蒸气）。
    Evaporation: liquid becomes gas; it gains heat (water absorbs heat and becomes water vapour).
  • 沸腾：水被加热到沸点（100°C）时转化成水蒸气。
    Boiling: when water is heated to its boiling point (100°C) it turns into water vapour.
  • 凝聚：气体变成液体，需要散热（水蒸气冷却变成水）。
    Condensation: gas becomes liquid; it loses heat (water vapour cools into water).
  • 生活例子：
    Everyday examples:
  • 融化的冰块：固体→液体（受热）。
    Melting ice: solid → liquid (gain heat).
  • 晾干衣服：液体→气体（受热，蒸发）。
    Clothes drying: liquid → gas (gain heat, evaporation).
  • 凝固的冰棍：液体→固体（散热）。
    Freezing an ice lolly: liquid → solid (lose heat).
  • 盖子底面的小水珠：气体→液体（散热，凝聚）。
    Droplets on the underside of a lid: gas → liquid (lose heat, condensation).
  • 吹干双手、沸腾的水：液体→气体（受热）。
    Blowing hands dry, boiling water: liquid → gas (gain heat).
  • 冷饮杯子外壁的小水珠：空气中的水蒸气遇冷凝聚成水滴，不是杯子漏水。
    Droplets on the outside of a cold drink glass: water vapour in the air cools and condenses into droplets – the glass is not leaking.
  • 云和雨的形成：阳光使海洋、河流和湖里的水蒸发成水蒸气，升到空中；
    How clouds and rain form: sunlight makes water from seas, rivers and lakes evaporate into water vapour that rises into the air;
  • 空中温度较低，水蒸气凝聚成小水滴，小水滴聚集在一起形成云；
    the air high up is cooler, so the vapour condenses into tiny droplets, which gather to form clouds;
  • 小水滴越聚越多、越来越重，无法漂浮在空中时就会降落，形成雨。
    the droplets keep gathering and get heavier until they cannot float, and fall as rain.
  • 人类、动物和植物也会释放水蒸气。
    People, animals and plants also release water vapour.
  • 水在地球表面和天空之间不断循环，这个过程叫水循环。
    Water keeps moving between the Earth's surface and the sky – this is the water cycle.
  • 雨水降落后流入江、河、湖和海，也会渗入地底，然后又开始新一轮的循环。
    Rain flows into rivers, lakes and seas or seeps underground, and a new cycle begins.
  • 山上的温度较低，水蒸气更容易凝聚，所以山上的云和雨比较密集。
    It is cooler on mountains, so more vapour condenses and there are more clouds and rain there.
  • 总结：云和雨的形成与水的蒸发和凝聚有关。
    Summary: the formation of clouds and rain involves evaporation and condensation.
🧪 在家试试：杯子外的小水珠 Try at home: droplets outside a cup S6 p.171-186
  1. 观察 Observing
    在杯子里放冰块和水，几分钟后看看杯子外壁。
    Put ice and water in a cup; look at the outside after a few minutes.
  2. 推断 Inferring
    小水珠来自空气中的水蒸气，遇冷凝聚成水滴。
    The droplets come from water vapour in the air that condensed on the cold cup.
  3. 预测 Predicting
    如果用温水，外壁不会出现小水珠。
    With warm water, no droplets will form outside.
  4. 沟通 Communicating
    画图说明这是凝聚（散热）。
    Draw it and explain that this is condensation (losing heat).
📌 记住！ Remember!
受热：融化、蒸发、沸腾；散热：凝固、凝聚。
Gain heat: melting, evaporation, boiling; lose heat: freezing, condensation.
💡 你知道吗？ Did you know? extra 补充
云是由许多小水滴（或小冰晶）组成的，不是水蒸气；水蒸气是看不见的。
Clouds are made of tiny water droplets (or ice crystals), not water vapour – water vapour is invisible.
