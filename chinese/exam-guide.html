💡 考试技巧 Exam technique

华文 UASA：1 小时 15 分钟，50 分 Chinese UASA: 1 h 15 min, 50 marks

⏰ 时间分配 Timing (75 分钟 min, 50 分 marks)

  1. 甲组 选择题 10 分：约 12 分钟。先读题目再读短文。Section 甲, 10 MCQ: about 12 min. Read the questions first, then the passage.
  2. 乙组 理解 15 分：约 18 分钟。每题看分数决定写多少。Section 乙, 15 marks: about 18 min. The marks tell you how much to write.
  3. 丙组 供料作文 10 分：约 12 分钟，不少于 40 字。Section 丙, guided writing: about 12 min, at least 40 characters.
  4. 丁组 作文 15 分：约 28 分钟（构思 5、写 18、检查 5）。Section 丁, composition: about 28 min (plan 5, write 18, check 5).
  5. 最后 5 分钟：检查姓名、漏答、错别字。Last 5 min: check your name, blanks and wrong characters.

甲组 选择题 Section 甲 (MCQ)

  1. 圈出题目的关键词：“不正确”“最适合”“主要”。Circle key words in the question: 不正确 (NOT correct), 最适合 (most suitable), 主要 (main).
  2. 回到短文找证据，在句子下划线。Go back to the passage and underline the evidence.
  3. 排除法：删去和文章相反的、没提到的、说得太绝对的。Eliminate: options that contradict, aren't mentioned, or are too absolute.
  4. 词义题：把选项放回句子里读一读。Word-meaning questions: put each option back into the sentence.
  5. 不会的先跳过，最后一定要猜一个，不要留空。Skip hard ones, come back, and never leave a blank.

乙组 理解 Section 乙 (written answers)

  1. 用题目的话开头回答（问“为什么”就答“因为……”）。Start your answer with the question's words (为什么 → 因为…).
  2. 2 分题写两个要点；3 分题写三个要点或“原因＋结果＋感想”。2-mark questions need two points; 3-mark questions need three.
  3. “你同意吗？”要先表态（同意／不同意），再给理由。“Do you agree?”: state your view first, then give a reason.
  4. “你学到什么？”用“我学到了……，所以我以后会……”。“What did you learn?”: 我学到了…, 所以我以后会…
  5. 写完整句子，标点清楚，字迹工整。Write full sentences with clear punctuation and neat characters.

丙组 供料作文 Section 丙 (guided writing)

  1. 用上资料里的每个要点，但要用自己的话连起来。Use every point in the material, linked in your own words.
  2. 结构：开头一句点题 → 中间写要点 → 结尾一句感想。Structure: opening line → the points → a closing feeling.
  3. 至少 40 字，最好写 60–80 字。At least 40 characters; 60–80 is safer.
  4. 不要照抄资料，也不要加和题目无关的内容。Don't copy the material word for word or add off-topic content.

丁组 作文 Section 丁 (composition)

  1. 审题：圈出题眼（例如“难忘”“第一次”），先想好中心。Read the title: circle its key word (难忘, 第一次) and decide your main idea.
  2. 列提纲：开头—经过（详写）—结尾，写在草稿上。Outline on rough paper: opening – events (in detail) – ending.
  3. 开头用环境、对话或问题吸引人；结尾写感受或启示，首尾呼应。Open with a scene, dialogue or question; end with a feeling or lesson that echoes the opening.
  4. 加分：五感描写、比喻、动作和心理描写、一两个成语。Bonus marks: five senses, similes, actions and thoughts, one or two 成语.
  5. 不少于 150 字（看题目要求），分段写。At least 150 characters (check the paper) in paragraphs.

⚠️ 最常丢分的地方 Where marks are lost most

  1. “的、得、地”用错。Mixing up 的 / 得 / 地.
  2. 错别字：再／在、己／已、做／作。Wrong characters: 再/在, 己/已, 做/作.
  3. 答案只抄原文一句，没有回答问题。Copying one line from the passage without answering the question.
  4. 作文偏题或流水账（每件事都写一点点）。Going off-topic or listing everything with no detail.
  5. 对话标点：说话人＋冒号＋引号。Dialogue punctuation: speaker + colon + quotation marks.
  6. 时间不够，作文没写完 —— 一定要看钟。Running out of time before finishing the composition — watch the clock.
