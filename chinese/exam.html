📝 考试练习Exam Practice · UASA 华文格式 format

格式参考 UASA 五年级华文试卷：甲组 现代文选择题 10 分（自动批改）、乙组 理解文 15 分、丙组 供料作文 10 分、丁组 作文 15 分（二选一，不少于150字；看参考答案和评分标准自己批改）。全卷 50 分，1 小时 15 分钟，一份试卷。Modelled on the UASA Standard 5 Chinese paper: 甲 passage MCQ 10 marks (auto-marked), 乙 comprehension 15, 丙 guided writing 10, 丁 composition 15 (choose 1 of 2 titles, ≥150 characters; self-marked with model answers and rubrics). One paper: 50 marks, 1 h 15 min.
🗓️ 考试范围：年中考：单元一、二（第1–10课）＋写作与语文知识；单元三（第11–15课）是边界范围，也要复习。年终 UASA：全年第1–20课（单元一至四）＋写作与语文知识。 年中模拟考以第1–10课为主，另有一组第11–15课的边界题；三份年终模拟考考全年。Mid-year: Units 1–2 (lessons 1–10) plus writing and language skills; Unit 3 (lessons 11–15) is borderline, so revise it too. Year-end UASA: all lessons 1–20 (Units 1–4) plus skills. Sources: the workbook 亮点UP! 综合练习 华文 5A places its 年中评估 after Unit 2 (lesson 10); Zekolah's mid-year (2nd assessment) paper covers lessons 9–15 and its year-end papers cover all 20 lessons. Yuk Chai's own notice decides — check it. The mid-year mock is built mainly on lessons 1–10 with one borderline section from lessons 11–15; the three year-end mocks test the whole year.
