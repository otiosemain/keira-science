🧪 7.1 生锈的物质和生锈的因素🛡️ 7.2 防锈的方法和好处

🧪7.1 生锈的物质和生锈的因素7.1 Rusting Objects and Factors for RustingS6 p.148-152, p.156

  • 物品放置一段时间后，表面会出现红褐色的物质，这就是锈。生锈是一种化学反应。
    After some time, a reddish-brown substance appears on the surface of some objects – this is rust. Rusting is a chemical reaction.
  • 生锈的物品表面变得粗糙，出现红褐色的粉末或碎片，也变得脆弱。
    A rusty object has a rough surface with reddish-brown powder or flakes, and becomes brittle.
  • 由铁制成的物品会生锈，例如灯柱、自行车、铁门、水龙头、栏杆和铁盖。
    Objects made of iron rust, for example lamp posts, bicycles, iron gates, taps, railings and iron lids.
  • 玻璃、木头等不含铁的物品不会生锈。 extra 补充
    Objects such as glass and wood do not contain iron and do not rust.
  • 铁完全生锈后，体积可膨大 8 倍；铁锈特别容易吸收水分，会使铁更快被腐蚀。
    When iron has completely rusted its volume can grow 8 times bigger; rust absorbs water easily, so the iron corrodes even faster.
  • 铁钉生锈的实验：把铁钉分别放在不同条件的试管里——有水、有油、沸水、无水氯化钙。
    Rusting experiment: iron nails are put in test tubes under different conditions – water, oil, boiled water, anhydrous calcium chloride.
  • 无水氯化钙用来吸收空气中的水分，使试管里保持干燥。
    Anhydrous calcium chloride absorbs moisture so the air in the tube stays dry.
  • 油倒在水面上，是为了不让空气（氧气）进入水中。沸水中溶解的空气较少。 extra 补充
    The oil on top of the water stops air (oxygen) from dissolving into the water. Boiled water has little dissolved air.
  • 结论：铁制品生锈需要水和空气中的氧气，两个因素必须同时具备；只有水或只有氧气，都不会生锈。
    Conclusion: iron rusts only when water and oxygen in the air are both present; with only water or only oxygen it does not rust.
  • 盐水和白醋会加快铁钉生锈，自来水生锈较慢。
    Salt water and white vinegar make an iron nail rust faster; tap water makes it rust more slowly.
  • 海边的铁制品特别容易生锈，因为空气潮湿，而且空气和水中含盐分。
    Iron objects near the sea rust especially fast because the air is damp and the air and water contain salt.
  • 操作性定义：把铁制品放在有水和空气的地方一段时间，如果表面出现红褐色物质，就是生锈了。 extra 补充
    Operational definition: if reddish-brown matter appears on an iron object kept with water and air for some time, it has rusted.

🧪 实验：铁钉生锈需要什么？ Investigation: what does an iron nail need to rust? S6 p.150-151
  1. 假设 Hypothesising
    铁钉要同时有水和空气才会生锈。
    An iron nail rusts only when there is both water and air.
  2. 控制变数 Controlling variables
    操纵：试管里的条件；反应：铁钉有没有生锈；固定：铁钉的大小、放置的时间。
    Manipulated: conditions in the tube; responding: does the nail rust; fixed: nail size, time left.
  3. 实验 Experimenting
    A 放水；B 放沸水再加一层油；C 放无水氯化钙并盖紧。
    A: water; B: boiled water with a layer of oil; C: anhydrous calcium chloride, sealed.
  4. 观察 Observing
    几天后，只有试管 A 的铁钉生锈。
    After a few days, only the nail in tube A has rusted.
  5. 结论 Conclusion
    生锈需要水和空气同时存在。
    Rusting needs water and air together.
📌 记住！ Remember!
生锈需要水和空气（氧气），两个因素必须同时具备。
Rusting needs water and air (oxygen) – both at the same time.
只有铁制成的物品会生锈。
Only things made of iron rust.
💡 你知道吗？ Did you know? extra 补充
不锈钢里加了一种叫“铬”的金属，所以不容易生锈，常用来做汤匙和锅。
Stainless steel contains chromium, so it hardly rusts – that is why spoons and pots are made of it.

🛡️7.2 防锈的方法和好处7.2 Rust Prevention and Its BenefitsS6 p.153-156

  • 防锈的原理：让铁制品不接触水和空气。
    The idea of rust prevention: keep iron objects from touching water and air.
  • 防锈方法：
    Ways to prevent rust:
  • 油漆（例：铁门、铁栏杆）。
    Paint (e.g. iron gates, railings).
  • 包裹塑料（例：铁丝衣架）。
    Coat with plastic (e.g. wire coat hangers).
  • 镀不锈金属（例：螺丝、罐头）。
    Plate with a non-rusting metal (e.g. screws, cans).
  • 涂上一层油（例：自行车链条、锯子）。
    Apply a layer of oil (e.g. bicycle chain, saw).
  • 自行车的不同部位用不同方法：车身油漆，把手包裹塑料，链条和轴心涂润滑油。 extra 补充
    Different parts of a bicycle use different methods: paint on the frame, plastic on the handlebar grips, oil on the chain and axles.
  • 给生锈的铁窗防锈的步骤：①用砂纸打磨去锈 → ②用抹布擦干净 → ③涂防锈油漆 → ④晾干后涂彩色油漆。 extra 补充
    Steps to protect a rusty iron window: ① sand off the rust → ② wipe clean → ③ apply anti-rust paint → ④ when dry, add a coat of colour paint.
  • 防锈的好处：
    Benefits of preventing rust:
  • 让物品保持美观。
    Keeps objects looking good.
  • 确保安全使用（例：汽车零件生锈会不安全）。
    Ensures safe use (e.g. rusty car parts are unsafe).
  • 避免危害健康（例：生锈的罐头会弄坏食物）。
    Avoids harm to health (e.g. a rusty can can spoil the food).
  • 延长使用期。
    Prolongs the lifespan of objects.
  • 节省维修费和时间。
    Saves repair costs and time.
📌 记住！ Remember!
防锈的原理：让铁不接触水和空气。
To prevent rust, keep iron away from water and air.
💡 你知道吗？ Did you know? extra 补充
自行车链条涂油，不但防锈，还能让链条转得更顺。
Oil on a bicycle chain prevents rust and helps the chain move smoothly.
⚠️ 常见错误 Common mistakes
  1. 生锈需要水和空气（氧气）同时存在，缺一个都不会生锈。
    Rusting needs water and air (oxygen) together; without either, no rust.
  2. 只有铁和含铁的物品会生锈；玻璃、木头、塑料不会生锈。
    Only iron and things containing iron rust; glass, wood and plastic don't.
  3. 实验装置的作用要说清：油层阻止空气进入水中；沸水含较少空气；无水氯化钙吸收水分。
    Know each part of the set-up: the oil layer keeps air out of the water; boiled water has less air; anhydrous calcium chloride absorbs water.
  4. 盐水会加快生锈，所以海边的铁器更容易生锈。
    Salt water makes rusting faster, so iron rusts quicker by the sea.
  5. 防锈方法要配对物品：链条涂油、铁门油漆、衣架包塑料、罐头镀不锈金属。
    Match the method to the object: chain — oil, gate — paint, hanger — plastic coat, can — plated with a non-rusting metal.
  6. 锈是红褐色、脆弱、会剥落的，不能保护里面的铁。
    Rust is reddish-brown, brittle and flakes off; it does not protect the iron underneath.
🇲🇾 生活中的科学 Science in real life (Malaysia)
  • 波德申等海边的铁栏杆特别容易生锈，因为海风潮湿又含盐分。
    Iron railings at beaches like Port Dickson rust fast because the sea air is damp and salty.
  • 家里的铁门要定期上漆；脚车链条要涂油。
    Repaint the iron gate at home regularly; oil your bicycle chain.
  • 乡村房屋的锌板屋顶，是在铁片上镀锌来防锈。
    Zinc roofs on kampung houses are iron sheets coated with zinc to prevent rust.
  • 生锈、凹陷的罐头食品不要吃，可能危害健康。
    Don't eat food from rusty, dented cans; it can harm your health.

  • “A 生锈了”是观察结果，不是结论。

    The conclusion names the factors for rusting and answers the question.
解释 Explaining 示范 Example 2
试管 B 的水面上加了一层油。
A layer of oil is added on the water in tube B.
❓
说明油层的作用。
State the purpose of the oil layer.
[1 分 marks]
❌ 弱答案 Weak answer · 0/1
让铁钉不生锈。
To stop the nail rusting.
✅ 满分答案 Full-mark answer · 1/1
油层阻止空气溶入水中，使铁钉只接触水、不接触空气。
The oil layer stops air dissolving into the water, so the nail touches water but not air.
💬 为什么？ Why?
  • 弱答案说了结果，但没有说出油层怎样做到。
    The weak answer gives the outcome, not how the oil does it.
  • 关键词：阻止空气进入。
    Key words: keeps air out.
