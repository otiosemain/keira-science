单元 7：生锈Unit 7: Rusting

这一单元学习什么是生锈、哪些东西会生锈、生锈的因素，以及怎样防锈和防锈的好处。
This unit covers what rusting is, which objects rust, the factors that cause rusting, and how to prevent rust and why it matters.

五年级科学 Year 5 Science · KSSR Semakan · 来源 Source: S6 p.147-158 (KPM 五年级科学课本 Textbook, 第7单元 + 考卷 exam papers S3-S5)

7.1 生锈的物质和生锈的因素7.1 Rusting Objects and Factors for RustingS6 p.148-152, p.156

  • 物品放置一段时间后，表面会出现红褐色的物质，这就是锈。生锈是一种化学反应。
    After some time, a reddish-brown substance appears on the surface of some objects – this is rust. Rusting is a chemical reaction.
  • 生锈的物品表面变得粗糙，出现红褐色的粉末或碎片，也变得脆弱。
    A rusty object has a rough surface with reddish-brown powder or flakes, and becomes brittle.
  • 由铁制成的物品会生锈，例如灯柱、自行车、铁门、水龙头、栏杆和铁盖。
    Objects made of iron rust, for example lamp posts, bicycles, iron gates, taps, railings and iron lids.
  • 玻璃、木头等不含铁的物品不会生锈。 extra 补充
    Objects such as glass and wood do not contain iron and do not rust.
  • 铁完全生锈后，体积可膨大 8 倍；铁锈特别容易吸收水分，会使铁更快被腐蚀。
    When iron has completely rusted its volume can grow 8 times bigger; rust absorbs water easily, so the iron corrodes even faster.
  • 铁钉生锈的实验：把铁钉分别放在不同条件的试管里——有水、有油、沸水、无水氯化钙。
    Rusting experiment: iron nails are put in test tubes under different conditions – water, oil, boiled water, anhydrous calcium chloride.
  • 无水氯化钙用来吸收空气中的水分，使试管里保持干燥。
    Anhydrous calcium chloride absorbs moisture so the air in the tube stays dry.
  • 油倒在水面上，是为了不让空气（氧气）进入水中。沸水中溶解的空气较少。 extra 补充
    The oil on top of the water stops air (oxygen) from dissolving into the water. Boiled water has little dissolved air.
  • 结论：铁制品生锈需要水和空气中的氧气，两个因素必须同时具备；只有水或只有氧气，都不会生锈。
    Conclusion: iron rusts only when water and oxygen in the air are both present; with only water or only oxygen it does not rust.
  • 盐水和白醋会加快铁钉生锈，自来水生锈较慢。
    Salt water and white vinegar make an iron nail rust faster; tap water makes it rust more slowly.
  • 海边的铁制品特别容易生锈，因为空气潮湿，而且空气和水中含盐分。
    Iron objects near the sea rust especially fast because the air is damp and the air and water contain salt.
  • 操作性定义：把铁制品放在有水和空气的地方一段时间，如果表面出现红褐色物质，就是生锈了。 extra 补充
    Operational definition: if reddish-brown matter appears on an iron object kept with water and air for some time, it has rusted.

7.2 防锈的方法和好处7.2 Rust Prevention and Its BenefitsS6 p.153-156

  • 防锈的原理：让铁制品不接触水和空气。
    The idea of rust prevention: keep iron objects from touching water and air.
  • 防锈方法：
    Ways to prevent rust:
  • 油漆（例：铁门、铁栏杆）。
    Paint (e.g. iron gates, railings).
  • 包裹塑料（例：铁丝衣架）。
    Coat with plastic (e.g. wire coat hangers).
  • 镀不锈金属（例：螺丝、罐头）。
    Plate with a non-rusting metal (e.g. screws, cans).
  • 涂上一层油（例：自行车链条、锯子）。
    Apply a layer of oil (e.g. bicycle chain, saw).
  • 自行车的不同部位用不同方法：车身油漆，把手包裹塑料，链条和轴心涂润滑油。 extra 补充
    Different parts of a bicycle use different methods: paint on the frame, plastic on the handlebar grips, oil on the chain and axles.
  • 给生锈的铁窗防锈的步骤：①用砂纸打磨去锈 → ②用抹布擦干净 → ③涂防锈油漆 → ④晾干后涂彩色油漆。 extra 补充
    Steps to protect a rusty iron window: ① sand off the rust → ② wipe clean → ③ apply anti-rust paint → ④ when dry, add a coat of colour paint.
  • 防锈的好处：
    Benefits of preventing rust:
  • 让物品保持美观。
    Keeps objects looking good.
  • 确保安全使用（例：汽车零件生锈会不安全）。
    Ensures safe use (e.g. rusty car parts are unsafe).
  • 避免危害健康（例：生锈的罐头会弄坏食物）。
    Avoids harm to health (e.g. a rusty can can spoil the food).
  • 延长使用期。
    Prolongs the lifespan of objects.
  • 节省维修费和时间。
    Saves repair costs and time.
