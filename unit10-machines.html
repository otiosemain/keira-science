🛠️10.1 工具和简单机械10.1 Tools and Simple MachinesS6 p.206-211, p.214

  • 我们周围有很多工具，它们帮助人完成工作，让工作更轻松。
    There are many tools around us; they help us do work more easily.
  • 许多工具由一种或几种简单机械组合而成。
    Many tools are made of one or more simple machines.
  • 六种简单机械：杠杆、滑轮、轮轴、齿轮、尖劈、螺旋。
    Six simple machines: lever, pulley, wheel and axle, gear, wedge and screw.
  • 指甲钳由杠杆和尖劈组成，帮助我们更轻松地修剪指甲。
    A nail clipper is made of a lever and a wedge, helping us trim nails more easily.
  • 手摇削笔机：轮轴（曲柄转动时带动齿轮和滚刀）、齿轮（让滚刀围绕铅笔旋转）、尖劈（锋利的滚刀把铅笔削尖）。
    Hand-crank pencil sharpener: wheel and axle (the crank turns the gears and cutter), gears (make the cutter turn around the pencil) and wedge (the sharp cutter sharpens the pencil).
  • 手表：轮轴（柄轴带动齿轮）、齿轮（控制表针转动）、螺旋（嵌合表内的零件）。
    Watch: wheel and axle (the stem turns gears), gears (control the hands) and screws (hold parts together).
  • 修剪树枝的工具：杠杆（减少所需的力）、尖劈（交错的刀刃）、滑轮（滑轮组轻松、省力地控制刀刃开合）。
    Tree-pruning tool: lever (reduces the force needed), wedge (the crossing blades) and pulley (a pulley set opens and closes the blades easily).
  • 手推车能帮助人们搬运重物；它由杠杆和轮轴组成。
    A wheelbarrow helps people move heavy things; it is made of a lever and a wheel and axle.
  • 每种简单机械都有自己的功用，缺一不可，组合起来工作才能完美。
    Each simple machine has its own job and none can be left out; they work best together.
  • 如果其中一个部件被卡住（例：削笔机的滚刀被笔芯卡住），曲柄就转不动，整个工具无法正常操作。
    If one part is jammed (e.g. the sharpener's cutter is blocked by lead), the crank cannot turn and the whole tool stops working.
  • 生活中的例子：跷跷板（杠杆）、旗杆（滑轮）、门把手（轮轴）、斧头（尖劈）、瓶盖（螺旋）。 extra 补充
    Everyday examples: seesaw (lever), flagpole (pulley), door knob (wheel and axle), axe (wedge), bottle cap (screw).
  • 活动 1 寻找园艺工具：从图中的园艺工具里，选出适合帮助美美和祖父在花园里种菜的工具，并说明它们的用途。 📗 课本 S6 p.206
    Activity 1 – find the garden tools: from the garden tools in the picture, choose those that can help Mei Mei and her grandfather grow vegetables in the garden and say what each is used for.
  • 活动 2 探索身边的工具：列出曾使用的 10 种工具，说出它们的名称、用途和如何帮助你；再小心拆卸手摇削笔机，观察里面的简单机械——轮轴、齿轮和尖劈。 📗 课本 S6 p.207-208
    Activity 2 – explore tools around us: list 10 tools you have used and say their names, uses and how they help you; then carefully take apart a hand-crank pencil sharpener and find its simple machines – wheel and axle, gears and wedge.
  • 剪刀、玩具车等日常用品也是由多种简单机械组成的。 📗 课本 S6 p.210
    Everyday items such as scissors and toy cars are also made of several simple machines.
  • 活动 3 简单机械“罢工”时：观察并小心拆卸出问题的工具，推断无法正常操作的原因，讨论恢复正常的方法。结论：一台机械组合里的任何一种简单机械损坏，都会使这工具无法正常操作。 📗 课本 S6 p.211
    Activity 3 – when a simple machine "goes on strike": look at and carefully take apart a faulty tool, work out why it can't work and discuss how to fix it. Conclusion: if any one simple machine in a combination is damaged, the tool cannot work properly.

♻️10.2 可永续使用的工具10.2 Tools That Can Be Used SustainablyS6 p.212-215

  • 永续使用的工具：不容易坏，能长久、安全地使用，不会浪费资源。
    A tool that can be used sustainably does not break easily and can be used safely for a long time without wasting resources.
  • 例：破旧、生锈的滑板和跷跷板对人有危害，必须修复或更换。
    e.g. a broken slide and a rusty seesaw are dangerous and must be repaired or replaced.
  • 制造可永续使用的工具时，需要考虑：
    When making a sustainable tool, think about:
  • 适当的材料
    suitable materials
  • 使用寿命
    lifespan
  • 维修与保养费用
    repair and maintenance cost
  • 制作成本
    production cost
  • 环保性
    environmental friendliness
  • 安全性
    safety
  • 设计和改良工具的步骤：列出需要改善的工具和问题 → 提出方法 → 画设计图 → 评估并选出一张 → 收集意见，再改良 → 分享作品。
    Steps to design and improve a tool: list the tool and its problem → suggest solutions → draw designs → evaluate and choose one → collect feedback and improve → share your work.
  • 制造可永续使用的工具是非常重要的。
    Making tools that can be used sustainably is very important.
  • 课后大挑战 / 科学实践王：用两种或以上的简单机械设计一个装置（例：帮伟宏把一箱杂物搬到阁楼），并说明用了哪些简单机械。 📗 课本 S6 p.214-215
    After-class challenge / Science practice king: design a device using two or more simple machines (e.g. to help Wei Hong move a box of things up to the attic) and say which simple machines you used.

📌 记住！ Remember!
可永续使用的工具：耐用、安全、不浪费资源。
Sustainable tools last long, are safe and do not waste resources.
⚠️ 常见错误 Common mistakes
  1. 课本的六种简单机械：杠杆、滑轮、轮轴、齿轮、尖劈、螺旋。
    The textbook's six simple machines: lever, pulley, wheel and axle, gear, wedge, screw.
  2. 一种工具可以由几种简单机械组成，例：指甲钳 = 杠杆 + 尖劈。
    One tool can combine several simple machines, e.g. nail clipper = lever + wedge.
  3. 杠杆：支点离阻力点越近，越省力。
    Lever: the closer the fulcrum is to the load, the less effort needed.
  4. 旗杆上的滑轮让我们向下拉就能把旗子升上去——改变用力的方向。
    The pulley on a flagpole lets you pull down to raise the flag — it changes the direction of force.
  5. 尖劈例子：斧头、刀；螺旋例子：瓶盖、螺丝。
    Wedge examples: axe, knife; screw examples: bottle cap, screw.
  6. 可永续使用的工具要考虑材料、寿命、维修费用、成本、环保和安全。
    A sustainable tool considers material, lifespan, upkeep cost, cost, environment and safety.
🇲🇾 生活中的科学 Science in real life (Malaysia)
  • 学校集会升旗：旗杆顶的滑轮让我们向下拉绳子就能升旗。
    Flag-raising at assembly: the pulley at the top lets you pull down to raise the flag.
  • 开榴梿或劈柴用的巴冷刀（parang）是尖劈。
    The parang used to open durians or chop wood is a wedge.
  • 脚车有轮轴、齿轮（链轮）和杠杆（刹车把手），是由几种简单机械组成的。
    A bicycle has wheel and axle, gears (sprockets) and levers (brake handles) — several machines together.
  • 修理和上油保养旧脚车，比丢掉买新的更环保。
    Repairing and oiling an old bicycle is greener than throwing it away.

✍️ 考试答题示范 Worked exam answers (Section C)

诠释资料 Interpreting data 示范 Example 1
用杠杆提起同一个重物，改变支点与重物的距离：10 cm 需 2 N，20 cm 需 4 N，30 cm 需 6 N。
Lifting the same load with a lever, changing the fulcrum-to-load distance: 10 cm needs 2 N, 20 cm 4 N, 30 cm 6 N.
❓
说出支点与重物的距离和所需的力之间的关系。
State the relationship between the fulcrum-to-load distance and the force needed.
[1 分 marks]
❌ 弱答案 Weak answer · 0/1
10 cm 最省力。
10 cm is the easiest.
✅ 满分答案 Full-mark answer · 1/1
支点与重物（阻力点）的距离越近，所需的力越小。
The closer the fulcrum is to the load, the smaller the force needed.
💬 为什么？ Why?
  • 弱答案只挑一个数据。
    The weak answer picks one value.
  • 关系句要包括两个变数和变化方向。
    A relationship names both variables and the direction of change.
控制变数 Variables 示范 Example 2
用滑轮提起不同重量的沙包，记录所需的力。
Sandbags of different weights are lifted with a pulley and the force needed is recorded.
❓
写出固定性变数。
State a fixed variable.
[1 分 marks]
❌ 弱答案 Weak answer · 0/1
滑轮。
The pulley.
✅ 满分答案 Full-mark answer · 1/1
使用同一个滑轮（或同一条绳子）。
Use the same pulley (or the same rope).
💬 为什么？ Why?
  • 固定性变数要写成“保持不变的东西”：同一个滑轮。
    A fixed variable is written as something kept the same: the same pulley.
