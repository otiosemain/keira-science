单元 10：机械Unit 10: Machines

这一单元学习我们周围的工具是由哪些简单机械组成的，以及怎样制造可永续使用的工具。
This unit covers which simple machines make up the tools around us, and how to make tools that can be used sustainably.

五年级科学 Year 5 Science · KSSR Semakan · 来源 Source: S6 p.205-215 (KPM 五年级科学课本 Textbook, 第10单元 + 考卷 exam papers S3-S5)

10.1 工具和简单机械10.1 Tools and Simple MachinesS6 p.206-211, p.214

  • 我们周围有很多工具，它们帮助人完成工作，让工作更轻松。
    There are many tools around us; they help us do work more easily.
  • 许多工具由一种或几种简单机械组合而成。
    Many tools are made of one or more simple machines.
  • 六种简单机械：杠杆、滑轮、轮轴、齿轮、尖劈、螺旋。
    Six simple machines: lever, pulley, wheel and axle, gear, wedge and screw.
  • 指甲钳由杠杆和尖劈组成，帮助我们更轻松地修剪指甲。
    A nail clipper is made of a lever and a wedge, helping us trim nails more easily.
  • 手摇削笔机：轮轴（曲柄转动时带动齿轮和滚刀）、齿轮（让滚刀围绕铅笔旋转）、尖劈（锋利的滚刀把铅笔削尖）。
    Hand-crank pencil sharpener: wheel and axle (the crank turns the gears and cutter), gears (make the cutter turn around the pencil) and wedge (the sharp cutter sharpens the pencil).
  • 手表：轮轴（柄轴带动齿轮）、齿轮（控制表针转动）、螺旋（嵌合表内的零件）。
    Watch: wheel and axle (the stem turns gears), gears (control the hands) and screws (hold parts together).
  • 修剪树枝的工具：杠杆（减少所需的力）、尖劈（交错的刀刃）、滑轮（滑轮组轻松、省力地控制刀刃开合）。
    Tree-pruning tool: lever (reduces the force needed), wedge (the crossing blades) and pulley (a pulley set opens and closes the blades easily).
  • 手推车能帮助人们搬运重物；它由杠杆和轮轴组成。
    A wheelbarrow helps people move heavy things; it is made of a lever and a wheel and axle.
  • 每种简单机械都有自己的功用，缺一不可，组合起来工作才能完美。
    Each simple machine has its own job and none can be left out; they work best together.
  • 如果其中一个部件被卡住（例：削笔机的滚刀被笔芯卡住），曲柄就转不动，整个工具无法正常操作。
    If one part is jammed (e.g. the sharpener's cutter is blocked by lead), the crank cannot turn and the whole tool stops working.
  • 生活中的例子：跷跷板（杠杆）、旗杆（滑轮）、门把手（轮轴）、斧头（尖劈）、瓶盖（螺旋）。 extra 补充
    Everyday examples: seesaw (lever), flagpole (pulley), door knob (wheel and axle), axe (wedge), bottle cap (screw).

10.2 可永续使用的工具10.2 Tools That Can Be Used SustainablyS6 p.212-215

  • 永续使用的工具：不容易坏，能长久、安全地使用，不会浪费资源。
    A tool that can be used sustainably does not break easily and can be used safely for a long time without wasting resources.
  • 例：破旧、生锈的滑板和跷跷板对人有危害，必须修复或更换。
    e.g. a broken slide and a rusty seesaw are dangerous and must be repaired or replaced.
  • 制造可永续使用的工具时，需要考虑：
    When making a sustainable tool, think about:
  • 适当的材料
    suitable materials
  • 使用寿命
    lifespan
  • 维修与保养费用
    repair and maintenance cost
  • 制作成本
    production cost
  • 环保性
    environmental friendliness
  • 安全性
    safety
  • 设计和改良工具的步骤：列出需要改善的工具和问题 → 提出方法 → 画设计图 → 评估并选出一张 → 收集意见，再改良 → 分享作品。
    Steps to design and improve a tool: list the tool and its problem → suggest solutions → draw designs → evaluate and choose one → collect feedback and improve → share your work.
  • 制造可永续使用的工具是非常重要的。
    Making tools that can be used sustainably is very important.
