单元 5：电Unit 5: Electricity

这一单元学习不同的电源、串联电路和并联电路，以及怎样安全使用电器和节省用电。
This unit covers different power sources, series circuits and parallel circuits, and how to use electrical appliances safely and save electricity.

五年级科学 Year 5 Science · KSSR Semakan · 来源 Source: S6 p.105-128 (KPM 五年级科学课本 Textbook, 第5单元 + 考卷 exam papers S3-S5)

5.1 电源5.1 Power SourcesS6 p.106-109, p.126

  • 电源是能提供电能的装置。我们在日常生活中常用到不同的电源。
    A power source is a device that provides electrical energy. We use different power sources every day.
  • 干电池：体积小，方便携带（例：闹钟、手电筒、计算器）。
    Dry cell: small and easy to carry (e.g. alarm clock, torch, calculator).
  • 蓄电池：大部分交通工具使用，例如汽车。
    Rechargeable battery (car battery): used in most vehicles, e.g. cars.
  • 蓄电池可以重复充电。 extra 补充
    A rechargeable battery can be charged again and again.
  • 小型发电机：自行车的车轮转动时，会带动小型发电机的小轮，从而产生电流使车灯发亮。
    Dynamo (small generator): when a bicycle wheel turns, it turns the small wheel of the dynamo, which makes electric current so the lamp lights up.
  • 发电机：靠汽油、柴油等燃料产生电能。
    Generator: uses fuels such as petrol and diesel to produce electrical energy.
  • 太阳能电池：适合用在人造卫星、计算器等。它是可更新的能源。
    Solar cell: suitable for satellites, calculators and so on. It is a renewable source of energy.
  • 氢燃料电池：是绿色能源之一，不会污染环境。它通过化学反应产生电能，适用于汽车及航空领域。
    Hydrogen fuel cell: a green energy source that does not pollute the environment. It produces electrical energy by a chemical reaction and is used in cars and aviation.
  • 发电站：利用动能推动发电机，再通过电缆把电能输送到家里。
    Power station: uses kinetic energy to drive generators, then sends the electrical energy to our homes through cables.
  • 可更新的能源（例：阳光、风、水流）不会用完；汽油和柴油用了就会减少。 extra 补充
    Renewable energy (e.g. sunlight, wind, flowing water) does not run out; petrol and diesel get used up.

5.2 串联和并联电路5.2 Series and Parallel CircuitsS6 p.110-118, p.126

  • 电路由电源、灯泡、开关和电线连接而成，电流才能流动。 extra 补充
    An electric circuit is made of a power source, bulbs, a switch and wires joined together so that current can flow.
  • 电路图用符号表示元件：干电池、灯泡、开关、电线。
    A circuit diagram uses symbols for components: dry cell, bulb, switch, wire.
  • 串联电路：灯泡是依次连接的，电流只有一条通路。
    Series circuit: the bulbs are connected one after another, so the current has only one path.
  • 并联电路：灯泡是并列连接的，电流有不同的分支通路。
    Parallel circuit: the bulbs are connected side by side, so the current has different branch paths.
  • 并联电路中的灯泡比串联电路中的灯泡更亮。
    Bulbs in a parallel circuit are brighter than bulbs in a series circuit.
  • 串联电路中，电量要平均分给每个灯泡；并联电路中，每条分支都获得足够的电量。 extra 补充
    In a series circuit the electrical energy is shared equally among the bulbs; in a parallel circuit each branch gets enough energy.
  • 增加灯泡：串联电路的灯泡亮度减弱；并联电路的灯泡亮度不变。
    Adding bulbs: bulbs in a series circuit become dimmer; bulbs in a parallel circuit stay the same.
  • 增加干电池：串联和并联电路的灯泡亮度都增加。
    Adding dry cells: bulbs in both series and parallel circuits become brighter.
  • 实验中，只改变一个操纵性变数（例：灯泡的数量），其他条件保持不变，这样才是公平测试。 extra 补充
    In an investigation, change only one manipulated variable (e.g. number of bulbs) and keep everything else the same – this is a fair test.
  • 开关：串联电路中拉上一个开关，所有灯泡都熄灭；并联电路中，只有该开关所控制的灯泡熄灭，其他灯泡依然发亮。
    Switch: in a series circuit, opening one switch makes all bulbs go out; in a parallel circuit only the bulb controlled by that switch goes out and the others stay lit.
  • 保险丝：电流过强时会自动切断电路，避免短路引起火患。
    Fuse: cuts the circuit automatically when the current is too strong, to prevent a short circuit causing a fire.
  • 多数灯饰的电路是串联的；多数家用插座中的电路是并联的。
    Most decorative lights are connected in series; most household sockets are connected in parallel.

5.3 安全使用电器与节省用电5.3 Safe Use of Electrical Appliances and Saving ElectricityS6 p.119-126

  • 影响用电量的因素：电器的种类、使用次数和使用时间长短。
    Factors that affect electricity consumption: the type of appliance, how often it is used and how long it is used.
  • 节省用电的方法：
    Ways to save electricity:
  • 不让冰箱门长时间开着。
    Do not leave the fridge door open for a long time.
  • 使用节能电器。
    Use energy-saving appliances.
  • 累积多件衣服才一起熨。
    Collect several clothes and iron them together.
  • 关掉不使用的电灯。
    Switch off lights that are not in use.
  • 错误使用电器可能导致触电、灼伤、死亡和火患。
    Using appliances wrongly can cause electric shock, burns, death and fire.
  • 安全使用电器的方法：
    Safe ways to use appliances:
  • 先擦干手再碰开关；不用湿的手碰开关。
    Dry your hands before touching a switch; never touch a switch with wet hands.
  • 不把尖锐物品插入插座。
    Do not put sharp objects into a socket.
  • 不用电线已损坏的电器。
    Do not use an appliance with a damaged wire.
  • 使用电器时不共用插座（多个电器共用一个插座很危险）。
    Do not share one socket among many appliances (it is dangerous).
  • 插入或拔出插头前，要先关掉电源。
    Switch off the power before plugging in or pulling out a plug.
  • 细读安全使用手册；不自行维修损坏的电器。
    Read the safety manual carefully; do not repair a broken appliance yourself.
  • 不在电线杆附近放风筝，要到空旷的场地去。 extra 补充
    Do not fly kites near electricity poles; go to an open field.
  • 遇到触电事故，绝不能直接用手拉开触电者；要先切断电源，用木棍等绝缘体隔开触电者和带电体，然后送院诊治。
    In an electric-shock accident, never pull the person with your bare hands; cut the power first, use an insulator such as a wooden stick to separate the person from the live object, then get medical help.
