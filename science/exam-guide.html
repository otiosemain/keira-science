📘 C 组答题技巧How to answer Section C (structured questions)

C 组有 4 题，每题 8 分，考的是科学过程技能。下面是每种题型的写法和例子。
Section C has 4 questions of 8 marks each and tests science process skills. Here is how to answer each type, with examples.

✍️ 题型和写法 Question types and how to write them

观察 Observation
写出看到、听到或测量到的事实，包括数据和单位。不要解释原因。
Write what you saw, heard or measured, with numbers and units. Do not explain.
例：30 分钟后，铁盘上的冰块完全融化了。
e.g. After 30 minutes the ice on the iron plate had melted completely.
推断 Inference
对观察作出合理的解释，常用“因为……所以……”。
Give a reasonable explanation of the observation, often with "because… so…".
例：铁盘较容易导热，所以冰块融化得较快。
e.g. The iron plate conducts heat better, so the ice melts faster.
假设 Hypothesis
写出操纵性变数和反应性变数的关系：“……越……，……越……”。
State how the manipulated and responding variables are related: "the more …, the more …".
例：灯泡越多，串联电路中的灯泡越暗。
e.g. The more bulbs in a series circuit, the dimmer they are.
变数 Variables
操纵性＝故意改变的；反应性＝测量或观察的；固定性＝保持不变的。每个都要写完整的名词短语。
Manipulated = changed on purpose; responding = measured or observed; fixed = kept the same. Write each as a full phrase.
例：操纵性变数：盘子的材料（不是“盘子”）。
e.g. Manipulated: the material of the plate (not just "plate").
诠释资料 / 关系 Interpreting data
看表格或图表，说出两个变数一起怎样变化，不要只读一个数字。
From a table or graph, say how the two variables change together; don't just read one number.
例：加热时间越长，水温越高，直到 100°C。
e.g. The longer the heating time, the higher the temperature, up to 100°C.
预测 Prediction
根据规律说出将来会发生什么，最好加上原因或数值。
Use the pattern to say what will happen next, ideally with a reason or a value.
例：蛇减少后，松鼠的数量会增加，因为天敌减少了。
e.g. With fewer snakes, squirrels will increase because they have fewer predators.
结论 Conclusion
回答实验的问题：说出变数之间的关系或答案，并说明假设是否被接受。
Answer the experiment's question: state the relationship or answer, and whether the hypothesis is accepted.
例：铁钉需要水和空气才会生锈。假设被接受。
e.g. An iron nail needs water and air to rust. The hypothesis is accepted.
操作性定义 Operational definition
用“做了什么”和“观察到什么”来下定义。
Define using what you did and what you observed.
例：生锈是把铁钉放在有水和空气的地方，表面出现红褐色物质。
e.g. Rusting is when a nail left with water and air gets a reddish-brown coating.
解释 Explaining a feature
说出特征（是什么）＋作用（怎样帮助它），各得 1 分。
Give the feature (what it is) + the function (how it helps): 1 mark each.
例：北极熊有厚毛（特征），能减少热量散失（作用）。
e.g. A polar bear has thick fur (feature) that reduces heat loss (function).

💡 拿分小贴士 Tips to score

  1. 先圈出题目的动词：说出、解释、预测、比较……每个动词要求的答案不一样。
    Circle the question's command word first: state, explain, predict, compare… each needs a different kind of answer.
  2. 看分数：2 分通常要写 2 个要点。
    Look at the marks: 2 marks usually means 2 points.
  3. 用科学词语：膨胀、凝聚、生产者、操纵性变数……不要用“东西”“那个”。
    Use science words: expand, condense, producer, manipulated variable… not "thing" or "that".
  4. 数据要带单位（cm、°C、分钟）。
    Always add units to numbers (cm, °C, minutes).
  5. 时间分配：75 分钟的试卷，A、B 组约 25 分钟，C 组约 40 分钟，留 10 分钟检查。
    Timing for a 75-minute paper: about 25 min for A and B, 40 min for C, 10 min to check.
