🧪第 1 课 · 1.1 科学程序技能Lesson 1 · 1.1 Science Process Skills

🤔 你有没有想过…… Have you ever wondered…
医生为什么不直接说“你生病了”，而要先量体温、听心跳？
Why doesn't the doctor just say "you're ill" – why measure your temperature and listen to your heart first?
医生像科学家一样，先观察和测量收集资料，再作出推断，答案才可靠。
Like a scientist, the doctor observes and measures to collect data first, then makes an inference – so the answer is reliable.
步骤 1 Step 1

观察和推断 Observing and inferring

观察：用眼、耳、鼻、舌、皮肤等感官（或放大镜、温度计等仪器）收集资料。推断：对观察到的现象作出合理的解释。例：看到钉子被磁铁吸起（观察），推断钉子是磁性物体（推断）。
Observing: using your senses (or instruments like a magnifier or thermometer) to collect information. Inferring: giving a sensible explanation of what you observed. e.g. a nail is picked up by a magnet (observation) → the nail is magnetic (inference).
🚫 别误会！ Don't be fooled!
❌ “钉子是磁性物体”是一个观察。"The nail is magnetic" is an observation.
✅ 那是推断（解释）；观察是“钉子被磁铁吸起”。That is an inference (explanation); the observation is "the nail is picked up by the magnet".
❓ 想一想 · Check
下列哪一个是观察？
Which of these is an observation?
看到的事实才是观察；其他两个是推断。
Only what you see is an observation; the others are inferences.
S1 p.1
步骤 2 Step 2

预测、诠释资料和沟通 Predicting, interpreting data and communicating

预测：根据观察、资料或规律，说出将来会发生的事。诠释资料：说明表格或图表里的资料有什么意思。沟通：用表格、图表、图画或文字记录资料并告诉别人。
Predicting: using observations, data or a pattern to say what will happen. Interpreting data: explaining what a table or graph means. Communicating: recording and sharing data with tables, graphs, drawings or words.
🧠 为什么？ · Why?
“预测”和“随便猜”有什么不同？
How is a prediction different from a guess?
预测是根据观察、资料或规律作出的；随便猜没有根据。
A prediction is based on observations, data or a pattern; a guess has no basis.
步骤 3 Step 3

其他技能 More skills

分类：按相同和不同的地方分组。测量和应用数目：用正确仪器和标准单位测量。运用空间与时间的关系：例如影子的方向和长度随时间改变。操作性定义：根据做了什么和观察到什么来下定义。假设：对变数之间的关系作出可以测试的说明。控制变数和实验也是科学程序技能，一共 12 种。
Classifying: grouping by similarities and differences. Measuring and using numbers: correct instruments and standard units. Space–time relationships: e.g. a shadow's direction and length change over the day. Operational definition: defined by what you did and what you observed. Hypothesis: a testable statement about how variables are related. Controlling variables and experimenting complete the 12 skills.
🏠 自己试试 Try it yourself
早上、中午和傍晚，在同一个地方量一量自己影子的长度，看看有什么变化。
Measure your shadow at the same spot in the morning, at noon and in the evening. How does it change?
❓ 想一想 · Check
“糖在热水里比在冷水里溶解得更快”是哪一种技能？
"Sugar dissolves faster in hot water than in cold" is which skill?
它说出两个变数（水温、溶解快慢）的关系，可以测试。
It links two variables (water temperature, dissolving speed) and can be tested.
S1 p.1-2
⭐ 大道理 Big idea
科学家先观察、测量，再推断、预测——每一步都要有根据。
Scientists observe and measure, then infer and predict – every step needs evidence.

🎚️第 2 课 · 1.2 变数Lesson 2 · 1.2 Variables

🤔 你有没有想过…… Have you ever wondered…
比较哪个牌子的电池最耐用时，为什么要用同一种手电筒？
When testing which battery brand lasts longest, why use the same kind of torch?
只改变电池的牌子，其他都保持不变，结果才公平——这就是控制变数。
Change only the battery brand and keep everything else the same so the result is fair – that is controlling variables.
步骤 1 Step 1

三种变数 Three kinds of variables

操纵性变数：我们故意改变的东西。反应性变数：我们测量或观察的结果。固定性变数：保持不变的东西，让实验公平。
Manipulated variable: what we change on purpose. Responding variable: the result we measure or observe. Constant (fixed) variables: what we keep the same so the test is fair.
🔁 打个比方 Think of it like this
像玩音响：你转的旋钮是操纵性变数，你听到的音量是反应性变数，其他没碰的按钮是固定性变数。
Like a speaker: the knob you turn is the manipulated variable, the loudness you hear is the responding variable, and the buttons you leave alone are constant.
❓ 想一想 · Check
植物实验中，“水的供应”被故意改变，它是什么变数？
In a plant experiment, "water supply" is changed on purpose. Which variable is it?
故意改变的 = 操纵性变数。
Changed on purpose = manipulated.
S1 p.2, p.7
步骤 2 Step 2

影子实验 The shadow experiment

旭文改变木块与光源的距离（操纵性），量屏幕上影子的长度（反应性），木块大小和光源位置不变（固定性）。结果：8 cm 时影子 10 cm；16 cm 时影子 4 cm。结论：物体越靠近光源，影子越大。
Xu Wen changes the distance between block and light (manipulated), measures the shadow length (responding) and keeps the block size and lamp position the same (constant). Result: 8 cm → 10 cm shadow; 16 cm → 4 cm. Conclusion: the closer to the light, the bigger the shadow.
🏠 自己试试 Try it yourself
晚上用手电筒和玩具：把玩具移近、移远手电筒，看墙上的影子怎样变。
At night, move a toy closer to and further from a torch and watch its shadow on the wall.
🧠 为什么？ · Why?
为什么物体越靠近光源，影子越大？
Why is the shadow bigger when the object is closer to the light?
物体越近光源，挡住的光越多，所以影子越大。
Closer to the light, it blocks more light, so the shadow is bigger.
S1 p.2, p.4
⭐ 大道理 Big idea
只改变一样，测量一样，其余保持不变。
Change one thing, measure one thing, keep the rest the same.

⚖️第 3 课 · 1.3 公平测试Lesson 3 · 1.3 Fair Test

🤔 你有没有想过…… Have you ever wondered…
两个同学比赛谁的纸飞机飞得远，一个在室内扔，一个在户外大风中扔，公平吗？
Two pupils compare paper planes – one throws indoors, one outside in strong wind. Is that fair?
不公平！除了纸飞机，风也不一样。公平测试只能改变一个变数。
Not fair! The wind is different too. A fair test changes only one variable.
步骤 1 Step 1

什么是公平测试？ What is a fair test?

实验中只有一个操纵性变数，其他变数都保持不变，就是公平测试。例：测试牙签、回形针、铁钉和吸管能不能导电，每次都用同一个电路，只换物体。
A fair test has only one manipulated variable, with all others kept the same. e.g. testing whether a toothpick, paper clip, nail and straw conduct electricity using the same circuit each time and changing only the object.
🚫 别误会！ Don't be fooled!
❌ 同时改变两样东西，可以更快得到答案。Changing two things at once gets the answer faster.
✅ 同时改变两样，就不知道是哪一样造成结果；每次只改变一样。If two things change you can't tell which caused the result; change one at a time.
🏠 自己试试 Try it yourself
比较两种纸巾吸水：用同样大小的纸巾、同样多的水、同样的时间——这样才公平。
Compare two kitchen towels: same size, same amount of water, same time – that's fair.
❓ 想一想 · Check
导电测试中，什么是操纵性变数？
In the conductor test, what is the manipulated variable?
只改变物体；电路和电池保持不变。
Only the object changes; circuit and cells stay the same.
🧠 为什么？ · Why?
结果中，灯泡在回形针和铁钉时发亮，这说明什么？
The bulb lit with the paper clip and nail. What does that show?
回形针和铁钉是电的导体；牙签和吸管不能让灯泡亮，是绝缘体。
The paper clip and nail are conductors; the toothpick and straw, which didn't light it, are insulators.
S1 p.9
⭐ 大道理 Big idea
公平测试 = 只改变一个变数。
Fair test = change only one variable.

🪵第 4 课 · 1.4 物体的特性与分类Lesson 4 · 1.4 Properties of Objects and Classifying

🤔 你有没有想过…… Have you ever wondered…
为什么很重的轮船能浮在海上，一颗小小的铁钉却会沉下去？
Why does a heavy ship float while a tiny nail sinks?
船身是空心的，里面充满空气，整艘船的密度小于水；铁钉是实心的，密度比水大。
The ship's hull is hollow and full of air, so the whole ship is less dense than water; the solid nail is denser than water.
步骤 1 Step 1

浮和沉 Floating and sinking

木塞、木棒、塑料叉会浮；玻璃弹子、铁钉、石头会沉。木塞能浮，因为它的密度比水小。浮力的操作性定义：把物体放进一盆水里，根据物体在水里的位置来确定浮力。
Corks, sticks and plastic forks float; marbles, nails and stones sink. Cork floats because it is less dense than water. Operational definition of buoyancy: put the object in a basin of water and judge by where it ends up in the water.
🔁 打个比方 Think of it like this
密度像“挤得多紧”：同样大小，挤得越紧（密度越大）的东西越容易沉。
Density is like "how tightly packed": for the same size, more tightly packed (denser) things sink more easily.
🏠 自己试试 Try it yourself
在洗碗盆里试试：橡皮擦、塑料匙、硬币、木夹子……先预测会浮还是会沉，再放进去看。
Try it in a basin: eraser, plastic spoon, coin, wooden peg – predict float or sink first, then test.
🧠 为什么？ · Why?
钢铁做的轮船为什么能浮？
Why can a steel ship float?
船身空心、充满空气，整艘船的密度小于水。
The hull is hollow and full of air, so the whole ship is less dense than water.
S1 p.5
步骤 2 Step 2

让光通过的能力 How well materials let light through

透明体（玻璃）让光完全通过；半透明体（描绘纸、塑料袋）让部分光通过；不透明体（木板、硬纸卡）不让光通过。用手电筒照射材料，透光能力越强，影子越淡。
Transparent (glass) lets light through; translucent (tracing paper, plastic bag) lets some through; opaque (wood, card) lets none through. Shine a torch on it: the more light it lets through, the fainter the shadow.
❓ 想一想 · Check
描绘纸属于哪一类？
Tracing paper is…
描绘纸让部分光通过。
Tracing paper lets some light through.
S1 p.8
步骤 3 Step 3

怎样分类？ How to classify

分类时先找出物体的共同点和不同点，再按一个特征分组，画成分类图。例：按状态把肥皂、盐分成固体，把汽水、牛奶、食油分成液体。
To classify, find similarities and differences, then group by one feature and draw a classification chart. e.g. by state: soap and salt are solids; fizzy drink, milk and cooking oil are liquids.
🚫 别误会！ Don't be fooled!
❌ 同一层分类可以同时用两个特征（例如“红色的”和“会浮的”）。One level of a chart can use two features at once (e.g. "red" and "floats").
✅ 每一次分组只用一个特征，否则会有东西两组都放得进去。Use one feature per split, or some items would fit both groups.
❓ 想一想 · Check
把厨房里的东西分成“会被磁铁吸”和“不会被磁铁吸”，用的是什么特征？
Grouping kitchen items into "attracted by a magnet" and "not attracted" uses which feature?
分类特征 = 物体有没有磁性。
The feature is whether the object is magnetic.
S6 p.1-10
⭐ 大道理 Big idea
物体的特性（浮沉、透光、磁性……）可以测试，也可以用来分类。
Properties (floating, letting light through, magnetism…) can be tested and used to classify.

🔧第 5 课 · 1.5 杠杆与螺钉的实验Lesson 5 · 1.5 Lever and Screw Investigations

🤔 你有没有想过…… Have you ever wondered…
用一根长棒和一块石头，为什么就能把很重的木箱撬起来？
How can a long pole and a stone lift a heavy crate?
棒子和石头组成杠杆：石头是支点。支点离木箱越近，就越省力。
The pole and stone make a lever with the stone as the pivot. The closer the pivot to the crate, the less effort needed.
步骤 1 Step 1

杠杆的三个点 The three points of a lever

支点：杠杆转动时不动的点（石头）。动力点：用力的地方。阻力点：重物的位置（木箱）。支点离阻力点越近，越省力；支点离动力点越近，越费力。
Pivot (fulcrum): the point that stays still (the stone). Effort point: where you push. Load point: where the load is (the crate). The closer the pivot to the load, the less effort; the closer to the effort, the more effort.
🎬 动画 Animation：杠杆：支点的位置 Lever: where the fulcrum is
支点在中间：所需的力和重物差不多。
Fulcrum in the middle: the effort is about the same as the load.
支点移近阻力点（重物）：所需的力变小，比较省力。
Fulcrum moved closer to the load: less effort is needed.
支点移近动力点（用力的地方）：所需的力变大，比较费力。
Fulcrum moved closer to the effort: more effort is needed.
1 / 3
🧠 为什么？ · Why?
用长棒撬石头时，为什么要把支点放得靠近石头？
When levering a rock, why put the pivot close to the rock?
支点离阻力点（石头）越近，越省力，用较小的力就能撬起。
The closer the pivot to the load (rock), the less effort needed.
S1 p.6
步骤 2 Step 2

螺钉的实验 The screw experiment

假设：螺纹越密，把螺钉钻入木板所需的力越小。操纵性变数 = 螺纹的紧密程度；反应性变数 = 钻入木板所需的力。
Hypothesis: the closer the threads, the less force needed to drive the screw into wood. Manipulated = how close the threads are; responding = force needed.
❓ 想一想 · Check
螺钉实验中，反应性变数是什么？
In the screw experiment, what is the responding variable?
我们测量的是所需的力。
We measure the force needed.
S1 p.6
⭐ 大道理 Big idea
杠杆和螺钉都能让工作更省力——要用实验和变数来证明。
Levers and screws make work easier – and we prove it with experiments and variables.

📝第 6 课 · 1.6 课本实验：做实验的步骤Lesson 6 · 1.6 Textbook experiments: steps of an experiment

🤔 你有没有想过…… Have you ever wondered…
科学家怎样确定一个想法是对还是错？
How do scientists find out whether an idea is right?
他们做实验：先作出假设，控制变数，记录结果，再看结果支持还是不支持假设。
They experiment: make a hypothesis, control variables, record results, then see whether the results support the hypothesis.
步骤 1 Step 1

做实验的步骤 Steps of an experiment

确认问题 → 作出假设 → 列出变数 → 准备材料和用具 → 进行实验 → 记录观察结果 → 诠释资料 → 作出推断 → 鉴定假设是否被接受 → 作出结论 → 写报告。
Identify the question → hypothesise → list variables → prepare materials → carry out → record results → interpret data → infer → decide whether the hypothesis is accepted → conclude → write a report.
❓ 想一想 · Check
作出假设之后，下一步是？
After making a hypothesis, the next step is…
先确定要改变、测量和保持不变的东西，才开始做。
Decide what to change, measure and keep the same before you start.
S6 p.2-10
步骤 2 Step 2

跳跃的青蛙和荡秋千 The jumping frog and the swing

青蛙实验：操纵性 = 塑料圈的大小；反应性 = 跳跃的距离（用米尺量，单位 cm）。秋千（摆）实验：摆的快慢的操作性定义是在固定时间（如 20 秒）内来回摆动的次数——次数越多，摆得越快。结果：绳子越长，摆得越慢。
Frog: manipulated = size of the plastic loop; responding = jump distance (measured with a metre rule in cm). Swing (pendulum): the operational definition of speed is the number of swings in a fixed time (e.g. 20 s) – more swings means faster. Result: the longer the string, the slower the swing.
🏠 自己试试 Try it yourself
用绳子和钥匙做一个摆：先用短绳，再用长绳，各数 20 秒内摆动几次。
Make a pendulum with string and a key: count swings in 20 seconds with a short string, then a long one.
🧠 为什么？ · Why?
为什么要用“20 秒内摆动的次数”来表示摆的快慢？
Why use "number of swings in 20 seconds" to describe how fast a pendulum swings?
“快慢”很难直接比较；数固定时间内的次数可以测量和比较，结果更客观。
"Fast" is hard to compare directly; counting swings in a fixed time gives a measurable, comparable result.
S6 p.2-10
⭐ 大道理 Big idea
实验让我们用证据来检验想法：假设 → 变数 → 结果 → 结论。
Experiments test ideas with evidence: hypothesis → variables → results → conclusion.

🧪1.1 科学程序技能1.1 Science Process SkillsS1 p.1-2, J-1, 课本 Textbook S6 p.1-10

  • 科学程序技能有 12 种，是科学家做研究时使用的方法。
    There are 12 science process skills. They are the methods scientists use when they investigate.
  • 观察：用眼睛、耳朵、鼻子等感官（或仪器）收集资料。例：用放大镜看叶子。 extra 补充
    Observing: using your senses (or instruments) to gather information. e.g. using a magnifying glass to look at a leaf.
  • 分类：根据相同和不同的地方，把物体分组。例：把形状不同的积木分成三角形、方形和圆形。 extra 补充
    Classifying: grouping objects by their similarities and differences. e.g. sorting blocks into triangles, squares and circles.
  • 测量和应用数目：用正确的仪器和标准单位来测量。例：用尺量纸飞机飞行的距离。 extra 补充
    Measuring and using numbers: using the right tool and standard units correctly. e.g. using a ruler to measure how far a paper plane flies.
  • 推断：对观察到的现象，作出合理的解释。例：钉子能被磁铁吸起，推断钉子是磁性物体。 extra 补充
    Inferring: giving a reasonable explanation for what you observed. e.g. the nail is picked up by a magnet, so we infer that the nail is a magnetic object.
  • 预测：根据观察、资料或规律，说出将来会发生的事。例：把青蛙关在密封的玻璃瓶里，预测青蛙会死亡。 extra 补充
    Predicting: saying what will happen in the future based on observations, data or a pattern. e.g. a frog sealed in a jar — we predict the frog will die.
  • 沟通：用表格、图表、图画或文字，把资料记录和告诉别人。 extra 补充
    Communicating: recording and sharing information using tables, charts, drawings or words.
  • 运用空间与时间的关系：描述物体的位置、形状、大小随时间的变化。例：影子的方向和长度会随着时间改变。 extra 补充
    Using space-time relationship: describing how position, shape or size of something changes with time. e.g. the direction and length of a shadow change through the day.
  • 控制变数：找出并控制操纵性变数、反应性变数和固定性变数。 extra 补充
    Controlling variables: identifying and controlling the manipulated, responding and fixed variables.
  • 操作性定义：根据做了什么和观察到什么来下定义。例：把要测试的物质涂在红色和蓝色石蕊试纸上，根据颜色变化来确定物质的酸碱性。 extra 补充
    Defining operationally: defining something by what you do and what you observe. e.g. rub a substance on red and blue litmus paper and decide if it is acidic or alkaline from the colour change.
  • 诠释资料：说明表格或图表里的资料有什么意思。例：磁铁 Q 吸起的图钉最多，所以磁力最强。 extra 补充
    Interpreting data: explaining what the data in a table or graph means. e.g. magnet Q picks up the most pins, so its magnetic force is the strongest.
  • 假设：对变数之间的关系作出可以测试的说明。例：糖在热水里比在冷水里溶解得更快。 extra 补充
    Making a hypothesis: a testable statement about the relationship between variables. e.g. sugar dissolves faster in hot water than in cold water.
  • 实验：通过做实验收集资料、解释资料、作出结论。 extra 补充
    Experimenting: carrying out an investigation to collect and interpret data and draw a conclusion.
📌 记住！ Remember!
先观察，再推断；推断是对观察的解释。
First observe, then infer: an inference explains an observation.
预测是说出将来会发生的事。
A prediction says what will happen in the future.

测量时要用标准单位，例如厘米（cm）、毫升（ml）、克（g），这样别人才能看得懂你的结果。
Use standard units such as centimetres (cm), millilitres (ml) and grams (g) so others can understand your results.

🎚️1.2 变数1.2 VariablesS1 p.2, p.4, p.7, J-1, J-2, 课本 Textbook S6 p.1-10

  • 在实验里，我们用变数来控制和比较结果。变数有三种：
    In an experiment we use variables to control and compare results. There are three kinds:
  • 操纵性变数：我们故意改变的东西。
    Manipulated variable: the thing we deliberately change.
  • 反应性变数：我们测量或观察的结果。
    Responding variable: the result we measure or observe.
  • 固定性变数：保持不变的东西，这样实验才公平。
    Fixed variable: the thing we keep the same, so the test is fair.
  • 例子（植物实验）：操纵性变数 = 水的供应；反应性变数 = 植物的生长情况；固定性变数 = 植物的种类。
    Example (plant experiment): manipulated variable = water supply; responding variable = how well the plant grows; fixed variable = type of plant.
  • 例子（影子实验，旭文）：操纵性变数 = 木块与光源的距离；反应性变数 = 屏幕上影子的长度；固定性变数 = 木块的大小、光源的位置等。
    Example (shadow experiment): manipulated variable = distance between the wooden block and the light source; responding variable = length of the shadow on the screen; fixed variables = size of the block, the light source, etc.
  • 假设：物体与光源的距离越近，影子越大。结果：8 cm 时影子 10 cm；16 cm 时影子 4 cm。
    Hypothesis: the nearer the object is to the light source, the bigger the shadow. Results: at 8 cm the shadow is 10 cm; at 16 cm the shadow is 4 cm.
  • 结论：物体靠近光源，影子越大。因为物体越近光源，挡住的光越多。
    Conclusion: the nearer the object is to the light source, the bigger the shadow, because it blocks more light.
  • 植物向光实验：把木箱上的洞口 P 盖住后，植物会向洞口 Q 生长。结论：植物会对阳光产生反应，朝阳光的方向生长。
    Plant-and-light experiment: when hole P is covered, the plants grow towards hole Q. Conclusion: plants respond to sunlight and grow towards it.

⚖️1.3 公平测试1.3 Fair TestS1 p.9, J-2, 课本 Textbook S6 p.1-10

  • 实验只有一个操纵性变数，其他变数都保持不变，就是公平测试。
    If an experiment has only one manipulated variable and all other variables stay the same, it is a fair test.
  • 例子：测试牙签、回形针、铁钉和吸管能不能导电。灯泡在回形针和铁钉时发亮，在牙签和吸管时不亮。
    Example: testing whether a toothpick, paper clip, iron nail and drinking straw conduct electricity. The bulb lights up with the paper clip and iron nail, but not with the toothpick and straw.
  • 这是公平测试：只有一个操纵性变数（物体的种类），而且每次都使用相同的电路装置。
    This is a fair test: there is only one manipulated variable (the type of object) and the same circuit set-up is used each time.
  • 结论：回形针和铁钉是电的导体；牙签和吸管是电的绝缘体。
    Conclusion: the paper clip and iron nail are electrical conductors; the toothpick and straw are electrical insulators.
🧪 公平测试：哪些物体能导电？ Fair test: which objects conduct electricity? S1 p.9, 课本 Textbook S6 p.1-10
  1. 假设 Hypothesising
    金属做的物体能导电。
    Objects made of metal conduct electricity.
  2. 控制变数 Controlling variables
    操纵：物体的种类；反应：灯泡亮不亮；固定：同一个电路。
    Manipulated: type of object; responding: does the bulb light; fixed: the same circuit.
  3. 实验 Experimenting
    把牙签、回形针、铁钉、吸管逐一接进电路。
    Connect a toothpick, paper clip, iron nail and straw into the circuit one at a time.
  4. 观察 Observing
    回形针和铁钉使灯泡发亮；牙签和吸管不亮。
    The bulb lights with the paper clip and nail, not with the toothpick or straw.
  5. 诠释资料 Interpreting data
    回形针和铁钉是导体；牙签和吸管是绝缘体。
    Paper clip and nail are conductors; toothpick and straw are insulators.

公平测试只能有一个操纵性变数。
A fair test has only one manipulated variable.

🪵1.4 物体的特性与分类1.4 Properties of Objects and ClassifyingS1 p.5, p.8, J-1, J-2, 课本 Textbook S6 p.1-10

  • 物体放进水里，有的会浮，有的会沉。木塞、木棒、塑料叉浮在水面；玻璃弹子、铁钉、石头沉下去。
    Objects put in water either float or sink. A cork, a wooden stick and a plastic fork float; a marble, an iron nail and a stone sink.
  • 浮力的操作性定义：把物体放进一盆水里，根据物体在水里的位置来确定物体的浮力。
    Operational definition of buoyancy: put the object in a basin of water and decide its buoyancy from its position in the water.
  • 木塞能浮在水面，是因为它的密度比水小。轮船很重却能浮，因为船身是空心的，里面充满空气，所以整艘船的密度小于水。
    A cork floats because its density is smaller than water. A ship is heavy yet floats because its hollow hull is filled with air, so the ship's overall density is smaller than water.
  • 根据让光通过的能力，物体可以分成三类：透明体（玻璃、眼镜片）、半透明体（描绘纸、塑料袋）、不透明体（木板、硬纸卡）。
    By how much light passes through, objects are classified into three groups: transparent (glass, spectacle lens), translucent (tracing paper, plastic bag) and opaque (wooden board, cardboard).
  • 透光能力的操作性定义：用手电筒照射材料，根据影子的清晰度来确定材料的透光能力。透光能力越强，影子越淡。
    Operational definition of how well a material lets light through: shine a torch on the material and judge from how clear the shadow is. The more light a material lets through, the fainter the shadow.
  • 实验：干电池越多，灯泡越亮。（1 个：暗淡；2 个：明亮；3 个：更明亮）
    Experiment: the more dry cells, the brighter the bulb. (1 cell: dim; 2 cells: bright; 3 cells: brighter)
  • 分类活动：把汽水、矿泉水、牛奶、食油、洗衣液、肥皂、盐、牙膏和水果等物质，先找出共同点和不同点，再分组并画成分类图。例：按状态分成固体（肥皂、盐、水果）和液体（汽水、牛奶、食油）。 📗 课本 S6 p.8
    Classifying activity: for substances such as fizzy drink, mineral water, milk, cooking oil, liquid detergent, soap, salt, toothpaste and fruit, first find their similarities and differences, then group them and draw a classification chart. e.g. by state: solids (soap, salt, fruit) and liquids (fizzy drink, milk, cooking oil).
💡 你知道吗？ Did you know? extra 补充
铁很重会沉，但用铁造的轮船里面是空心的，充满空气，所以能浮在海上。
Iron sinks, but an iron ship is hollow and full of air, so it floats.

🔧1.5 杠杆与螺钉的实验1.5 Lever and Screw InvestigationsS1 p.6, J-1, 课本 Textbook S6 p.1-10

  • 用棒子和石头撬起木箱：石头是支点，用力的地方是动力点，木箱是阻力点。
    Lifting a box with a stick and a stone: the stone is the fulcrum, where you push is the effort, and the box is the load.
  • 支点离阻力点（木箱）越近，越省力；支点离动力点越近，越费力。
    The nearer the fulcrum is to the load (the box), the less effort needed; the nearer the fulcrum is to the effort, the more effort needed.
  • 假设：螺纹越密，把螺钉钻入木板所需的力越小。操纵性变数 = 螺纹的紧密程度；反应性变数 = 把螺钉钻入木板所需的力。
    Hypothesis: the closer the screw threads, the smaller the force needed to drive the screw into wood. Manipulated variable = how close the threads are; responding variable = force needed to drive the screw into the wood.
📌 记住！ Remember!
支点离阻力点越近，越省力。
The closer the fulcrum is to the load, the less effort you need.

📝1.6 课本实验：做实验的步骤1.6 Textbook experiments: steps of an experiment课本 Textbook S6 p.2-10

  • 做实验的步骤：确认问题 → 作出假设 → 列出变数 → 准备材料和用具 → 进行实验 → 记录观察结果 → 诠释资料 → 作出推断 → 鉴定假设是否被接受 → 作出结论 → 写实验报告。 📗 课本 S6 p.2-5
    Steps of an experiment: identify the problem → make a hypothesis → list the variables → prepare materials → carry out the experiment → record observations → interpret data → make an inference → check whether the hypothesis is accepted → draw a conclusion → write an experiment report.
  • 实验 1 跳跃的青蛙：问题——塑料圈的大小会影响青蛙模型跳跃的远近吗？假设例子——塑料圈越小，青蛙模型跳得越远。 📗 课本 S6 p.2
    Experiment 1, the jumping frog: problem — does the size of the plastic loop affect how far the frog model jumps? Example hypothesis — the smaller the plastic loop, the farther the frog model jumps.
  • 青蛙实验的变数：操纵性变数 = 塑料圈的大小；反应性变数 = 青蛙模型跳跃的距离；固定性变数 = 同一个青蛙模型、同一个盒子和起跳的地方。 📗 课本 S6 p.3
    Variables in the frog experiment: manipulated = size of the plastic loop; responding = distance the frog jumps; fixed = the same frog model, the same box and starting point.
  • 用米尺测量跳跃的距离，读取以厘米 (cm) 为单位的数字，把结果记录在表格里，再根据结果预测：例如塑料圈的长方形长度是 30 cm 时，青蛙会跳多远。 📗 课本 S6 p.5
    Measure the jump with a metre rule, reading the number in centimetres (cm), record the results in a table, then predict from them: e.g. how far the frog will jump when the rectangle for the loop is 30 cm long.
  • 实验 2 荡秋千的诀窍：问题——什么因素影响秋千（摆）的快慢？例：绳子较长的秋千会荡得比较快吗？操纵性变数 = 绳子的长度；反应性变数 = 摆的快慢；固定性变数 = 摆锤的质量、开始摆动的位置。 📗 课本 S6 p.7
    Experiment 2, the swing: problem — what affects how fast a swing (pendulum) swings? e.g. does a swing with a longer rope swing faster? Manipulated = length of the string; responding = how fast it swings; fixed = mass of the bob, starting position.
  • 摆的快慢的操作性定义：在固定的时间内（例如 20 秒），摆来回摆动的次数；次数越多，摆得越快。结果：绳子越长，摆得越慢。 extra 补充
    Operational definition of how fast a pendulum swings: the number of swings in a fixed time (e.g. 20 seconds); more swings means faster. Result: the longer the string, the slower it swings.
  • 课后挑战：妈妈说加入发酵粉能让面团变大。可以设计实验验证假设“发酵粉越多，面团越大”：操纵性变数 = 发酵粉的分量；反应性变数 = 面团的大小；固定性变数 = 面粉和水的分量、等待的时间。 📗 课本 S6 p.10
    Challenge: Mum says baking powder makes dough bigger. Test the hypothesis "the more baking powder, the bigger the dough": manipulated = amount of baking powder; responding = size of the dough; fixed = amount of flour and water, waiting time.
⚠️ 常见错误 Common mistakes
  1. 概念混淆 Concept mix-up
    把观察和推断混淆：观察是用感官看到、听到的事实；推断是对观察的解释。
    Mixing up observation and inference: an observation is what your senses detect; an inference explains it.
  2. 答题错误 Exam-answer error
    假设要写出两个变数的关系：“……越……，……越……”。只写“植物会长高”不是假设。
    A hypothesis must link two variables: "the more/the …, the more/the …". "The plant will grow" is not a hypothesis.
  3. 概念混淆 Concept mix-up
    操纵性变数是我们故意改变的；反应性变数是我们测量的。不要调换。
    The manipulated variable is what you change on purpose; the responding variable is what you measure. Don't swap them.
  4. 用词陷阱 Wording trap
    变数要写完整：写“盘子的材料”，不要只写“盘子”；写“完全融化所需的时间”，不要只写“时间”。
    Write variables in full: "the material of the plate", not just "plate"; "time taken to melt completely", not just "time".
  5. 概念混淆 Concept mix-up
    公平测试只改变一个变数，其他都保持不变。
    A fair test changes only one variable and keeps the others the same.
  6. 答题错误 Exam-answer error
    测量结果要写单位（cm、°C、秒）。
    Always write the unit with a measurement (cm, °C, seconds).
  7. 用词陷阱 Wording trap
    ❌ 把“推断”和“预测”混用。✅ 推断解释已经发生的事；预测说出将来会发生的事。
    ❌ Mixing up inferring and predicting. ✅ An inference explains what has happened; a prediction says what will happen.
  8. 用词陷阱 Wording trap
    ❌ 固定性变数只写“一样”。✅ 写出什么保持一样：“干电池的数量保持不变。”
    ❌ Writing only "the same". ✅ Say what stays the same: "the number of dry cells is kept the same."
  9. 概念混淆 Concept mix-up
    ❌ 观察只能用眼睛。✅ 观察可以用所有感官，也可以用放大镜、温度计等仪器。
    ❌ Observing only uses the eyes. ✅ Use all your senses and instruments like magnifiers and thermometers.
  10. 概念混淆 Concept mix-up
    ❌ 半透明体完全不让光通过。✅ 半透明体让部分光通过；不透明体才完全不让光通过。
    ❌ Translucent things let no light through. ✅ Translucent lets some through; opaque lets none.
  11. 看图/读表错误 Diagram/table reading
    ❌ 读图表时不看轴的名称和单位。✅ 先看横轴、纵轴写的是什么、单位是什么，再读数。
    ❌ Reading a graph without checking the axes. ✅ First check what each axis shows and its unit.
  12. 看图/读表错误 Diagram/table reading
    ❌ 画线形图时把反应性变数放在横轴。✅ 一般把操纵性变数放在横轴，反应性变数放在纵轴。
    ❌ Putting the responding variable on the x-axis. ✅ Usually the manipulated variable goes on the x-axis, the responding variable on the y-axis.
  13. 答题错误 Exam-answer error
    ❌ 结果和假设相反，却写“假设被接受”。✅ 根据结果决定：符合 → 接受；不符合 → 拒绝。
    ❌ Writing "accepted" when results contradict the hypothesis. ✅ Decide from the results: match → accept; don't match → reject.
  14. 答题错误 Exam-answer error
    ❌ 操作性定义写成词典的定义。✅ 要写做了什么 + 观察到什么。
    ❌ Writing a dictionary definition as an operational definition. ✅ Write what you did + what you observed.
  15. 答题错误 Exam-answer error
    ❌ 分类时一次用两个特征。✅ 每次分组只用一个特征，并写出组名。
    ❌ Using two features in one split. ✅ Use one feature per split and name each group.

The nail is made of a magnetic material (iron), so the magnet attracts it.
💬 评分理由 Mark by mark
  • 弱答案只是把观察再说一遍，没有解释原因。
    The weak answer just repeats the observation; it gives no reason.
  • 推断要用“因为/所以”解释看到的现象。
    An inference explains what you saw, often with "because/so".
🧩 答题句型 Answer frame
句型：“……可能是因为……是磁性物体/由铁制成。”
Frame: "… probably because … is magnetic / made of iron."
观察 Observation 示范 Example 3
把三种物体放进一盆水里：木塞、铁钉、塑料叉。
A cork, a nail and a plastic fork are put into a basin of water.
❓
写出一个观察。
State one observation.
[1 分 marks]
❌ 弱答案 Weak answer · 0/1
木塞的密度比水小。
Cork is less dense than water.
✅ 满分答案 Full-mark answer · 1/1
木塞和塑料叉浮在水面，铁钉沉到盆底。
The cork and fork float on the surface; the nail sinks to the bottom.
💬 评分理由 Mark by mark
  • “密度比水小”是解释（推断），不是看到的。
    "Less dense" is an explanation (inference), not something you see.
🧩 答题句型 Answer frame
句型：“……浮在水面/沉到底；……。”只写看到的。
Frame: "… floats / sinks; …." Write only what you see.
分类 Classifying 示范 Example 4
物体：玻璃、描绘纸、木板、塑料袋、眼镜片、硬纸卡。
Items: glass, tracing paper, wooden board, plastic bag, spectacle lens, card.
❓
根据让光通过的能力，把它们分成三组。
Classify them into three groups by how well they let light through.
[3 分 marks]
❌ 弱答案 Weak answer · 1/3
玻璃、眼镜片；其他。
Glass, lens; others.
✅ 满分答案 Full-mark answer · 3/3
透明体：玻璃、眼镜片；半透明体：描绘纸、塑料袋；不透明体：木板、硬纸卡。
Transparent: glass, lens; translucent: tracing paper, plastic bag; opaque: board, card.
💬 评分理由 Mark by mark
  • 每组 1 分：组名 + 正确成员。
    1 mark per group: name + correct members.
  • “其他”不是一组。
    "Others" is not a group.
🧩 答题句型 Answer frame
句型：“组 1（特征）：……；组 2（特征）：……；组 3（特征）：……。”
Frame: "Group 1 (feature): …; group 2: …; group 3: …."
诠释资料（表格/图表） Interpreting data (table/graph) 示范 Example 5
影子实验：木块与光源的距离 8 cm，影子 10 cm；12 cm，影子 7 cm；16 cm，影子 4 cm。
Shadow test: 8 cm → 10 cm shadow; 12 cm → 7 cm; 16 cm → 4 cm.
❓
说出距离和影子长度的关系。
State the relationship between distance and shadow length.
[1 分 marks]
❌ 弱答案 Weak answer · 0/1
影子会变。
The shadow changes.
✅ 满分答案 Full-mark answer · 1/1
木块与光源的距离越近，影子越长（越大）。
The closer the block to the light, the longer (bigger) the shadow.
💬 评分理由 Mark by mark
  • 关系要说出两个变数和变化方向（越……越……）。
    Name both variables and the direction (the … the …).
🧩 答题句型 Answer frame
句型：“……越……，……越……。”
Frame: "The …-er …, the …-er …."
预测 Prediction 示范 Example 6
同上的影子实验。
Same shadow experiment.
❓
预测距离 20 cm 时影子的长度。
Predict the shadow length at 20 cm.
[1 分 marks]
❌ 弱答案 Weak answer · 0/1
10 cm。
10 cm.
✅ 满分答案 Full-mark answer · 1/1
影子会比 4 cm 更短（大约 1 cm 左右）。
The shadow will be shorter than 4 cm (around 1 cm).
💬 评分理由 Mark by mark
  • 规律：距离越远，影子越短；20 cm 比 16 cm 远，所以要比 4 cm 短。
    Pattern: further away → shorter; 20 cm is further than 16 cm, so shorter than 4 cm.
  • 给出合理的范围或数值都可以得分。
    A sensible range or value scores.
🧩 答题句型 Answer frame
句型：“根据规律，……时……会比……更……（大约……）。”
Frame: "Following the pattern, at … the … will be …-er than … (about …)."
操作性定义 Operational definition 示范 Example 7
用手电筒照射不同的材料，观察屏幕上的影子。
A torch shines on different materials; the shadows on a screen are observed.
❓
写出“透光能力”的操作性定义。
Give an operational definition of "ability to let light through".
[1 分 marks]
❌ 弱答案 Weak answer · 0/1
透光能力是光能不能穿过。
It's whether light can pass through.
✅ 满分答案 Full-mark answer · 1/1
用手电筒照射材料，根据屏幕上影子的深浅（清晰度）来确定：影子越淡，透光能力越强。
Shine a torch on the material and judge by how dark the shadow is: the fainter the shadow, the more light it lets through.
💬 评分理由 Mark by mark
  • 要说出“做了什么”（用手电筒照射）和“观察什么”（影子深浅）。
    Say what was done (torch) and what was observed (shadow darkness).
🧩 答题句型 Answer frame
句型：“用……（做什么），根据……（观察到什么）来确定……。”
Frame: "By … (doing), judge … from … (what is observed)."
结论 / 关系 Conclusion / relationship 示范 Example 8
（练习情境）摆的实验：绳长 20 cm，20 秒摆 22 次；40 cm，16 次；60 cm，13 次。
Pendulum: 20 cm → 22 swings in 20 s; 40 cm → 16; 60 cm → 13.
❓
作出结论，并说出假设“绳子越长，摆得越快”是否被接受。
Conclude, and say whether the hypothesis "the longer the string, the faster it swings" is accepted.
[2 分 marks]
❌ 弱答案 Weak answer · 0/2
绳子很重要，假设被接受。
String matters; hypothesis accepted.
✅ 满分答案 Full-mark answer · 2/2
绳子越长，摆得越慢（固定时间内摆动次数越少）。所以假设被拒绝。
The longer the string, the slower the swing (fewer swings in the time). So the hypothesis is rejected.
💬 评分理由 Mark by mark
  • 第 1 分：结论要根据结果写出关系。
    Mark 1: the conclusion states the relationship from the results.
  • 第 2 分：结果和假设相反，要写“被拒绝”。
    Mark 2: results contradict the hypothesis, so it is rejected.
🧩 答题句型 Answer frame
句型：“……越……，……越……。所以假设被接受/被拒绝。”
Frame: "The more …, the … . So the hypothesis is accepted/rejected."

📌 一定要记住 Must-know facts
  • 先观察，再推断；推断是对观察的解释。
    First observe, then infer: an inference explains an observation.
  • 预测是说出将来会发生的事。
    A prediction says what will happen in the future.
  • 改变的 = 操纵性变数；测量的 = 反应性变数；不变的 = 固定性变数。
    What you change = manipulated; what you measure = responding; what stays the same = fixed.
  • 公平测试只能有一个操纵性变数。
    A fair test has only one manipulated variable.
  • 支点离阻力点越近，越省力。
    The closer the fulcrum is to the load, the less effort you need.
⚠️ 别犯错 Watch out
  • 把观察和推断混淆：观察是用感官看到、听到的事实；推断是对观察的解释。
    Mixing up observation and inference: an observation is what your senses detect; an inference explains it.
  • 假设要写出两个变数的关系：“……越……，……越……”。只写“植物会长高”不是假设。
    A hypothesis must link two variables: "the more/the …, the more/the …". "The plant will grow" is not a hypothesis.
  • 操纵性变数是我们故意改变的；反应性变数是我们测量的。不要调换。
    The manipulated variable is what you change on purpose; the responding variable is what you measure. Don't swap them.
