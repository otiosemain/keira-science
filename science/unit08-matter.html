🧱第 1 课 · 8.1 物质的形态Lesson 1 · 8.1 States of Matter

🤔 你有没有想过…… Have you ever wondered…
为什么果汁倒进不同的杯子会变成杯子的形状，冰块却不会？
Why does juice take the shape of any cup, but an ice cube doesn't?
果汁是液体，没有固定的形状；冰块是固体，有固定的形状。
Juice is a liquid with no fixed shape; ice is a solid with a fixed shape.
步骤 1 Step 1

什么是物质？ What is matter?

世界万物几乎都由物质组成。物质有质量，也会占据空间。连看不见的空气也是物质：吹了气的气球比没吹气的重。彩虹是光形成的现象，不是物质。
Almost everything is made of matter. Matter has mass and takes up space. Even invisible air is matter: a blown-up balloon is heavier than a flat one. A rainbow is made by light, so it is not matter.
❓ 想一想 · Check
下列哪一个不是物质？
Which of these is NOT matter?
彩虹是光的折射和反射形成的现象。
A rainbow is formed by light being bent and reflected.
🧠 为什么？ · Why?
用衣架天平怎样证明空气有质量？
How does a coat-hanger balance show that air has mass?
两边挂着大小相同的气球，其中一个吹满空气后，那一边会下沉，表示空气有质量。
Hang two balloons; when one is filled with air, that side goes down, showing air has mass.
S6 p.162-163
步骤 2 Step 2

固体、液体和气体 Solids, liquids and gases

固体有固定的形状和体积。液体会流动，没有固定的形状，但有固定的体积。气体没有固定的形状和体积，但同样占据空间，也有质量。
A solid has a fixed shape and volume. A liquid flows, has no fixed shape but a fixed volume. A gas has neither, yet still takes up space and has mass.
🏠 自己试试 Try it yourself
用量杯量 200 mℓ 的水，倒进碗里，再倒回量杯。水的形状变了，体积变了吗？
Measure 200 mℓ of water, pour it into a bowl, then back into the jug. The shape changed – did the volume?
❓ 想一想 · Check
哪一种形态有固定的体积，但没有固定的形状？
Which state has a fixed volume but no fixed shape?
液体会流动，跟着容器改变形状，体积不变。
A liquid flows into the container's shape but keeps its volume.
S6 p.166
步骤 3 Step 3

一切都由粒子组成 Everything is made of particles

所有物质都由粒子组成。固体的粒子紧密排列，只在固定的位置上振动；液体的粒子之间有距离，可以短距离移动；气体的粒子之间距离很大，移动得很快，所以气体可以被压缩。
All matter is made of particles. In a solid they are packed tightly and only vibrate in place; in a liquid they have small gaps and move short distances; in a gas they are far apart and move fast, so a gas can be compressed.
🔁 打个比方 Think of it like this
固体像周会时排好队的学生；液体像在礼堂里四处走动的学生；气体像在操场上踢足球、跑来跑去的学生。
A solid is like pupils lined up at assembly; a liquid like pupils wandering around the hall; a gas like pupils running around the field playing football.
🚫 别误会！ Don't be fooled!
❌ 固体的粒子完全不会动。Particles in a solid don't move at all.
✅ 固体的粒子会在固定的位置上振动，只是不能到处移动。Solid particles vibrate in fixed positions – they just can't move around.
🧠 为什么？ · Why?
为什么注射器里的空气可以被压缩，装满水就很难压？
Why can you squash air in a syringe but hardly squash water?
气体粒子之间的距离很大，可以被挤近；液体粒子之间的距离小得多。
Gas particles are far apart and can be pushed closer; liquid particles are already much closer.
S6 p.167-168
步骤 4 Step 4

水的三种形态 The three states of water

水有三种形态：冰块（固体）、水（液体）和水蒸气（气体）。它们都是水，只是形态不同。
Water has three states: ice (solid), water (liquid) and water vapour (gas). All are water, just in different states.
❓ 想一想 · Check
水蒸气是水的哪一种形态？
Water vapour is water in which state?
水蒸气是看不见的气体。
Water vapour is an invisible gas.
S6 p.169-170
⭐ 大道理 Big idea
物质有质量、占空间，由粒子组成；粒子排得越松，越像气体。
Matter has mass, takes up space and is made of particles; the looser the particles, the more gas-like it is.

💧第 2 课 · 8.2 水的形态变化和水循环Lesson 2 · 8.2 Changes in the State of Water and the Water Cycle

🤔 你有没有想过…… Have you ever wondered…
为什么洗完热水澡，浴室的镜子会变得模糊？
Why does the bathroom mirror fog up after a hot shower?
热水产生的水蒸气碰到较冷的镜子，散热凝聚成许多小水珠，镜子就模糊了。
Water vapour from the hot water touches the cooler mirror, loses heat and condenses into tiny droplets, so the mirror fogs.
步骤 1 Step 1

受热时的变化 Changes when heat is gained

融化：固体 → 液体（冰块融化成水）。蒸发：液体 → 气体，在一般温度下就会发生（晾干衣服）。沸腾：水加热到沸点 100°C 时变成水蒸气。这三个过程都需要受热。
Melting: solid → liquid (ice to water). Evaporation: liquid → gas, even at normal temperatures (clothes drying). Boiling: water at 100°C turning to vapour. All three need heat gained.
🎬 动画 Animation：物质的形态变化（粒子） Changes of state (particles)
固体：粒子紧密排列，只在原位振动。
Solid: particles are packed tightly and only vibrate in place.
冰块受热融化成水：粒子之间有距离，可以振动和短距离移动。
Ice melts when heated: particles have gaps and can move short distances.
水受热蒸发成水蒸气：粒子之间的距离很大，移动得很快。
Water evaporates when heated: particles are far apart and move fast.
水蒸气散热凝聚成水；水再散热就会凝固成冰。
Water vapour condenses when cooled; cool water further and it freezes into ice.
1 / 4
🚫 别误会！ Don't be fooled!
❌ 融化和溶解是一样的。Melting and dissolving are the same.
✅ 融化：冰受热变成水；溶解：糖在水中散开——是两回事。Melting: ice gains heat and becomes water; dissolving: sugar spreads into water – different things.
❓ 想一想 · Check
晾在阳光下的湿衣服变干了，水经历了什么过程？
Wet clothes dry in the sun. What happened to the water?
液体受热变成气体 = 蒸发。
Liquid gaining heat to become gas = evaporation.
S6 p.171-177
步骤 2 Step 2

散热时的变化 Changes when heat is lost

凝固：液体 → 固体（水结成冰棍）。凝聚：气体 → 液体（锅盖底面的小水珠、冷饮杯外壁的小水珠）。这两个过程都需要散热。
Freezing: liquid → solid (ice lollies). Condensation: gas → liquid (droplets under a pot lid, on a cold drink glass). Both need heat lost.
🚫 别误会！ Don't be fooled!
❌ 冷饮杯外的水珠是从杯子里漏出来的。The water outside a cold glass leaked out.
✅ 是空气中的水蒸气碰到冰冷的杯子，凝聚成水珠。It is water vapour in the air condensing on the cold glass.
🏠 自己试试 Try it yourself
把冰水倒进一个干的玻璃杯，等 5 分钟，用纸巾摸摸杯子外面。水珠是从哪里来的？
Pour iced water into a dry glass, wait 5 minutes and touch the outside with a tissue. Where did the droplets come from?
🧠 为什么？ · Why?
煮汤时，锅盖底面为什么会有小水珠？
Why do droplets form under a pot lid when cooking soup?
汤里的水蒸发成水蒸气，碰到较冷的锅盖，散热凝聚成水。
Water from the soup evaporates; the vapour touches the cooler lid, loses heat and condenses.
S6 p.175-177
步骤 3 Step 3

云和雨是怎样形成的？ How do clouds and rain form?

阳光使海洋、河流和湖的水蒸发成水蒸气，人类、动植物也会释放水蒸气。水蒸气升到高空，那里温度较低，便凝聚成小水滴，聚在一起形成云。小水滴不断碰撞聚合，越来越重，就落下成雨——体积约增大 100 多万倍！
The Sun makes water in seas, rivers and lakes evaporate; people, animals and plants also give off vapour. High up it is colder, so the vapour condenses into tiny droplets that gather as clouds. Droplets keep merging and get heavier until they fall as rain – over a million times bigger!
🔁 打个比方 Think of it like this
天空像一个巨大的“锅盖”：下面的水蒸气上升，碰到冷的高空，就变成水滴落回来。
The sky is like a giant pot lid: vapour rises, meets the cold up high, and drips back down.
❓ 想一想 · Check
云是由什么组成的？
What are clouds made of?
水蒸气在高空凝聚成小水滴，聚在一起就是云。
Vapour condenses into droplets high up, which gather as clouds.
S6 p.178-182
步骤 4 Step 4

不停的水循环 The never-ending water cycle

雨水流进江河湖海，也渗入地底，然后又再蒸发——水在地面和天空之间不断循环，这就是水循环。山上温度较低，水蒸气更容易凝聚，所以山上的云和雨较多。我们要节约用水，保持水源清洁。
Rain flows into rivers and seas or soaks into the ground, then evaporates again – water keeps cycling between ground and sky: the water cycle. Mountains are colder, so vapour condenses more easily and there is more cloud and rain. We should save water and keep water sources clean.
🧠 为什么？ · Why?
为什么高山上常常有云和雨？
Why do high mountains often have cloud and rain?
山上的温度较低，水蒸气更容易凝聚成小水滴，形成云和雨。
It is colder up in the mountains, so vapour condenses more easily into droplets, forming cloud and rain.
S6 p.180-183
⭐ 大道理 Big idea
受热：融化、蒸发、沸腾；散热：凝固、凝聚——水就这样在天空和地面之间循环。
Gain heat: melt, evaporate, boil; lose heat: freeze, condense – that's how water cycles between sky and ground.
🌏 那又怎样？科学与生活 So what? Science in my life
  • 马来西亚多雨，正是因为太阳把大量海水蒸发，再在高空凝聚成雨。
    Malaysia is rainy because the Sun evaporates lots of sea water, which condenses into rain up high.
  • 晾衣服、吹干头发、煮饭冒白气，都是形态变化。
    Drying clothes, blow-drying hair and steam from cooking are all changes of state.
  • 我们喝的自来水来自雨水汇成的河流和水坝——节约用水就是保护水循环。
    Our tap water comes from rivers and dams fed by rain – saving water protects the cycle.
  • 知道气体可以被压缩，就明白脚车轮胎为什么能打进很多空气。
    Knowing gases can be compressed explains how a bicycle tyre holds so much air.

🧱8.1 物质的形态8.1 States of MatterS6 p.160-170, p.184

  • 世界万物几乎都是由物质组成的。物质有质量，也会占据空间。
    Almost everything in the world is made of matter. Matter has mass and occupies space.
  • 物质的形态有三种：固体、液体和气体。
    Matter exists in three states: solid, liquid and gas.
  • 固体例子：土壤、文具盒；液体例子：河水、咖啡；气体例子：氧气、二氧化碳。
    Solids: soil, pencil case. Liquids: river water, coffee. Gases: oxygen, carbon dioxide.
  • 彩虹是光的折射和反射形成的自然现象，不是物质。
    A rainbow is a natural effect of light bending and reflecting – it is not matter.
  • 质量：用衣架天平比较，物质有质量。装了空气的气球也有质量。
    Mass: using a hanger balance shows that matter has mass. A balloon filled with air also has mass.
  • 体积：物质所占据的空间。可以用量筒测量液体的体积。
    Volume: the space occupied by matter. A measuring cylinder measures the volume of a liquid.
  • 固体：有固定的形状和体积，会占据空间，也有质量。
    Solid: has a fixed shape and volume, occupies space and has mass.
  • 液体：会流动，没有固定的形状，但有固定的体积，会占据空间，也有质量。
    Liquid: flows, has no fixed shape but has a fixed volume, occupies space and has mass.
  • 气体：没有固定的形状和体积，但会占据空间，也有质量。
    Gas: has no fixed shape or volume, but occupies space and has mass.
  • 气体中的粒子之间距离很大，所以气体可以被压缩（例：注射器里的空气）。
    The particles in a gas are far apart, so a gas can be compressed (e.g. air in a syringe).
  • 所有的物质都是由粒子组成的。不同形态的物质，粒子的排列方式不同。
    All matter is made of particles. The particles are arranged differently in each state.
  • 固体的粒子紧密地排列在一起，只会在固定的位置上振动（像排队的学生）。
    In a solid, particles are packed closely together and only vibrate in fixed positions (like students standing in lines).
  • 液体的粒子之间有距离，可以振动和短距离移动（像到处走动的学生）。
    In a liquid, particles have gaps between them and can vibrate and move short distances (like students walking about).
  • 气体的粒子之间有很大的距离，可以振动和移动得很快（像踢足球的学生）。
    In a gas, particles are far apart and vibrate and move very fast (like students playing football).
  • 水有三种形态：水蒸气（气体）、水（液体）和冰块（固体）。
    Water has three states: water vapour (gas), water (liquid) and ice (solid).
  • 图中例子（师注）：鸭舌帽是固体，矿泉水是液体，篮球里的空气是气体；衣服、裤子和鞋子也是固体。 📗 课本 S6 p.166
    Examples in the picture (teacher's note): the cap is a solid, the mineral water is a liquid, the air in the basketball is a gas; clothes, trousers and shoes are also solids.
  • 活动 3 模拟粒子：每组 12 名同学，三组分别站在老师画的三个不同大小的格子里，不踏出格子走动，比较走动的情况，判断各组模拟的是固体、液体还是气体的粒子（只能在原位振动 → 固体；可短距离移动 → 液体；移动得最自由 → 气体）。 📗 课本 S6 p.168
    Activity 3 – modelling particles: in each group of 12, students stand in three differently sized squares drawn by the teacher and walk about without stepping out; compare how they move and decide which state each group models (vibrating in place → solid; short-distance moving → liquid; moving most freely → gas).
  • 活动 4 水的形态：轮流抽图卡，说出卡上的水是以什么形态存在，最后概括水有三种形态：固体（冰块）、液体（水）、气体（水蒸气）。 📗 课本 S6 p.169
    Activity 4 – forms of water: take turns drawing a picture card and say which state the water on it is in; conclude that water has three states: solid (ice), liquid (water) and gas (water vapour).

💧8.2 水的形态变化和水循环8.2 Changes in the State of Water and the Water CycleS6 p.171-186

  • 融化：固体变成液体，需要受热（冰块融化成水）。
    Melting: solid becomes liquid; it gains heat (ice melts into water).
  • 凝固：液体变成固体，需要散热（水结成冰块）。
    Freezing: liquid becomes solid; it loses heat (water turns into ice).
  • 蒸发：液体变成气体，需要受热（水吸收热量后蒸发成水蒸气）。
    Evaporation: liquid becomes gas; it gains heat (water absorbs heat and becomes water vapour).
  • 沸腾：水被加热到沸点（100°C）时转化成水蒸气。
    Boiling: when water is heated to its boiling point (100°C) it turns into water vapour.
  • 凝聚：气体变成液体，需要散热（水蒸气冷却变成水）。
    Condensation: gas becomes liquid; it loses heat (water vapour cools into water).
  • 生活例子：
    Everyday examples:
  • 融化的冰块：固体→液体（受热）。
    Melting ice: solid → liquid (gain heat).
  • 晾干衣服：液体→气体（受热，蒸发）。
    Clothes drying: liquid → gas (gain heat, evaporation).
  • 凝固的冰棍：液体→固体（散热）。
    Freezing an ice lolly: liquid → solid (lose heat).
  • 盖子底面的小水珠：气体→液体（散热，凝聚）。
    Droplets on the underside of a lid: gas → liquid (lose heat, condensation).
  • 吹干双手、沸腾的水：液体→气体（受热）。
    Blowing hands dry, boiling water: liquid → gas (gain heat).
  • 冷饮杯子外壁的小水珠：空气中的水蒸气遇冷凝聚成水滴，不是杯子漏水。
    Droplets on the outside of a cold drink glass: water vapour in the air cools and condenses into droplets – the glass is not leaking.
  • 云和雨的形成：阳光使海洋、河流和湖里的水蒸发成水蒸气，升到空中；
    How clouds and rain form: sunlight makes water from seas, rivers and lakes evaporate into water vapour that rises into the air;
  • 空中温度较低，水蒸气凝聚成小水滴，小水滴聚集在一起形成云；
    the air high up is cooler, so the vapour condenses into tiny droplets, which gather to form clouds;
  • 小水滴越聚越多、越来越重，无法漂浮在空中时就会降落，形成雨。
    the droplets keep gathering and get heavier until they cannot float, and fall as rain.
  • 人类、动物和植物也会释放水蒸气。
    People, animals and plants also release water vapour.
  • 水在地球表面和天空之间不断循环，这个过程叫水循环。
    Water keeps moving between the Earth's surface and the sky – this is the water cycle.
  • 雨水降落后流入江、河、湖和海，也会渗入地底，然后又开始新一轮的循环。
    Rain flows into rivers, lakes and seas or seeps underground, and a new cycle begins.
  • 山上的温度较低，水蒸气更容易凝聚，所以山上的云和雨比较密集。
    It is cooler on mountains, so more vapour condenses and there are more clouds and rain there.
  • 总结：云和雨的形成与水的蒸发和凝聚有关。
    Summary: the formation of clouds and rain involves evaporation and condensation.
  • 活动 5 水的神奇变身术（五个过程）：水放进冰箱冷冻格 2 小时（凝固）；冰块放户外 5 分钟（融化）；把水加热到 100°C（沸腾）；热水倒入烧杯并盖上铁盖，5 分钟后盖底出现水珠（凝聚）；150 mℓ 温水放户外 2 小时，水位下降（蒸发）。 📗 课本 S6 p.172-173
    Activity 5 – the magical changes of water (five processes): water in the freezer for 2 hours (freezing); ice outdoors for 5 minutes (melting); water heated to 100°C (boiling); hot water in a beaker with a metal lid – after 5 minutes droplets form under the lid (condensation); 150 mℓ warm water outdoors for 2 hours – the level drops (evaporation).
  • 活动 6 物质受热和散热后的变化：把 150 mℓ 水加热至沸腾；橙汁倒入杯子，10 分钟后杯子外壁出现小水珠（凝聚）；有颜色的水放进冰箱冷冻（凝固）；有颜色的冰块放在太阳底下（融化）。 📗 课本 S6 p.175
    Activity 6 – changes when matter gains or loses heat: heat 150 mℓ of water until it boils; pour orange juice into a cup – after 10 minutes droplets appear on the outside (condensation); coloured water in the freezer (freezing); coloured ice in the sun (melting).
  • 活动 8 自制云和雨：玻璃杯倒入热水，让点燃的线香的烟雾停留在杯里 5 秒后取出，把装冰块的培养皿放在杯口上，观察杯里和培养皿底部的变化——用来模拟水蒸气遇冷凝聚成水滴的云和雨的形成。 📗 课本 S6 p.180
    Activity 8 – make your own cloud and rain: pour hot water into a glass, let smoke from a lit incense stick stay inside for 5 seconds, then put a dish of ice on the glass mouth and observe the inside of the glass and the underside of the dish – it models how clouds and rain form as water vapour cools and condenses.
  • 活动 9 水循环模型（制作立体的水循环模型，贴上水的形态变化标签，用它解说云和雨的形成）和活动 10 小小花园盒：铝箔容器装水，与石头、泥土、绿色植物放进透明盒子，盖上盖子放在太阳下，一个小时后观察。石头代表山，泥土和绿色植物代表草地，水代表池塘。 📗 课本 S6 p.182-183
    Activity 9 – water-cycle model (make a 3-D model, label the changes of state, and use it to explain how clouds and rain form) and Activity 10 – mini garden box: put an aluminium-foil container of water, stones, soil and green plants in a clear box, close it and leave it in the sun; observe after one hour. Stones stand for mountains, soil and plants for grassland, water for a pond.
  • 科学百宝箱：空中的水滴一开始非常小，在降雨过程中不断碰撞聚合，最后落下来的雨水体积约增大了 100 多万倍。 📗 课本 S6 p.182
    Science treasure box: water droplets in the air start out very tiny; as rain forms they keep colliding and merging, so the raindrops that finally fall have grown in volume by more than a million times ("100-odd ten-thousand" times).
  • 环保小尖兵：水是日常生活中最重要的物质之一，要养成节省用水的习惯，不能浪费水，也必须保持水源的清洁。 📗 课本 S6 p.182
    Eco-warrior: water is one of the most important substances in daily life; form the habit of saving water, never waste it, and keep water sources clean.
  • 水蒸气凝聚成水时粒子的变化（答题小能手）：气体中的粒子距离很大；散热后粒子互相靠近，变成距离较近、可以短距离移动的液体粒子。 📗 课本 S6 p.183
    Particles when water vapour condenses (quick check): gas particles are far apart; after losing heat they move closer together and become liquid particles that are nearer and can move over short distances.

⚠️ 常见错误 Common mistakes
  1. 概念混淆 Concept mix-up
    气体也有质量，也会占据空间（装了空气的气球比较重）。
    Gases also have mass and take up space (an inflated balloon is heavier).
  2. 概念混淆 Concept mix-up
    粒子：固体紧密、只在原位振动；液体有距离、能短距离移动；气体距离很大、移动得很快。
    Particles: solid — packed tight, vibrate in place; liquid — spaced, move short distances; gas — far apart, move fast.
  3. 用词陷阱 Wording trap
    融化（冰变水，受热）≠ 溶解（糖在水中）。
    Melting (ice to water, heating) ≠ dissolving (sugar in water).
  4. 用词陷阱 Wording trap
    凝聚：水蒸气变水；凝固：水变冰——不要混淆。
    Condensation: steam to water; freezing: water to ice — don't mix them up.
  5. 概念混淆 Concept mix-up
    蒸发在一般温度下就会发生；沸腾要达到沸点 100°C。
    Evaporation happens at ordinary temperatures; boiling needs 100°C.
  6. 概念混淆 Concept mix-up
    冷饮杯外的水珠来自空气中的水蒸气，不是杯子漏水。
    Droplets outside a cold glass come from water vapour in the air, not a leak.
  7. 用词陷阱 Wording trap
    ❌ 说水壶口的“白气”就是水蒸气。✅ 水蒸气是看不见的；“白气”是水蒸气遇冷凝聚成的小水滴。
    ❌ Saying the white "steam" at a kettle spout is water vapour. ✅ Water vapour is invisible; the white mist is tiny droplets of condensed vapour.
  8. 概念混淆 Concept mix-up
    ❌ 冰融化成水后变成了另一种物质。✅ 只是形态改变了，冰、水、水蒸气都是水。
    ❌ Ice becomes a different substance when it melts. ✅ Only its state changes – ice, water and vapour are all water.
  9. 概念混淆 Concept mix-up
    ❌ 云是水蒸气。✅ 云是由许多小水滴聚集而成的。
    ❌ Clouds are water vapour. ✅ Clouds are made of many tiny water droplets.
  10. 概念混淆 Concept mix-up
    ❌ 液体没有固定的体积。✅ 液体没有固定的形状，但有固定的体积。
    ❌ Liquids have no fixed volume. ✅ Liquids have no fixed shape but a fixed volume.
  11. 看图/读表错误 Diagram/table reading
    ❌ 看形态变化图时把箭头方向弄反。✅ 先看箭头从哪里到哪里：固体 → 液体是融化（受热），液体 → 固体是凝固（散热）。
    ❌ Reading change-of-state arrows backwards. ✅ Check start and end: solid → liquid is melting (gain heat); liquid → solid is freezing (lose heat).
  12. 看图/读表错误 Diagram/table reading
    ❌ 把粒子挤在一起的图说成气体。✅ 气体的粒子相距很远、四处散开；挤在一起整齐排列的是固体。
    ❌ Calling a tightly packed particle picture a gas. ✅ Gas particles are far apart and spread out; tightly packed, orderly particles are a solid.
  13. 答题错误 Exam-answer error
    ❌ 证明空气有质量只写“气球变重”。✅ 写出观察和意义：“装了空气的气球那一边下沉，表示空气有质量。”
    ❌ Writing just "the balloon got heavier". ✅ "The side with the air-filled balloon went down, showing air has mass."
  14. 答题错误 Exam-answer error
    ❌ 解释云和雨只写“水上天，又下来”。✅ 写出过程：蒸发 → 水蒸气上升 → 遇冷凝聚成小水滴 → 形成云 → 小水滴聚集变重 → 下雨。
    ❌ "Water goes up and comes down." ✅ Give the steps: evaporate → vapour rises → cools and condenses → cloud → droplets merge and get heavy → rain.

推断 Inference 示范 Example 1
把冰块放进玻璃杯，几分钟后杯子外壁出现小水珠。
Ice is put in a glass; after a few minutes droplets appear outside the glass.
❓
作出一个推断。
Make an inference.
[1 分 marks]
❌ 弱答案 Weak answer · 0/1
杯子漏水。
The glass is leaking.
✅ 满分答案 Full-mark answer · 1/1
空气中的水蒸气碰到冷的杯壁，散热后凝聚成小水珠。
Water vapour in the air touches the cold glass, loses heat and condenses into droplets.
💬 评分理由 Mark by mark
  • 杯子没有漏水，这个推断不合理。
    The glass does not leak, so this inference is not reasonable.
  • 关键词：水蒸气、散热、凝聚。
    Key words: water vapour, lose heat, condense.
🧩 答题句型 Answer frame
句型：“……可能是因为空气中的水蒸气遇冷凝聚成……。”
Frame: "… probably because water vapour in the air cooled and condensed into …."
结论 Conclusion 示范 Example 2
三件相同的湿衣服分别晾在阳光下、树荫下和房间里，干的时间：1 小时、2 小时、4 小时。
Three identical wet shirts dry in the sun, in the shade and indoors: 1 h, 2 h, 4 h.
❓
作出一个结论。
Write a conclusion.
[1 分 marks]
❌ 弱答案 Weak answer · 0/1
阳光下的衣服最快干。
The shirt in the sun dried fastest.
✅ 满分答案 Full-mark answer · 1/1
温度越高，湿衣服干得越快，因为水蒸发得越快。
The higher the temperature, the faster the wet shirt dries, because water evaporates faster.
💬 评分理由 Mark by mark
  • 弱答案只是复述结果。
    The weak answer just restates the result.
  • 结论要说出变数之间的关系，最好加上原因（蒸发）。
    Give the relationship between variables, ideally with the reason (evaporation).
🧩 答题句型 Answer frame
句型：“衣服晾在……的地方，干得越快/越慢。”或“温度越高，水蒸发得越快。”
Frame: "Clothes in … dry faster/slower" or "The higher the temperature, the faster water evaporates."
作出假设 Hypothesis 示范 Example 3
三件相同的湿毛巾分别晾在阳光下、树荫下和房间里，记录变干的时间。
Three identical wet towels hang in the sun, in the shade and indoors; the drying times are recorded.
❓
作出一个假设。
Make a hypothesis.
[1 分 marks]

阳光下会干。
It dries in the sun.
✅ 满分答案 Full-mark answer · 1/1
周围的温度越高，湿毛巾干得越快。
The higher the surrounding temperature, the faster the towel dries.
💬 评分理由 Mark by mark
  • “会干”三条都会干，没有比较；要说出变数之间的关系。
    All three dry eventually – "will dry" compares nothing; state the relationship.
🧩 答题句型 Answer frame
句型：“……越高，……越快。”
Frame: "The higher …, the faster …."
控制变数 Variables 示范 Example 4
同上：湿毛巾实验。
As above: the wet towels.
❓
写出操纵性变数、反应性变数和固定性变数。
State the manipulated, responding and constant variables.
[3 分 marks]
❌ 弱答案 Weak answer · 1/3
地方；时间；毛巾。
Place; time; towel.
✅ 满分答案 Full-mark answer · 3/3
操纵性：晾毛巾的地方（周围的温度）；反应性：毛巾变干所需的时间；固定性：毛巾的大小和种类、湿的程度（水量）。
Manipulated: where the towel hangs (surrounding temperature); responding: time taken to dry; constant: size and type of towel, how wet it is.
💬 评分理由 Mark by mark
  • 第 1 分：“地方”可以，写清楚“晾的地方/温度”更好。
    Mark 1: "place" is OK; "place where it hangs / temperature" is better.
  • 第 2 分：“时间”要写“变干所需的时间”。
    Mark 2: "time" must be "time taken to dry".
  • 第 3 分：“毛巾”要写“毛巾的大小/种类”。
    Mark 3: "towel" → "size/type of towel".

操作性定义 Operational definition 示范 Example 7
烧杯里的 150 mℓ 温水放在户外 2 小时。

❓

❌ 弱答案 Weak answer · 0/1
蒸发是水变成气体。

    Mark 1: where the vapour comes from (hot water evaporating).

    Mark 2: it cools and condenses into droplets.
