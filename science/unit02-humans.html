🦴第 1 课 · 2.1 人体的骨骼系统Lesson 1 · 2.1 The Human Skeletal System

🤔 你有没有想过…… Have you ever wondered…
如果人体没有骨头，会变成什么样子？
What would you look like if you had no bones?
会像一个软软的水袋，站不起来，脑和心脏也没有保护。骨骼支撑身体、保护器官，还决定我们的体型。
You'd be like a floppy water bag – unable to stand, with your brain and heart unprotected. The skeleton supports, protects and gives us our shape.
步骤 1 Step 1

骨头的工作 What bones do

头骨像头盔，保护脑，也形成脸部支架。肋骨像坚固的笼子，保护心脏和肺。脊柱在身体正中央，支撑头部和身体，并保护脊髓。手部骨骼让我们抓、握、举；足部骨骼支撑全身重量，让我们行走、跳跃。
The skull is a helmet protecting the brain and framing the face. The ribs are a strong cage protecting the heart and lungs. The backbone runs down the middle, holding up the head and body and protecting the spinal cord. Hand bones let us grip and lift; foot bones carry our weight and let us walk and jump.
❓ 想一想 · Check
哪一个部分保护心脏和肺？
Which part protects the heart and lungs?
肋骨像笼子包住心脏和肺。
The rib cage surrounds the heart and lungs.
S1 p.10-12
步骤 2 Step 2

206 块骨头 206 bones

成人有 206 块骨头。初生婴儿约有 300 块，长大时有些骨头会合并。我们长大时骨头也会变长，所以会长高。骨头虽然坚硬，受到猛烈撞击还是会骨折。
Adults have 206 bones. A newborn has about 300; some join together as we grow. Bones also get longer, which is why we grow taller. Hard as they are, a heavy knock can still break them.
🧠 为什么？ · Why?
婴儿的骨头比大人多，为什么大人反而只有 206 块？
Babies have more bones than adults – why do adults have only 206?
婴儿的一些骨头在长大时会合并成一块。
Some of a baby's bones join together as it grows.
S6 p.12-19
步骤 3 Step 3

关节：骨头的“铰链” Joints: the body's hinges

关节是骨头和骨头连结的地方，让我们活动自如。关节处有光滑的软骨减少摩擦，韧带把骨头牢牢连接。肘关节只能让手臂伸直或弯曲，不能转圈。
A joint is where bones meet, letting us move. Smooth cartilage reduces friction and ligaments hold the bones together. The elbow only lets the arm straighten or bend – it can't turn in a circle.
🔁 打个比方 Think of it like this
像门的铰链：门板是骨头，铰链是关节——没有铰链，门就打不开。
Like a door hinge: the door is the bone, the hinge is the joint – no hinge, no opening.
🚫 别误会！ Don't be fooled!
❌ 关节是一块特别的骨头。A joint is a special bone.
✅ 关节是骨头和骨头连结的地方，不是骨头。A joint is the place where bones meet, not a bone.
🏠 自己试试 Try it yourself
用硬纸卡包住手肘，试试用手拿起水杯喝水。有什么困难？
Wrap card round your elbow and try to drink from a cup. What's difficult?
❓ 想一想 · Check
竹签和胶纸的模型中，胶纸代表什么？
In the bamboo-stick-and-tape model, what does the tape stand for?
竹签 = 骨头，胶纸 = 关节。
Stick = bone, tape = joint.
🧠 为什么？ · Why?
为什么用纸卡包住手肘后，手臂不能弯曲？
Why can't the arm bend once the elbow is wrapped in card?
纸卡让肘关节不能活动；没有关节的活动，骨头不能弯曲。
The card stops the elbow joint moving; without the joint, the bones can't bend.
S6 p.12-19
步骤 4 Step 4

保护骨骼 Looking after the skeleton

骑脚车、玩滑板时要戴头盔、护膝等防护装备；坐车要系安全带。这些都能保护骨头和关节，避免受伤。
Wear a helmet and knee pads for cycling or skating, and a seat belt in the car – they protect bones and joints from injury.
❓ 想一想 · Check
建筑工人戴安全帽，主要保护哪个系统？
A construction worker's hard hat mainly protects which system?
安全帽保护头骨，避免被硬物撞击。
The hard hat protects the skull from falling objects.
S1 p.15
⭐ 大道理 Big idea
骨骼让我们站立、活动，并像盔甲一样保护重要器官。
The skeleton lets us stand and move, and guards our vital organs like armour.

❤️第 2 课 · 2.2 人体的血液循环系统Lesson 2 · 2.2 The Human Circulatory System

🤔 你有没有想过…… Have you ever wondered…
跑完步后，为什么心“砰砰”跳得特别快？
After running, why does your heart pound so fast?
肌肉工作需要更多氧气和能量，心脏就跳得更快，把含氧的血液更快地送到全身。
Working muscles need more oxygen and energy, so the heart beats faster to send oxygen-rich blood round the body more quickly.
步骤 1 Step 1

循环系统的三个部分 Three parts of the circulatory system

血液循环系统 = 心脏（推送血液）+ 血管（输送血液）+ 血液（把氧气和营养素送到全身，把二氧化碳和废物带走）。成年人体内约有 5 公升血液。
The circulatory system = heart (pumps blood) + blood vessels (carry blood) + blood (delivers oxygen and nutrients, takes away carbon dioxide and waste). An adult has about 5 litres of blood.
🔁 打个比方 Think of it like this
像交通系统：心脏是车站，血管是道路，血液是送货的车——交通一停，货物送不到，城市就瘫痪。
Like a transport system: the heart is the depot, vessels are the roads, blood is the delivery van – if traffic stops, nothing gets delivered.
🚫 别误会！ Don't be fooled!
❌ 血管把血液推送到全身。Blood vessels pump blood round the body.
✅ 心脏推送血液，血管输送血液。The heart pumps; the vessels carry.
❓ 想一想 · Check
营养素是从哪个系统来，再由血液送到全身？
Nutrients come from which system before the blood carries them round the body?
营养素由消化系统吸收。
Nutrients are absorbed by the digestive system.
S1 p.13-14
步骤 2 Step 2

血液走的路线 The route blood takes

人体各部分 → 心脏 → 肺 → 心脏 → 人体各部分。从身体回来的血液含较多二氧化碳，被送到肺；在肺里排出二氧化碳、吸收氧气；含丰富氧气的血液回到心脏，再被推送到全身。
Body → heart → lungs → heart → body. Blood returning from the body carries more carbon dioxide and is sent to the lungs; there it gets rid of CO₂ and picks up oxygen; oxygen-rich blood returns to the heart and is pumped to the body.
🎬 动画 Animation：血液循环的途径 The path of blood
① 血液在人体各部分放下氧气和营养素，带走二氧化碳和废物（蓝色）。
① In the body, blood drops off oxygen and nutrients and picks up carbon dioxide and waste (blue).
② 含较多二氧化碳的血液回到心脏。
② Blood high in carbon dioxide returns to the heart.
③ 心脏把它推送到肺：二氧化碳排出，氧气进入血液（变红色）。
③ The heart pumps it to the lungs: carbon dioxide leaves, oxygen enters (turns red).
④ 含丰富氧气的血液回到心脏。
④ Oxygen-rich blood returns to the heart.
⑤ 心脏把含氧气多的血液推送到人体各部分，再重新开始循环。
⑤ The heart pumps oxygen-rich blood to the body, and the cycle starts again.

❓ 想一想 · Check
从肺回到心脏的血液，含较多什么？
Blood returning from the lungs to the heart is rich in…
肺里的氧气进入血液。
Oxygen from the lungs enters the blood.
🧠 为什么？ · Why?
为什么血液从身体回来后，一定要先去肺？
Why must blood from the body go to the lungs first?
它含有较多二氧化碳，要在肺里排出二氧化碳，并重新获得氧气。
It carries more CO₂, which must be removed in the lungs, where it also picks up fresh oxygen.
S6 p.20-29
步骤 3 Step 3

心跳和运动 Heartbeat and exercise

心脏每分钟跳动大约 70 至 75 次，每天约 10 万次以上。运动越剧烈，呼吸和心跳越快（跳绳比走路快）。长期运动能让静息心率下降，表示心脏和肺更强健。
The heart beats about 70–75 times a minute – over 100 000 times a day. The harder you exercise, the faster you breathe and the faster your heart beats (skipping > walking). Regular exercise lowers the resting rate – a sign of a stronger heart and lungs.
🏠 自己试试 Try it yourself
用两根手指轻按手腕，数一数 1 分钟脉搏跳几次。原地跳 1 分钟后再数一次，比较结果。
Press two fingers on your wrist and count your pulse for 1 minute. Jump on the spot for 1 minute and count again. Compare.
❓ 想一想 · Check
哪一个活动后心跳最快？
After which activity is the heart rate highest?
越剧烈的运动，心跳越快。
The more vigorous the activity, the faster the heart.
S1 p.13-14
步骤 4 Step 4

如果循环停了 If circulation stops

血液循环系统不能正常运作，身体就得不到氧气和营养素，也不能把有毒的废物送到肾和皮肤等排泄器官，最终会导致器官衰竭，甚至死亡。
If the circulatory system fails, the body gets no oxygen or nutrients and toxic waste can't reach excretory organs like the kidneys and skin – leading to organ failure and even death.
🧠 为什么？ · Why?
为什么捐血可以拯救生命？
Why can donating blood save lives?
失血的病人缺少血液来输送氧气和营养素；别人捐的血可以补充。
A patient who lost blood can't carry enough oxygen and nutrients; donated blood replaces it.
S6 p.20-29
⭐ 大道理 Big idea
心脏日夜不停地推送血液，把氧气和营养送到每个细胞，再把废物带走。
The heart pumps day and night, delivering oxygen and nutrients to every cell and taking waste away.

🤝第 3 课 · 2.3 人体各系统之间的关系Lesson 3 · 2.3 Relationship Between Body Systems

🤔 你有没有想过…… Have you ever wondered…
骑自行车时，只有轮子在工作吗？
When a bicycle moves, are only the wheels working?
不是！链条、踏板、刹车……每个部件互相合作，自行车才能走。人体的系统也一样。
No! Chain, pedals, brakes… all work together. Our body systems are the same.
步骤 1 Step 1

系统互相合作 Systems work together

循环 + 呼吸：把氧气送到全身，把二氧化碳排出。消化 + 循环：消化系统吸收的营养素由血液送到全身。骨骼 + 循环：骨骼保护心脏和肺；血液给骨骼送来帮助生长的营养素。排泄 + 循环：血液把废物送到排泄系统排出体外。
Circulatory + respiratory: oxygen in, CO₂ out. Digestive + circulatory: nutrients absorbed are carried by blood. Skeletal + circulatory: bones protect the heart and lungs; blood brings nutrients for bone growth. Excretory + circulatory: blood carries waste to be removed.
🔁 打个比方 Think of it like this
人体的系统就像自行车的部件：各有功能，又互相联系，构成完整的个体。
Body systems are like bicycle parts: each has a job, yet they link up to make one whole.
❓ 想一想 · Check
哪两个系统合作把氧气送到全身？
Which two systems work together to get oxygen round the body?
肺吸进氧气，血液把它送到全身。
Lungs take in oxygen; blood carries it.
S1 p.15
步骤 2 Step 2

一个出问题，其他也受影响 When one fails, others suffer

吃饭时被噎着：影响呼吸系统和消化系统。骨折并肿胀：影响骨骼系统和血液循环系统（周围血管受损）。各系统之间有直接或间接的关系。
Choking on food affects the respiratory and digestive systems. A swollen fracture affects the skeletal and circulatory systems (nearby vessels are damaged). Systems are linked directly or indirectly.
🧠 为什么？ · Why?
为什么骨折的地方会肿起来，还会影响血液循环系统？
Why does a fracture swell and affect circulation?
骨折时周围的血管受损，影响血液流动。
The blood vessels around the break are damaged, affecting blood flow.
❓ 想一想 · Check
吃饭时被噎着，影响哪两个系统？
Choking on food affects which two systems?
食物卡住，呼吸和吞咽都受影响。
Stuck food blocks breathing and swallowing.
S1 p.15
⭐ 大道理 Big idea
人体是一个团队：每个系统各有工作，但谁也离不开谁。
The body is a team: each system has a job, but none can manage alone.

🛡️第 4 课 · 2.4 保护人体所有系统Lesson 4 · 2.4 Protecting All Body Systems

🤔 你有没有想过…… Have you ever wondered…
为什么医生常说“少油、少盐、少糖”？
Why do doctors keep saying "less oil, less salt, less sugar"?
太多油、盐、糖可能让血管阻塞，心脏和全身系统都会受影响。
Too much oil, salt and sugar can block blood vessels, affecting the heart and every system.
步骤 1 Step 1

保护所有系统的好习惯 Habits that protect every system

常运动：增强心脏和肺，强健骨骼。远离香烟、多吸新鲜空气、勤洗手：保护呼吸系统。多吃蔬菜、喝足够的水：消化和排泄顺畅。少油少盐少糖：避免血管阻塞。早睡：让各系统休息。做好安全措施（系安全带）：保护骨骼。
Exercise strengthens heart, lungs and bones. Avoid smoke, breathe fresh air, wash hands: protect breathing. Vegetables and water: smooth digestion and excretion. Less oil, salt, sugar: avoid blocked vessels. Sleep early: let systems rest. Safety measures (seat belts): protect bones.
🚫 别误会！ Don't be fooled!
❌ 一个系统生病，只是那个系统的事。If one system is ill, only that system is affected.
✅ 一个系统无法正常运作，其他系统也会受影响。If one system fails, the others are affected too.
🏠 自己试试 Try it yourself
做一张“我的健康一周”表：每天记录运动、喝水的杯数和睡觉时间。
Make a "My healthy week" chart: log exercise, glasses of water and bedtime each day.
❓ 想一想 · Check
多吃蔬菜主要能避免什么？
Eating more vegetables mainly helps prevent…
蔬菜帮助消化系统，避免便秘。
Vegetables help digestion and prevent constipation.
🧠 为什么？ · Why?
在巴士站戴口罩，保护哪个系统？为什么？
Wearing a mask at a bus stop protects which system, and why?
呼吸系统——避免吸入车辆排出的废气。
The respiratory system – it stops you breathing in exhaust fumes.
S6 p.34-37
⭐ 大道理 Big idea
健康的习惯保护的不只是一个系统，而是整个人。
Healthy habits protect not one system but the whole person.

🦴2.1 人体的骨骼系统2.1 The Human Skeletal SystemS1 p.10-12, J-2, 课本 Textbook S6 p.12-19

  • 骨骼系统由许多骨头组成，决定人的体型。
    The skeletal system is made of many bones and determines a person's body shape.
  • 头骨：像一顶头盔，保护脑，并形成脸部支架。
    Skull: like a helmet, it protects the brain and forms the frame of the face.
  • 肋骨：像一个坚固的笼子，保护心脏、肺、肝等内脏。
    Rib cage: like a strong cage, it protects organs such as the heart, lungs and liver.
  • 脊柱：位于身体正中央，支撑头部和身体，让身体挺直。
    Vertebral column (backbone): in the middle of the body, it supports the head and body and keeps the body upright.
  • 手部骨骼：进行各种伸展和抬、举、抓、握等动作。
    Arm and hand bones: allow stretching, lifting, grasping and holding.
  • 足部骨骼：支撑整个人体的重量。
    Leg and foot bones: support the weight of the whole body.
  • 关节是骨头和骨头之间连结的地方，使我们能够活动自如。
    A joint is where one bone joins another, so we can move freely.
  • 身体的关节有：颈椎骨关节、肩关节、肘关节、腕关节、指关节、腰椎骨关节、髋关节、膝关节、踝关节、趾关节。 extra 补充
    Joints in the body: neck (cervical) vertebrae joint, shoulder, elbow, wrist, finger, lower-back (lumbar) vertebrae joint, hip, knee, ankle and toe joints.
  • 肘关节只能让手臂伸直或弯曲，不能转圈。
    The elbow joint only lets the arm straighten or bend; it cannot rotate in a circle.
  • 骑滑板车、玩滑板时要戴护膝等防护装备，保护骨头和关节，避免受伤。
    Wear protective gear such as knee pads when scooting or skateboarding, to protect bones and joints from injury.
  • 人体有 206 块大小和形状不同的骨头。初生婴儿约有 300 块骨头，长大时有些骨头会合并，所以成人只有 206 块。 📗 课本 S6 p.12
    The human body has 206 bones of different sizes and shapes. A newborn baby has about 300 bones; as the body grows some bones fuse together, so an adult has only 206.
  • 脊柱也包含并保护脊髓。足部骨骼除了支撑体重，也让我们能行走、跳跃和蹲下。 📗 课本 S6 p.14-15
    The backbone also contains and protects the spinal cord. Besides supporting body weight, leg and foot bones let us walk, jump and squat.
  • 骨骼虽然坚硬，但受到猛烈的撞击还是会断（骨折）。 📗 课本 S6 p.14
    Although bones are hard, they can still break (fracture) from a strong impact.
  • 关节处骨头的表面覆盖着光滑的软骨，能减少活动时骨头之间的摩擦；韧带把骨头牢固地连接在一起。 📗 课本 S6 p.17
    At a joint, the ends of the bones are covered with smooth cartilage, which reduces friction between bones when we move; ligaments hold the bones firmly together.
  • 模拟关节（活动）：一根竹签不折断就很难弯曲；两根竹签用胶纸连接后就能弯曲。竹签代表骨头，胶纸代表关节。用纸卡包住手肘后，手肘就不能弯曲。 📗 课本 S6 p.17
    Modelling a joint (activity): one skewer cannot bend without breaking, but two skewers joined with tape can bend. The skewers stand for bones and the tape for the joint. With a card wrapped around the elbow, the arm cannot bend.
  • 骨骼系统的五个重要性：① 形成脸部支架；② 支撑身体；③ 决定个人体型；④ 保护体内重要的器官；⑤ 透过关节让身体能灵活地活动。 📗 课本 S6 p.19
    Five reasons the skeletal system is important: ① forms the frame of the face; ② supports the body; ③ decides body shape; ④ protects important organs; ⑤ lets the body move flexibly through joints.
  • 我们长大时，骨头也会变长，所以我们会长高。 📗 课本 S6 p.18
    As we grow, our bones grow longer, so we become taller.

关节是骨头和骨头连结的地方。
A joint is where two bones meet.
💡 你知道吗？ Did you know? extra 补充
成年人大约有 206 块骨头；婴儿的骨头更多，长大后有些会连在一起。
An adult has about 206 bones; a baby has more, and some join together as it grows.

❤️2.2 人体的血液循环系统2.2 The Human Circulatory SystemS1 p.13-14, J-2, J-3, 课本 Textbook S6 p.20-29

  • 血液循环系统由心脏、血管和血液组成。
    The circulatory system is made up of the heart, blood vessels and blood.
  • 心脏：把血液推送到肺和全身。
    Heart: pumps blood to the lungs and the whole body.
  • 血管：输送血液到全身。
    Blood vessels: carry blood around the body.
  • 血液：把氧气和营养素输送到全身各部位，同时把二氧化碳和其他废物带走。
    Blood: carries oxygen and nutrients to all parts of the body and takes away carbon dioxide and other waste.
  • 肺：氧气和二氧化碳在这里进行交换。
    Lungs: where oxygen and carbon dioxide are exchanged.
  • 从身体各部位回到心脏和肺的血液，含有较多的二氧化碳；从心脏推送到全身的血液，含有较多的氧气。
    Blood returning from the body to the heart and lungs has more carbon dioxide; blood pumped from the heart to the body has more oxygen.
  • 营养素来自消化系统，由血液输送到全身。
    Nutrients come from the digestive system and are carried by the blood to the whole body.
  • 正常人的心脏每分钟跳动约 60 至 80 次。
    A normal person's heart beats about 60 to 80 times per minute.
  • 如果血液循环系统不能正常运作，身体得不到足够的氧气和营养素，也不能把废物排出体外，最终会导致器官衰竭，甚至死亡。
    If the circulatory system cannot work properly, the body does not get enough oxygen and nutrients and cannot remove waste, which may lead to organ failure and even death.
  • 运动越剧烈，呼吸率和心跳越快。例：跳绳比走路呼吸更快。 extra 补充
    The more strenuous the activity, the faster the breathing rate and heartbeat. e.g. skipping rope makes you breathe faster than walking.
  • 长期骑自行车等运动，可以让静息心率下降，说明肺和心脏更强健。 extra 补充
    Regular exercise such as cycling can lower the resting heart rate, showing a stronger heart and lungs.
  • 成年人体内约有 5 公升的血液。捐血可以拯救别人的生命。 📗 课本 S6 p.20
    An adult has about 5 litres of blood. Donating blood can save other people's lives.
  • 血液循环的途径（1）：① 血液里的氧气进入人体各部分，同时人体产生的二氧化碳等废物进入血液；② 二氧化碳含量较高的血液回到心脏，被推送到肺；③ 二氧化碳在肺部排出体外，同时肺里的氧气进入血管。 📗 课本 S6 p.24-25
    The path of blood (1): ① oxygen in the blood goes into body parts, while carbon dioxide and other waste from the body go into the blood; ② blood high in carbon dioxide returns to the heart and is pumped to the lungs; ③ in the lungs carbon dioxide leaves the body and oxygen enters the blood vessels.
  • 血液循环的途径（2）：④ 重新获得丰富氧气的血液回到心脏；⑤ 氧气含量较高的血液被心脏推送到人体各部分。简记：人体各部分 → 心脏 → 肺 → 心脏 → 人体各部分。 📗 课本 S6 p.24-25
    The path of blood (2): ④ blood rich in oxygen again returns to the heart; ⑤ the heart pumps this oxygen-rich blood to all body parts. Short form: body → heart → lungs → heart → body.
  • 心脏每分钟跳动大约 70 至 75 次，每天跳动约 10 万次以上。 📗 课本 S6 p.25
    The heart beats about 70 to 75 times a minute, over 100,000 times a day.
  • 血液循环系统的重要性：把氧气和营养素输送到人体各部分，提供能量以维持生命；人体进行生命过程时产生有毒的废物，血液把它们带到排泄器官，如肾和皮肤。 📗 课本 S6 p.28
    Why the circulatory system is important: it carries oxygen and nutrients to every body part to give energy for life; life processes produce toxic waste, which the blood carries to excretory organs such as the kidneys and skin.
  • 血液循环系统就像交通系统：交通受到干扰，人们就难以出行；血液循环系统无法运作，人体就得不到氧气和营养素，最终会导致死亡。 📗 课本 S6 p.29
    The circulatory system is like a traffic system: when traffic is disrupted people cannot travel; when circulation fails the body gets no oxygen or nutrients and will eventually die.
🧪 实验：运动和心跳 Investigation: exercise and heart rate S1 p.13-14, 课本 Textbook S6 p.20-29
  1. 假设 Hypothesising
    运动越剧烈，心跳越快。
    The more vigorous the activity, the faster the heartbeat.
  2. 控制变数 Controlling variables
    操纵：活动的种类；反应：每分钟心跳次数；固定：同一个人、活动时间一样长。
    Manipulated: type of activity; responding: heartbeats per minute; fixed: same person, same time for each activity.
  3. 测量和应用数目 Measuring
    坐着、走路、跳绳各 1 分钟后，数一数脉搏。
    Count the pulse after 1 minute of sitting, walking and skipping.
  4. 沟通 Communicating
    把结果记录在表格里。
    Record the results in a table.
  5. 结论 Conclusion
    跳绳后心跳最快。
    The heartbeat is fastest after skipping.

Oxygen and carbon dioxide are exchanged in the lungs.

🤝2.3 人体各系统之间的关系2.3 Relationship Between Body SystemsS1 p.15, J-3, 课本 Textbook S6 p.30-40

  • 身体的各个系统不是单独工作的，而是互相配合。
    The body systems do not work alone; they work together.
  • 血液循环系统和呼吸系统合作：把氧气输送到身体各部分，同时把二氧化碳排出体外。
    The circulatory system and respiratory system work together: they carry oxygen to all body parts and remove carbon dioxide from the body.
  • 消化系统吸收营养素，通过血液循环带到全身；骨骼系统保护体内的重要器官。
    The digestive system absorbs nutrients, which the circulatory system carries to the whole body; the skeletal system protects important organs inside the body.
  • 吃饭时被噎着：影响呼吸系统和消化系统。
    Choking on food: affects the respiratory and digestive systems.
  • 骨折并肿胀：影响骨骼系统和血液循环系统（周围的血管受损，影响血液流动）。
    A broken bone with swelling: affects the skeletal and circulatory systems (nearby blood vessels are damaged, which affects blood flow).
  • 建筑工人戴安全帽：保护骨骼系统（头骨），避免头部被硬物撞击。
    A construction worker wears a safety helmet: protects the skeletal system (skull) from being hit by hard objects.
  • 在巴士站戴口罩：保护呼吸系统，避免吸入废气。
    Wearing a mask at the bus stop: protects the respiratory system from breathing in exhaust fumes.
  • 人体的系统就像自行车的部件：每个部件都有各自的功能，互相合作，自行车才能移动。各系统既有各自的功能，又互相联系，构成一个完整的个体。 📗 课本 S6 p.30
    Body systems are like the parts of a bicycle: each part has its own job, and they work together so the bicycle can move. Each system has its own function and is linked to the others, making one complete person.
  • 消化系统吸收的营养素由血液循环系统输送到身体各部分，血液循环系统也为消化系统输送氧气。 📗 课本 S6 p.32
    Nutrients absorbed by the digestive system are carried by the circulatory system to all body parts; the circulatory system also brings oxygen to the digestive system.
  • 骨骼系统保护心脏和肺等内脏免受伤害；血液循环系统为骨骼系统输送有助骨骼生长的营养素。 📗 课本 S6 p.33
    The skeletal system protects organs like the heart and lungs from injury; the circulatory system brings the skeletal system nutrients that help bones grow.
  • 人体的排泄物通过血液循环系统送到排泄系统排出体外；血液循环系统也为排泄系统输送氧气和营养素。 📗 课本 S6 p.33
    Body waste is carried by the circulatory system to the excretory system to be removed; the circulatory system also brings oxygen and nutrients to the excretory system.
  • 各系统之间有直接或间接的关系。如果一个系统无法正常运作，其他系统的功能也会受到影响。 📗 课本 S6 p.32, p.35
    The systems are linked directly or indirectly. If one system cannot work properly, the others are affected too.

🛡️2.4 保护人体所有系统2.4 Protecting All Body Systems课本 Textbook S6 p.34-37

  • 我们应好好保护人体所有系统，使生活健康。如果一个系统无法正常运作，其他系统的功能也会受到影响。 📗 课本 S6 p.34-35
    We should protect all body systems to stay healthy. If one system cannot work properly, the other systems are affected too.
  • 常运动：增强心脏功能、促进血液循环，也增强肺功能为身体提供氧气，还能强健骨骼。 📗 课本 S6 p.36
    Exercise often: it strengthens the heart and helps blood circulation, strengthens the lungs to supply oxygen, and makes bones strong.
  • 多吸新鲜空气并远离香烟，保护呼吸系统；以正确的步骤勤洗手，避免 2019 冠状病毒（COVID-19）等病菌通过呼吸系统进入体内。 📗 课本 S6 p.36
    Breathe fresh air and stay away from cigarette smoke to protect the respiratory system; wash hands often the correct way so germs such as COVID-19 do not enter through the respiratory system.
  • 多吃蔬菜，避免便秘（消化系统）；喝足够的水，使排泄系统顺畅。 📗 课本 S6 p.36-37
    Eat plenty of vegetables to avoid constipation (digestive system); drink enough water to keep the excretory system working well.
  • 吃含丰富营养的食物，为各系统提供所需的营养素；饮食应少油、少盐、少糖，以免血管阻塞。 📗 课本 S6 p.37
    Eat nutritious food to give every system the nutrients it needs; eat less oil, salt and sugar to avoid blocked blood vessels.
  • 早睡，让心脏和所有系统得到充分的休息；做好安全措施（例：坐车系安全带），保护骨骼系统。 📗 课本 S6 p.37
    Sleep early so the heart and all systems get enough rest; take safety measures (e.g. wear a seat belt in the car) to protect the skeletal system.

    The rib cage protects the heart and lungs; the skull protects the brain; the backbone supports the body and protects the spinal cord — don't mix them up.
  2. 概念混淆 Concept mix-up
    关节是骨头和骨头连结的地方，不是一块骨头。

  3. 概念混淆 Concept mix-up
    从肺回到心脏的血液含较多氧气；从身体回到心脏的血液含较多二氧化碳。

    心脏推送血液，血管输送血液——不要说血管推送血液。

📌 一定要记住 Must-know facts
  • 头骨保护脑；肋骨保护心脏和肺；脊柱让身体挺直。
    The skull protects the brain; the rib cage protects the heart and lungs; the backbone keeps us upright.
  • 关节是骨头和骨头连结的地方。
    A joint is where two bones meet.
  • 血液循环系统 = 心脏 + 血管 + 血液。
    Circulatory system = heart + blood vessels + blood.
  • 在肺里交换氧气和二氧化碳。
    Oxygen and carbon dioxide are exchanged in the lungs.
  • 身体的系统互相配合，不是单独工作的。
    Body systems work together, not alone.
⚠️ 别犯错 Watch out
  • 肋骨保护心脏和肺；头骨保护脑；脊柱支撑身体并保护脊髓——不要配错。
    The rib cage protects the heart and lungs; the skull protects the brain; the backbone supports the body and protects the spinal cord — don't mix them up.
  • 关节是骨头和骨头连结的地方，不是一块骨头。
    A joint is the place where bones meet, not a bone.
  • 从肺回到心脏的血液含较多氧气；从身体回到心脏的血液含较多二氧化碳。
    Blood returning from the lungs is rich in oxygen; blood returning from the body is rich in carbon dioxide.
