🌵第 1 课 · 4.1 植物的物种生存Lesson 1 · 4.1 Survival of Plant Species

🤔 你有没有想过…… Have you ever wondered…
吃榴梿前，为什么要戴手套或用厚布拿？
Why do people use gloves or a thick cloth to hold a durian?
榴梿外壳有尖刺！这是植物保护自己的方法——植物不能逃跑，只能靠身体的特征自卫。
The durian husk has thorns! It's how the plant protects itself – plants can't run away, so they rely on body features.
步骤 1 Step 1

植物怎样自卫？ How plants defend themselves

尖刺：刺伤靠近的动物（玫瑰、仙人掌、凤梨、榴梿、含羞草）。胶乳：黏黏的液体让皮肤发痒（波罗蜜、木瓜）。细毛：碰到会痒（竹、甘蔗、羊角豆）。毒素：误食会中毒（夹竹桃、海檬果、万年青）。臭味：让害虫避开（莱佛士花、万寿菊）。
Thorns prick animals (rose, cactus, pineapple, durian, mimosa). Latex – sticky sap that itches (jackfruit, papaya). Fine hairs itch (bamboo, sugar cane, okra). Poison if eaten (oleander, sea mango, dumb cane). Bad smell keeps pests away (rafflesia, marigold).
🔁 打个比方 Think of it like this
植物像一座不能搬家的城堡：没法逃，就装上“刺墙”“毒药”和“臭气”来防守。
A plant is a castle that can't move: no escape, so it builds thorn walls, poison and stink defences.
🚫 别误会！ Don't be fooled!
❌ 胶乳和毒素是一样的。Latex and poison are the same.
✅ 胶乳让皮肤发痒；毒素是误食后会中毒。Latex makes skin itch; poison harms you if eaten.
🏠 自己试试 Try it yourself
在家附近找一找有刺、有细毛或有臭味的植物（只看不摸，不放进嘴里！）。
Look near home for plants with thorns, hairs or strong smells (look, don't touch or taste!).
❓ 想一想 · Check
夹竹桃用什么方法保护自己？
How does oleander protect itself?
夹竹桃有毒，误食会中毒。
Oleander is poisonous if eaten.
🧠 为什么？ · Why?
为什么植物要靠身体的特征来自卫，而不像动物一样逃跑？
Why do plants rely on body features instead of running away?
植物不能到处移动以躲避危险。
Plants cannot move to escape danger.
S6 p.84-85
步骤 2 Step 2

沙漠里的仙人掌 The desert cactus

蜡质层锁住水分；粗厚的茎储存水分；针状叶叶面面积小，减少水分流失（也能自卫）；长根伸到土壤深处找水。
A waxy layer locks in water; a thick stem stores water; needle-like leaves have a small surface so less water is lost (and they defend); long roots reach deep for water.
❓ 想一想 · Check
仙人掌的针状叶有什么作用？
What do a cactus's needle-like leaves do?
一个特征两种作用。
One feature, two jobs.
🧠 为什么？ · Why?
为什么仙人掌的根很长？
Why are a cactus's roots so long?
沙漠很少下雨，长根可以伸到土壤深处寻找水源。
Deserts get little rain; long roots reach deep into the soil for water.
S1 p.28-30
步骤 3 Step 3

干旱、寒冷和强风 Drought, cold and strong wind

落叶：橡胶树、面包树在干旱季节落叶，减少水分蒸发。粗厚的树皮：松树抵御严寒。卷曲的叶子、细毛：减少水分流失。强风地区：椰子树羽状叶让风穿过，柔韧的茎随风摆动不易折断（椰子树、竹、稻）。
Shedding leaves: rubber trees drop leaves in the dry season to reduce water loss. Thick bark: pines withstand cold. Curled leaves and fine hairs cut water loss. Windy places: coconut palms have feather-like leaves that let wind through and flexible stems that bend without snapping (coconut, bamboo, paddy).
🚫 别误会！ Don't be fooled!
❌ 橡胶树落叶是因为生病。Rubber trees drop leaves because they're sick.
✅ 落叶是为了在干旱季节减少水分流失。It's to reduce water loss in the dry season.
🏠 自己试试 Try it yourself
拿一张完整的纸和一张剪成很多条的纸，在风扇前比较哪一张被吹得更厉害。
Hold a whole sheet and a sheet cut into strips in front of a fan. Which gets pushed harder?
❓ 想一想 · Check
椰子树能抵抗强风，是因为它有？
Coconut palms withstand strong wind because of…
羽状叶减少风阻，柔韧的茎不易折断。
Feather-like leaves reduce wind resistance; flexible stems don't snap.
S6 p.86-91
⭐ 大道理 Big idea
植物不能移动，所以每片叶、每根刺、每条根都是它的生存工具。
Plants can't move, so every leaf, thorn and root is a survival tool.

🌬️第 2 课 · 4.2 植物的传播方法Lesson 2 · 4.2 Methods of Seed and Fruit Dispersal

🤔 你有没有想过…… Have you ever wondered…
路边的树下为什么很少长满同一种小树苗？
Why isn't the ground under a roadside tree crowded with its own seedlings?
植物会把种子传播到远处。种子落在母树下，要和母树争阳光、水和养料，长不好。
Plants spread their seeds far away. Seeds under the parent would compete with it for light, water and nutrients and grow poorly.
步骤 1 Step 1

为什么要传播种子？ Why disperse seeds?

种子离母树越远，与母树争夺阳光、水和养料的竞争越少，幼苗能长得更好，新的地方也能长出新植物，物种就能生存。
The further seeds go, the less they compete with the parent for light, water and nutrients, so seedlings grow better and the species spreads and survives.
🧠 为什么？ · Why?
如果种子都落在母树下，会怎样？
What happens if all the seeds fall under the parent tree?
幼苗要和母树争夺阳光、水和养料，长得不好，甚至死亡。
The seedlings compete with the parent for light, water and nutrients and grow poorly or die.
S6 p.93-104
步骤 2 Step 2

四种传播方法 Four ways of dispersal

靠风力：种子细小轻盈，有薄翅（龙脑香、青龙木）或伞状细毛（蒲公英）。靠水力：有蜡质外皮、纤维和气室，能浮（椰子、莲蓬、红树）。靠自动爆裂：果实成熟干瘪后裂开，把种子弹出（凤仙花、羊角豆、橡胶）。靠人类或动物：鲜艳香甜的果实被吃掉（木瓜种子随粪便排出；芒果种子太大，被丢到别处），或有钩刺勾在衣服和毛上（梵天花、相思草）。
Wind: small, light seeds with thin wings (keruing, angsana) or parachute hairs (dandelion). Water: waxy skin, fibres and air spaces so they float (coconut, lotus, mangrove). Explosion: dry ripe pods split and fling seeds (balsam, okra, rubber). Humans or animals: bright sweet fruits are eaten (papaya seeds pass out in droppings; mango seeds are too big and get thrown away), or hooks catch on clothes and fur (Caesarweed, love grass).
🔁 打个比方 Think of it like this
种子像要去旅行的人：有的坐“风的飞机”，有的坐“水的船”，有的“弹射出发”，有的搭动物的“顺风车”。
Seeds are travellers: some fly the "wind plane", some take the "water boat", some are catapulted, some hitch a ride on animals.
🎬 动画 Animation：种子的传播方法 How seeds are dispersed
靠风力：种子轻盈、有薄翅或细毛（龙脑香、蒲公英），被风吹到远处。
By wind: light seeds with thin wings or hairs (keruing, dandelion) are blown far away.
靠水力：椰子有不透水的外皮、纤维和气室，浮在水面漂流。
By water: the coconut's waterproof skin, fibres and air spaces let it float away.
靠自动爆裂：果实成熟干瘪后裂开，把种子弹出去（凤仙花、羊角豆、橡胶）。
By explosion: the ripe, dry fruit splits and flings seeds out (balsam, okra, rubber).
靠人类或动物：动物吃了鲜艳香甜的果实，把种子带到远处；有钩刺的种子黏在衣服或毛上。
By humans or animals: animals eat bright, sweet fruit and carry the seeds away; hooked seeds cling to clothes or fur.

🚫 别误会！ Don't be fooled!
❌ 椰子很大很重，所以靠动物传播。The coconut is big and heavy, so animals spread it.

(With an adult) Put a dry coconut husk or lotus pod in a bucket of water. Does it float or sink?

The dry ripe pod splits and flings out the seeds.

步骤 3 Step 3

植物和动物互相帮助 Plants and animals help each other

植物给动物食物和氧气；动物帮植物传播种子，呼出的二氧化碳供植物进行光合作用。如果植物无法传播种子，可能会绝种，生态平衡也会受影响。
Plants give animals food and oxygen; animals spread seeds and breathe out carbon dioxide for photosynthesis. If plants can't disperse, they may become extinct, upsetting the balance of nature.
❓ 想一想 · Check
动物怎样帮助植物？
How do animals help plants?
动物吃果实或带着钩刺种子，把种子带到远处。
Animals eat fruits or carry hooked seeds far away.
S6 p.93-104
⭐ 大道理 Big idea
看种子的结构，就能猜出它怎样旅行：翅膀→风，气室→水，干瘪的荚→爆裂，甜果或钩刺→动物。
A seed's structure reveals how it travels: wings → wind, air spaces → water, dry pods → explosion, sweet fruit or hooks → animals.

🌵 4.1 植物的物种生存🌬️ 4.2 植物的传播方法

🌵4.1 植物的物种生存4.1 Survival of Plant SpeciesS1 p.28-30, J-5, 课本 Textbook S6 p.82-92

  • 植物有不同的方法保护自己，避免被动物吃掉：
    Plants protect themselves in different ways so that animals do not eat them:
  • 尖刺：刺伤靠近的动物。例：玫瑰、仙人掌。
    Thorns: prick animals that come near. e.g. rose, cactus.
  • 胶乳：黏黏的液体，会让皮肤发痒。例：菠萝蜜、木瓜、羊角豆。
    Latex: a sticky liquid that can make skin itch. e.g. jackfruit, papaya, okra.
  • 细毛：碰到会让皮肤发痒。例：甘蔗、南瓜。
    Fine hairs: make the skin itch when touched. e.g. sugarcane, pumpkin.
  • 毒素：误食会中毒，甚至死亡。例：软枝黄蝉、海檬果、夹竹桃。
    Toxins (poison): eating them can poison or even kill. e.g. yellow allamanda, sea mango (pong-pong), oleander.
  • 臭味：气味强烈，某些害虫避而远之。例：莱佛士花、九层塔。
    Foul or strong smell: the strong smell keeps some pests away. e.g. Rafflesia, basil.
  • 含羞草的刺会刺伤靠近的动物。
    The touch-me-not plant has thorns that prick animals that come near.
  • 适应恶劣气候（例：沙漠里的仙人掌）：
    Adapting to harsh climates (e.g. the cactus in the desert):
  • 蜡质层：锁住水分，避免水分流失。
    Waxy layer: locks in water so water is not lost.
  • 粗厚的茎：储存水分。
    Thick stem: stores water.
  • 针状叶：叶面面积小，减少水分流失。
    Needle-like leaves: small surface area, so less water is lost.
  • 长根：伸到土壤深处寻找水源。
    Long roots: reach deep into the soil to find water.
  • 橡胶树在干旱的季节会落叶，减少水分流失。
    Rubber trees shed their leaves in the dry season to reduce water loss.
  • 抵抗强风（例：椰子树）：羽状的叶子让风穿过，坚韧有弹性的茎不容易折断。
    Resisting strong wind (e.g. coconut palm): feather-like leaves let wind pass through, and a tough, flexible trunk does not break easily.
  • 课本例子（自卫）：尖刺——玫瑰、仙人掌、凤梨、榴梿、含羞草；胶乳——波罗蜜、木瓜、芋头；细毛——竹、甘蔗、茅草、羊角豆；毒素——海檬果、夹竹桃、万年青；臭味——莱佛士花、烟草叶、万寿菊、香叶天竺葵。 📗 课本 S6 p.84-85
    Textbook examples (defence): thorns — rose, cactus, pineapple, durian, touch-me-not (mimosa); latex — jackfruit, papaya, yam; fine hairs — bamboo, sugar cane, lalang, okra; poison — sea mango, oleander, dumb cane; bad smell — rafflesia, tobacco leaf, marigold, scented geranium.
  • 植物不能到处移动以躲避危险，只能靠本身特有的特征保护自己。 📗 课本 S6 p.84
    Plants cannot move around to escape danger, so they protect themselves with their own special features.
  • 落叶：干旱的季节和寒冷的冬季水分较少，有些植物（如面包树、橡胶树、枫树）会在固定季节落叶，减少水分从叶子蒸发。粗厚的树皮：寒冷地区的植物（如松树）有粗厚的树皮，能抵御严寒。 📗 课本 S6 p.86-87
    Shedding leaves: in dry seasons and cold winters water is scarce, so some plants (e.g. baobab, rubber tree, maple) drop their leaves at a fixed season to reduce water loss. Thick bark: plants in cold places (e.g. pine) have thick bark to withstand the cold.
  • 仙人掌以外的例子：针状叶——杉树、松树；长根——骆驼刺、芦荟；蜡质茎——红烛花、甘蔗；储水的茎——多肉植物、香蕉。 📗 课本 S6 p.88-89
    Examples besides the cactus: needle-like leaves — fir and pine; long roots — camelthorn and aloe vera; waxy stems — anthurium and sugar cane; water-storing stems — succulents and banana plants.
  • 卷曲的叶子：减少曝露在阳光下的面积，减少水分流失（例：热带雨林的蕨类）。细毛：叶子和茎上的细毛能避免阳光直接曝晒，减缓水分蒸发（例：番茄、南瓜）。 📗 课本 S6 p.90
    Curled leaves: less leaf area faces the sun, so less water is lost (e.g. rainforest ferns). Fine hairs: hairs on leaves and stems block direct sunlight and slow water loss (e.g. tomato, pumpkin).
  • 强风地区：羽状叶减少风的阻力（椰子树、蕨）；柔韧的茎有弹性，随风摆动不易折断（椰子树、竹、稻）；扎实、出土的根能抵抗潮水和强风。 📗 课本 S6 p.91
    Windy places: feather-like leaves reduce wind resistance (coconut tree, fern); flexible stems sway with the wind without snapping (coconut tree, bamboo, paddy); firm roots above the ground resist tides and strong wind.

🌬️4.2 植物的传播方法4.2 Methods of Seed and Fruit DispersalS1 p.31-32, J-6, 课本 Textbook S6 p.93-104

  • 植物传播种子或果实，让新的植物能生长，物种才能生存。
    Plants disperse seeds or fruits so new plants can grow and the species survives.
  • 靠风力：种子细小轻盈，有薄翅或伞状结构。例：蒲公英。
    By wind: seeds are small and light, with thin wings or umbrella-like parts. e.g. dandelion.
  • 靠水力：有蜡质外皮，壳内有纤维和气室，能浮在水面。例：椰子、莲蓬。
    By water: have a waxy outer skin, with fibre and air spaces inside the husk so they float. e.g. coconut, lotus seed pod.
  • 靠自动爆裂：果实成熟后变得干瘪，裂开时把种子弹出去。例：橡胶树。
    By self-explosion (bursting): the fruit dries out when ripe and splits open, flinging the seeds out. e.g. rubber tree.
  • 靠动物：果实鲜艳、果肉香甜，被动物吃掉后，种子随粪便排出。例：芒果、番石榴。
    By animals: bright, sweet-smelling fleshy fruit is eaten, and the seeds are passed out in droppings. e.g. mango, guava.
  • 靠人类或动物：种子或果实长有钩刺，勾在动物的毛或人的衣服上。例：梵天花。
    By humans or animals: seeds or fruits with hooks cling to animal fur or clothes. e.g. Urena (hooked burr).
  • 种子离母树越远，与母树抢阳光、水和养料的竞争越少，幼苗能长得更好。
    The farther seeds land from the mother plant, the less they compete with it for sunlight, water and nutrients, so the seedlings grow better.
  • 动物和植物互相帮助：植物给动物食物，动物帮植物传播种子。
    Animals and plants help each other: plants provide food for animals, and animals help plants disperse seeds.
  • 植物利用动物呼出的二氧化碳进行光合作用，释放动物需要的氧气。两者的生存，为了维持生态平衡，避免物种绝灭。
    Plants use the carbon dioxide animals breathe out for photosynthesis and release oxygen that animals need. Both help to keep the ecological balance and avoid species becoming extinct.
  • 课本把传播方法分成 4 种：靠风力、靠水力、靠自动爆裂、靠人类或动物（被吃掉或用钩刺依附）。 📗 课本 S6 p.102
    The textbook groups dispersal into 4 methods: by wind, by water, by explosion, and by humans or animals (eaten, or carried on hooks).
  • 靠风力的例子：龙脑香、青龙木、茅草、蒲公英。龙脑香和青龙木的种子有薄翅，干瘪后变得更轻；蒲公英的种子细小、有细毛、呈伞状。 📗 课本 S6 p.96
    Wind examples: keruing (dipterocarp), angsana, lalang, dandelion. Keruing and angsana seeds have thin wings and get lighter when dry; dandelion seeds are tiny, hairy and umbrella-shaped.
  • 靠水力的例子：莲、红树、椰子、水椰（亚答树）。长在河边、海边或沼泽的植物，果实通常能浮在水面。莲蓬轻盈，内有气囊和纤维。 📗 课本 S6 p.97
    Water examples: lotus, mangrove, coconut, nipah palm. Plants by rivers, the sea or swamps usually have fruits that float. The lotus pod is light, with air sacs and fibres.
  • 靠自动爆裂的例子：凤仙花、羊角豆、相思豆、橡胶、凤凰木、臭豆。果实成熟后干瘪、裂开，把种子弹到远处。 📗 课本 S6 p.98
    Explosion examples: balsam, okra, saga (rosary pea), rubber, flame of the forest, petai. The ripe fruit dries, splits open and flings the seeds far away.
  • 靠人类或动物：果实颜色鲜艳、果肉香甜；木瓜种子多、细小坚硬、不能消化，随粪便排出；芒果种子太大不能吞，被丢到别处；相思草、梵天花、含羞草的种子有钩刺，依附在衣服或毛发上。 📗 课本 S6 p.99
    Humans or animals: fruits are brightly coloured and sweet; papaya seeds are many, small, hard and undigested, passing out in droppings; mango seeds are too big to swallow and get thrown elsewhere; seeds of love grass, Caesarweed (梵天花) and mimosa have hooks that cling to clothes or fur.
  • 每种植物的存在都对自然环境有贡献，例如为生物提供食物来源、吸收二氧化碳并释放氧气。如果植物无法传播种子，它们可能会绝种，生态平衡也会受到影响。 📗 课本 S6 p.101
    Every plant contributes to nature, e.g. it is a food source for living things and takes in carbon dioxide and releases oxygen. If plants cannot disperse seeds they may die out, upsetting the balance of nature.

⚠️ 常见错误 Common mistakes
  1. 概念混淆 Concept mix-up
    椰子靠水力传播（不是风力）：它有不透水的蜡质外皮，壳内有纤维和气室。
    The coconut is dispersed by water (not wind): waterproof waxy skin, fibres and air spaces inside.
  2. 概念混淆 Concept mix-up
    凤仙花、羊角豆、橡胶靠自动爆裂；梵天花、相思草靠钩刺（人类或动物）。
    Balsam, okra and rubber disperse by explosion; Caesarweed and love grass by hooks (humans or animals).
  3. 概念混淆 Concept mix-up
    仙人掌的针状叶减少水分流失；它的尖刺也用来自卫——一个特征两种作用。
    The cactus's needle-like leaves reduce water loss and its spines also defend it — one feature, two uses.
  4. 概念混淆 Concept mix-up
    落叶是为了减少水分流失，不是因为植物生病。
    Shedding leaves is to reduce water loss, not because the plant is sick.
  5. 答题错误 Exam-answer error
    种子传到远处，是为了减少与母株争夺阳光、水和养料。
    Seeds are spread far to avoid competing with the parent plant for light, water and nutrients.
  6. 概念混淆 Concept mix-up
    胶乳让皮肤发痒；毒素（如夹竹桃）误食会中毒——不要混淆。
    Latex makes skin itchy; poison (e.g. oleander) harms you if eaten — don't confuse them.
  7. 概念混淆 Concept mix-up
    ❌ 植物遇到危险也能逃走。✅ 植物不能移动，只能靠本身的特征（刺、毒、胶乳……）保护自己。
    ❌ Plants can escape danger. ✅ Plants can't move, so they rely on their own features (thorns, poison, latex…).
  8. 安全 Safety
    ❌ 看到漂亮的野果或植物就可以摸、可以吃。✅ 有些植物有毒素（夹竹桃、海檬果）或胶乳、细毛，误食会中毒、碰到会发痒——只看不摸。
    ❌ Pretty wild fruits and plants are safe to touch or eat. ✅ Some have poison (oleander, sea mango) or latex/hairs that itch – look, don't touch or taste.
  9. 概念混淆 Concept mix-up
    ❌ 靠风力传播的种子又大又重。✅ 它们细小轻盈，有薄翅或伞状细毛。
    ❌ Wind-dispersed seeds are big and heavy. ✅ They are small and light, with thin wings or parachute hairs.
  10. 用词陷阱 Wording trap
    ❌ 写“种子会飞”。✅ 写“种子靠风力传播，因为它轻、有薄翅。”
    ❌ "The seed flies." ✅ "The seed is dispersed by wind because it is light with thin wings."
  11. 看图/读表错误 Diagram/table reading
    ❌ 看图时只凭颜色判断传播方法。✅ 看结构：薄翅/细毛→风；纤维气室→水；干瘪的荚→爆裂；钩刺或甜果→人类或动物。
    ❌ Judging dispersal from colour alone. ✅ Look at the structure: wings/hairs → wind; fibres/air → water; dry pods → explosion; hooks or sweet fruit → animals.
  12. 答题错误 Exam-answer error
    ❌ 解释仙人掌只写“它很耐旱”。✅ 写特征 + 作用：“针状叶叶面小，减少水分流失。”
    ❌ "It survives drought." ✅ Feature + job: "Needle-like leaves have a small surface, reducing water loss."
  13. 答题错误 Exam-answer error
    ❌ 预测落在母树下的幼苗长得最好。✅ 母树下的幼苗要争夺阳光、水和养料，长得较差。
    ❌ Predicting that seedlings under the parent grow best. ✅ They must compete for light, water and nutrients, so they grow worse.
  14. 概念混淆 Concept mix-up
    ❌ 莲蓬靠风力传播。✅ 莲蓬轻盈、有气囊和纤维，靠水力传播。
    ❌ The lotus pod is dispersed by wind. ✅ It is light with air sacs and fibres, dispersed by water.
  15. 概念混淆 Concept mix-up
    ❌ 椰子树的羽状叶是为了减少水分流失。✅ 羽状叶让风穿过，减少风的阻力，帮助抵抗强风。
    ❌ Coconut palms' feather-like leaves reduce water loss. ✅ They let wind pass through, reducing wind resistance.
🇲🇾 生活中的科学 Science in real life (Malaysia)
  • 马来西亚的橡胶树每年在较干旱的季节（大约二、三月）落叶。
    Malaysian rubber trees shed their leaves in the drier season (around February–March).
  • 在学校草场跑步后，袜子上常黏着有钩刺的种子——这就是靠人类传播。
    After running on the school field, hooked seeds often stick to your socks — dispersal by humans.
  • 海边的椰子树和红树林：果实落入水中，漂流到别处生长。
    Coconut trees and mangroves by the sea: fruits fall into the water and float to new places.
  • 巴刹里的榴梿有尖刺，波罗蜜切开会流出黏黏的胶乳。
    At the pasar, durians have thorns and cut jackfruit oozes sticky latex.

结论 Conclusion 示范 Example 1
用风扇吹三种种子 P、Q、R，量出种子被吹到的距离：P 120 cm（有薄翅），Q 15 cm，R 8 cm。
A fan blows seeds P, Q and R; distances: P 120 cm (thin wings), Q 15 cm, R 8 cm.
❓
根据结果作出一个结论。
Write a conclusion from the results.
[1 分 marks]
❌ 弱答案 Weak answer · 0/1
P 最远。
P went farthest.
✅ 满分答案 Full-mark answer · 1/1
有薄翅的种子被风吹得最远，所以种子 P 靠风力传播。
Seeds with thin wings are blown the farthest, so seed P is dispersed by wind.
💬 评分理由 Mark by mark
  • “P 最远”只是重复结果。
    "P went farthest" only repeats the result.
  • 结论要把特征和结果连起来，回答实验的问题。
    A conclusion links the feature to the result and answers the experiment's question.
