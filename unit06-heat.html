🌡️6.1 热与温度6.1 Heat and TemperatureS6 p.130-134, p.144

  • 热是把物质变热的一种能。
    Heat is a form of energy that makes matter hot.
  • 温度是物质的冷热程度。我们不能单凭触摸来确定冷热程度，所以要用温度计准确测量。
    Temperature is how hot or cold matter is. We cannot rely on touch alone to tell how hot or cold something is, so we measure it accurately with a thermometer.
  • 热总是从温度较高的一端传到温度较低的一端。
    Heat always flows from the hotter end to the colder end.
  • 常用的温度单位是摄氏度（°C）。开尔文（K）和华氏温度（°F）也是温度单位。
    The usual unit of temperature is degree Celsius (°C). Kelvin (K) and Fahrenheit (°F) are also units of temperature.
  • 实验室温度计有酒精温度计和水银温度计两种。
    There are two kinds of laboratory thermometer: the alcohol thermometer and the mercury thermometer.
  • 温度计的部分：玻璃泡、刻度、玻璃管、酒精柱（或水银柱）、捏手处。
    Parts of a thermometer: bulb, scale, glass tube, alcohol column (or mercury column) and handle.
  • 酒精柱或水银柱会根据周围的冷热程度而上升或下降。
    The alcohol or mercury column rises or falls depending on how hot or cold the surroundings are.
  • 水银是有毒的液体，使用水银温度计时要小心。
    Mercury is a poisonous liquid, so be careful when using a mercury thermometer.
  • 正确使用温度计：
    Using a thermometer correctly:
  • 以直立方式拿着温度计的捏手处。
    Hold the thermometer upright by its handle.
  • 把玻璃泡浸入水里，不能碰到烧杯底和烧杯壁。
    Put the bulb in the water; it must not touch the bottom or the sides of the beaker.
  • 等酒精柱或水银柱停止移动后才读数；读数时温度计不能离开热水。
    Read only after the column stops moving; the thermometer must stay in the hot water while reading.
  • 读数时，视线要与液柱的弯月面平行。
    When reading, keep your eye level with the curved top of the liquid column.
  • 水开始变成冰块时的温度是冰点（0°C）；水开始变成水蒸气时的温度是沸点（100°C）。
    The temperature at which water starts to turn into ice is the freezing point (0°C); the temperature at which water starts to turn into water vapour is the boiling point (100°C).
  • 水达到沸点后，继续加热，温度保持不变（100°C）。
    Once water reaches its boiling point, its temperature stays the same (100°C) even if you keep heating.
  • 热水搁置在室内，温度会逐渐下降至室温，然后保持不变。
    Hot water left in a room cools down gradually to room temperature and then stays the same.
  • 室温一般是 25°C 至 27°C。 extra 补充
    Room temperature is usually 25°C to 27°C.
  • 活动 2 水的冰点和沸点：烧杯装满冰块，用本生灯加热，每隔 2 分钟测量并记录温度，把资料整理成线形图，再分享说明水的冰点和沸点。加热时本生灯的火势不能太大，以免烧杯破裂。 📗 课本 S6 p.133
    Activity 2 – freezing and boiling point of water: fill a beaker with ice, heat it with a Bunsen burner, measure and record the temperature every 2 minutes, put the data into a line graph, then share the freezing and boiling points. Keep the flame small so the beaker does not crack.
  • 线形图（课本图）：温度先在 0°C（冰点）保持一段时间，冰块融化后逐渐上升，约在第 12 分钟到达 100°C（沸点），之后继续加热，温度保持不变。课本提示：不同的液体有不同的沸点。 📗 课本 S6 p.133-134
    The line graph (textbook figure): the temperature first stays at 0°C (freezing point) for a while, then rises gradually once the ice has melted, reaching 100°C (boiling point) at about 12 minutes; after that, even with continued heating, it stays the same. The textbook adds: different liquids have different boiling points.
  • 活动 3 热水会降温吗？：把 250 mℓ 热水倒入烧杯，搁置室内 30 分钟，每隔 2 分钟测量水温。结果：水温逐渐下降至室温，然后保持不变——热从热水传到周围较冷的空气中，逐渐散热。 📗 课本 S6 p.134
    Activity 3 – does hot water cool down?: pour 250 mℓ of hot water into a beaker, leave it in the room for 30 minutes and measure the temperature every 2 minutes. Result: the temperature falls gradually to room temperature, then stays the same – heat passes from the hot water to the cooler air around it (losing heat gradually).

🔥6.2 热对物质的影响（热胀冷缩）6.2 Effects of Heat on Matter (Thermal Expansion and Contraction)S6 p.135-146

  • 物质受热后：变热、温度上升、膨胀。
    When matter gains heat: it becomes hotter, its temperature rises and it expands.
  • 物质散热后：变冷、温度下降、收缩。
    When matter loses heat: it becomes colder, its temperature falls and it contracts.
  • 铁球和铁环实验：原本铁球能穿过铁环；加热后铁球膨胀，不能穿过；放进冷水后铁球收缩，又能穿过。
    Ball-and-ring experiment: at first the iron ball passes through the ring; after heating it expands and cannot pass; after cooling in cold water it contracts and passes through again.
  • 彩色水放进热水中，水位上升；放进冷水中，水位下降。
    Coloured water in hot water: the water level rises; in cold water: the level falls.
  • 玻璃瓶里的空气受热会膨胀，使套在瓶口的气球变大；放进冷水后，空气收缩，气球变小。
    Air in a glass bottle expands when heated and blows up the balloon on the neck; in cold water the air contracts and the balloon shrinks.
  • 水散热收缩，但降到冰点结成冰块时反而会膨胀，这是一种特殊的膨胀现象。
    Water contracts when it loses heat, but when it freezes into ice at the freezing point it expands – a special case of expansion.
  • 应用热胀冷缩原理的例子：
    Examples of thermal expansion and contraction:
  • 火车铁轨之间留有空隙，是为了避免铁轨在天气热时因膨胀而变形。
    Gaps are left between railway tracks to stop the tracks bending when they expand in hot weather.
  • 电线松弛地悬挂在电线杆上，是为了防止电线在天气冷时因收缩而被扯断。
    Electric cables hang loosely on poles so they are not pulled and snapped when they contract in cold weather.
  • 高架桥和桥面上都留有空隙，是为了避免热天时因膨胀而裂开。
    Gaps are left in flyovers and bridge decks so they do not crack when they expand in hot weather.
  • 人行道地砖之间留有空隙，是为了避免热天时地砖膨胀而裂开。
    Gaps are left between pavement tiles so the tiles do not crack when they expand in hot weather.
  • 解决生活难题：把凹陷的乒乓球放进热水，里面的空气膨胀，乒乓球就恢复原状。
    Solving everyday problems: put a dented ping-pong ball into hot water; the air inside expands and the ball pops back into shape.
  • 把拧不开的瓶盖放进热水里浸一浸，金属盖受热膨胀，就容易拧开。 extra 补充
    Dip a stuck bottle cap in hot water; the metal cap expands and becomes easy to unscrew.
  • 紧紧套在一起的两个玻璃杯：在上面的杯子加入冰块，上杯冷却收缩，就能分开。
    Two glasses stuck together: put ice in the top glass so it cools and contracts, and they come apart.
  • 应用热胀冷缩原理的用具：温度计、消防喷头等。
    Devices that use thermal expansion and contraction: thermometers, fire sprinklers, etc.
  • 活动 4 感觉热与冷：把塑料、铁和木制的汤匙同时浸入热水 5 分钟，取出后触摸感觉温度；再浸入冷水 5 分钟，取出触摸比较。结论：材料受热会变热，散热后会变冷。（热水不能太烫，以免烫伤） 📗 课本 S6 p.135
    Activity 4 – feeling hot and cold: put plastic, iron and wooden spoons into hot water for 5 minutes, take them out and feel their temperature; then put them in cold water for 5 minutes and feel again. Conclusion: materials become hot when they gain heat and become cold when they lose heat. (Don't use water that is too hot – avoid scalds.)
  • 活动 5 测量水的温度：P、Q 烧杯各装 250 mℓ 水并测温。把加热了 3 分钟的铁球放进 P 烧杯，水温上升；把冰块加进 Q 烧杯，水温下降。（热从较热的物体传给较冷的物体） 📗 课本 S6 p.136
    Activity 5 – measuring the water temperature: put 250 mℓ of water in each of beakers P and Q and measure the temperature. Put an iron ball heated for 3 minutes into P – the water temperature rises; add ice cubes to Q – the water temperature falls. (Heat passes from the hotter object to the cooler one.)
  • 活动 6 热胀冷缩小魔术：A 铁球——加热 2 分钟后穿不过铁环，放进冷水 2 分钟后又能穿过；B 彩色水——锥形瓶浸热水 5 分钟，水位上升；再浸冷水 5 分钟，水位下降（用标记笔在玻璃管上标出水位，方便比较）；C 空气——气球套在玻璃瓶口，放进热水气球胀大，放进冷水气球缩小。 📗 课本 S6 p.136-137
    Activity 6 – expansion and contraction magic: A iron ball – after 2 minutes of heating it cannot pass through the ring; after 2 minutes in cold water it can again; B coloured water – flask in hot water for 5 minutes, level rises; then in cold water for 5 minutes, level falls (mark the level on the glass tube with a marker to compare); C air – a balloon over a bottle mouth inflates in hot water and shrinks in cold water.
  • 突破思维（推理）：在体积和温度相同的两杯水中，分别加入 3 块和 6 块冰块，2 分钟后水温不会一样——冰块越多，吸走的热量越多，水温降得越低。 📗 课本 S6 p.138
    Think harder (reasoning): if 3 and 6 ice cubes are added to two cups of water of equal volume and temperature, after 2 minutes the temperatures will not be the same – more ice takes away more heat, so the water gets colder.
  • 用粒子解释（师注）：物质受热时，粒子的振动幅度加大，所以膨胀；散热时，粒子的振动幅度减少，所以收缩。 📗 课本 S6 p.138
    Explaining with particles (teacher's note): when a substance gains heat, its particles vibrate more widely, so it expands; when it loses heat, they vibrate less, so it contracts.
  • 热气球：空气受热膨胀后会变得较轻并上升，热气球就是利用这个原理。 📗 课本 S6 p.138
    Hot-air balloon: air that expands when heated becomes lighter and rises – this is how a hot-air balloon works.

💡 你知道吗？ Did you know? extra 补充

⚠️ 常见错误 Common mistakes
  1. 热是一种能；温度是冷热程度——两者不同。
    Heat is a form of energy; temperature is how hot or cold something is — not the same.
  2. 读温度计时，视线要与液柱的弯月面平行，玻璃泡不能碰到烧杯。
    Read the thermometer with your eye level with the liquid, and keep the bulb off the beaker.
  3. 热总是从温度较高的地方传到温度较低的地方，不会反过来。
    Heat always flows from hotter to colder, never the other way.
  4. 水沸腾后继续加热，温度保持在 100°C，不会再上升。
    Once water boils, further heating keeps it at 100°C; it does not rise.
  5. 热胀冷缩：物质受热膨胀、散热收缩；水结冰时却会膨胀（特殊情况）。
    Expansion and contraction: matter expands when heated and contracts when cooled; water expands when it freezes (a special case).
  6. 温度的单位写 °C，不要只写数字。
    Write the unit °C, not just a number.

✍️ 考试答题示范 Worked exam answers (Section C)

诠释资料 Interpreting data 示范 Example 1
加热一杯水，每 2 分钟记录温度：0 分 28°C、2 分 46°C、4 分 64°C、6 分 82°C、8 分 100°C、10 分 100°C。
A beaker of water is heated; temperature every 2 min: 0 min 28°C, 2 min 46°C, 4 min 64°C, 6 min 82°C, 8 min 100°C, 10 min 100°C.
❓
(a) 说出加热时间和水温的关系。(b) 为什么 8 分钟后温度不再上升？
(a) State the relationship between heating time and water temperature. (b) Why does the temperature stop rising after 8 min?
[2 分 marks]
❌ 弱答案 Weak answer · 0/2
(a) 温度上升。(b) 因为水满了。
(a) The temperature goes up. (b) Because the water is full.
✅ 满分答案 Full-mark answer · 2/2
(a) 加热时间越长，水温越高（直到 100°C）。(b) 水已达到沸点 100°C，继续加热时水在沸腾，温度保持不变。
(a) The longer it is heated, the higher the temperature (up to 100°C). (b) The water has reached its boiling point of 100°C; while it boils the temperature stays the same.
💬 为什么？ Why?
  • (a) 要说出两个变数的关系，“温度上升”只说了一个。
    (a) Give the link between two variables; "temperature goes up" names only one.
  • (b) 要用沸点这个科学词语。
    (b) Use the science word boiling point.
推断 Inference 示范 Example 2
铁球原本能穿过铁环；把铁球加热后，它不能穿过铁环。
The iron ball could pass through the ring; after heating, it cannot.
❓
作出一个推断。
Make an inference.
[1 分 marks]
❌ 弱答案 Weak answer · 0/1
铁球很热。
The ball is hot.
✅ 满分答案 Full-mark answer · 1/1
铁球受热膨胀，体积变大，所以不能穿过铁环。
The ball expanded when heated, its volume became bigger, so it cannot pass through the ring.
💬 为什么？ Why?
  • “很热”是观察，不是解释。
    "Hot" is an observation, not an explanation.
  • 用“受热膨胀”说明为什么穿不过。
    Use "expands when heated" to explain why it cannot pass.
