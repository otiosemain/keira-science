🦔第 1 课 · 3.1 动物的物种生存Lesson 1 · 3.1 Survival of Animal Species

🤔 你有没有想过…… Have you ever wondered…
在花园里，为什么你很难发现停在树枝上的竹节虫？
In a garden, why is a stick insect so hard to spot on a branch?
它的颜色和形状像树枝——这叫伪装，让天敌看不见它。动物有很多这样的“生存秘诀”。
Its colour and shape match the twig – that's camouflage, hiding it from predators. Animals have many such survival tricks.
步骤 1 Step 1

为什么要“物种生存”？ Why survival of the species?

动物必须确保物种能继续生存，让后代延续下去，避免绝种。它们用外形特征或特殊本能来自卫、适应气候和保护后代。
Animals must make sure their species survives and has young to carry on, avoiding extinction. They use body features or special behaviour to defend themselves, cope with climate and protect their young.
❓ 想一想 · Check
“物种生存”最主要的目的是什么？
What is the main aim of survival of the species?
物种生存 = 物种能继续存在。
Survival = the species carries on existing.
S1 p.18
步骤 2 Step 2

自卫的方法 Ways to defend

角（鹿、犀牛）；尖刺（豪猪、针鼹）；硬壳（乌龟缩进壳里）；坚硬的鳞片+蜷曲身体（穿山甲）；喷出黑墨（鱿鱼）；自断身体部位（壁虎）；伪装（变色龙、竹节虫）；假眼睛（蝴蝶）；体形膨胀（河豚）；毒刺/毒牙（蝎子、毒蛇）；难闻的气味（臭鼬）；群居（大象）；强壮的腿（斑马、兔子）。
Horns (deer, rhino); spines (porcupine, echidna); hard shell (tortoise pulls in); hard scales + rolling up (pangolin); ink (squid); dropping a body part (gecko); camouflage (chameleon, stick insect); false eyes (butterfly); puffing up (pufferfish); stings/fangs (scorpion, snake); bad smell (skunk); living in groups (elephant); strong legs (zebra, rabbit).
🚫 别误会！ Don't be fooled!
❌ 伪装和假眼睛是同一种方法。Camouflage and false eyes are the same.
✅ 伪装是融入环境、不被发现；假眼睛是吓跑天敌。Camouflage hides by blending in; false eyes scare predators away.
❓ 想一想 · Check
壁虎自断尾巴，有什么用？
Why does a gecko drop its tail?
掉下的尾巴吸引天敌，壁虎趁机逃走。
The dropped tail distracts the predator while the gecko escapes.
🧠 为什么？ · Why?
大象为什么群居？
Why do elephants live in groups?
群居可以从各个方向及时发现天敌，减少被攻击的可能。
A group can spot predators from every direction, reducing attacks.
S6 p.42-61
步骤 3 Step 3

适应寒冷和炎热 Coping with cold and heat

寒冷：厚毛、厚脂肪、小耳朵减少热量散失（北极狐、北极熊、海象）；企鹅有浓密羽毛；有些动物迁徙或冬眠（降低呼吸率和心率，减少能量消耗）。炎热：耳廓狐的大耳朵帮助散热；骆驼的驼峰储存脂肪；很多动物躲进阴凉的地洞，夜晚才出来。
Cold: thick fur, thick fat, small ears cut heat loss (Arctic fox, polar bear, walrus); penguins have dense feathers; some migrate or hibernate (lower breathing and heart rate to save energy). Hot: the fennec fox's big ears lose heat; a camel's hump stores fat; many animals hide in cool burrows and come out at night.
🔁 打个比方 Think of it like this
像我们的衣服：去冷的地方穿厚外套（厚毛、脂肪），热天穿薄衣、开风扇（大耳朵散热）。
Like our clothes: a thick coat for cold places (fur, fat); light clothes and a fan on hot days (big ears lose heat).
🏠 自己试试 Try it yourself
（有大人陪同）用手摸一摸冰水，再把手放进装满食油的塑料袋里摸冰水（补充活动）。哪一次比较冷？
(With an adult, extra activity) Touch ice water with a bare hand, then with your hand inside a bag of cooking oil. Which feels colder?
❓ 想一想 · Check
耳廓狐的大耳朵有什么作用？
What do the fennec fox's big ears do?
大耳朵表面积大，散热快。
Large ears have more surface to lose heat.
🧠 为什么？ · Why?
北极熊的毛下是黑色皮肤，有什么好处？
What's the advantage of the black skin under a polar bear's fur?
黑色皮肤能吸收阳光的热量，帮助保暖。
Black skin absorbs heat from sunlight, helping it stay warm.
S6 p.42-61
步骤 4 Step 4

保护后代 Protecting the young

繁殖方法有胎生（猴子、老虎、鲸）和卵生（鸟、鱼、蛇）。保护卵：藏起来（海龟埋在沙里）、孵卵（鸡、企鹅）、守护卵（蛇、鳄鱼）、黏液包住卵（青蛙）。保护幼儿：哺乳、喂养、育儿袋（袋鼠）、含在嘴里（鳄鱼）、群居保护。
Animals are viviparous (monkey, tiger, whale) or oviparous (birds, fish, snakes). Eggs are hidden (turtles bury them), incubated (hen, penguin), guarded (snake, crocodile) or wrapped in jelly (frogs). Young are fed milk, fed food, kept in a pouch (kangaroo), carried in the mouth (crocodile) or protected by the group.
❓ 想一想 · Check
企鹅保护卵的方法是？
How does a penguin protect its egg?
企鹅雌性产卵，雄性孵卵。
The female lays; the male incubates.
S6 p.42-61
⭐ 大道理 Big idea
每一个特征和行为，都是为了让物种活下去、传下去。
Every feature and behaviour helps the species survive and carry on.

🎨第 2 课 · 3.2 创造动物模型Lesson 2 · 3.2 Creating an Animal Model

🤔 你有没有想过…… Have you ever wondered…
如果你要设计一只住在沙漠里的新动物，它需要什么特征？
If you designed a new desert animal, what features would it need?
它要能自卫，也要能适应炎热干燥：例如尖刺、大耳朵散热、躲在地洞、从食物中获取水分……
It must defend itself and cope with heat and dryness: e.g. spines, big ears to lose heat, a burrow, getting water from food…
步骤 1 Step 1

每个特征都要有作用 Every feature needs a job

设计动物模型时，每个特征都要写出作用：尖刺——使敌人不敢靠近；尾巴——使身体保持稳定；坚硬的鳞片——保护身体；含剧毒的平滑表皮——让敌人中毒。
When designing an animal model, give each feature its job: spines – keep enemies away; tail – keep balance; hard scales – protect the body; smooth poisonous skin – poison enemies.
🚫 别误会！ Don't be fooled!
❌ 模型画得越漂亮分数越高。A prettier drawing scores more.
✅ 分数在于特征 + 作用标示清楚，并符合环境。Marks come from clearly labelled feature + job that fit the habitat.
🏠 自己试试 Try it yourself
用回收材料（纸盒、瓶盖、吸管）做一只“沙漠动物”，给每个部分贴上标签：特征 + 作用。
Build a "desert animal" from recycled materials and label each part: feature + job.
🧠 为什么？ · Why?
为什么沙漠动物模型最好有大耳朵？
Why give a desert animal model big ears?
大耳朵能帮助散热，适应炎热的气候。
Big ears help lose heat in the hot climate.
❓ 想一想 · Check
哪一个特征最适合帮助动物在寒冷地区生存？
Which feature best helps an animal survive in a cold place?
厚脂肪减少热量散失。
Thick fat reduces heat loss.
S1 p.23
⭐ 大道理 Big idea
好的设计 = 特征 + 作用 + 符合栖息地。
A good design = feature + job + fits the habitat.

🔗第 3 课 · 3.3 生物之间的食物联系Lesson 3 · 3.3 Food Relationships Between Living Things

🤔 你有没有想过…… Have you ever wondered…
你早餐吃的米饭里的能量，最先是从哪里来的？
Where did the energy in your breakfast rice first come from?
来自太阳！稻用阳光进行光合作用制造食物，我们吃了稻米，就间接得到太阳的能量。
From the Sun! The rice plant used sunlight to make food by photosynthesis; eating rice passes the Sun's energy to us.
步骤 1 Step 1

食物链 Food chains

食物链表示“吃”与“被吃”的关系。例：稻 → 蚱蜢 → 鸡 → 蛇。箭头从被吃者指向吃者，也表示能量传递的方向。稻自己制造食物，是生产者；其他是消费者。
A food chain shows who eats whom. e.g. paddy → grasshopper → chicken → snake. Arrows point from the eaten to the eater – the direction energy flows. Paddy makes its own food (producer); the rest are consumers.
🔁 打个比方 Think of it like this
箭头像“送能量的快递”：从被吃的生物送到吃它的生物。
The arrow is an "energy delivery": from the eaten to the eater.
🚫 别误会！ Don't be fooled!
❌ 食物链的第一个是太阳。A food chain starts with the Sun.
✅ 太阳是能源，但食物链第一个一定是生产者（绿色植物）。The Sun is the energy source, but a chain starts with a producer (green plant).
❓ 想一想 · Check
“辣椒 → 蝴蝶 → 螳螂 → 麻雀 → 猫头鹰”中，生产者是？
In "chilli → butterfly → mantis → sparrow → owl", the producer is…
绿色植物自己制造食物。
Green plants make their own food.
🧠 为什么？ · Why?
为什么能量不会完全传给下一个生物？
Why isn't all the energy passed on to the next organism?
每个生物都把一部分能量用来进行生命过程（如活动、呼吸）。
Each organism uses some energy for its own life processes (moving, breathing…).
S1 p.24-25
步骤 2 Step 2

食物网和数量变化 Food webs and population changes

几条食物链连在一起成为食物网。例（油棕园）：油棕 → 老鼠 → 蛇；油棕 → 松鼠 → 蛇；老鼠 → 猫头鹰。一个生物的数量改变，同一栖息地里其他生物的数量也会受影响：蛇锐减 → 老鼠、松鼠增加。
Linked chains form a food web. e.g. oil palm estate: oil palm → rat → snake; oil palm → squirrel → snake; rat → owl. If one population changes, others in the habitat are affected: fewer snakes → more rats and squirrels.
🎬 动画 Animation：食物网：数量的变化 Food web: population changes
油棕是生产者；老鼠和松鼠吃油棕；蛇吃老鼠和松鼠。
Oil palm is the producer; rats and squirrels eat it; snakes eat rats and squirrels.
如果大量的蛇被捕捉，老鼠和松鼠的天敌减少了……
If many snakes are caught, rats and squirrels have fewer predators…
……所以老鼠和松鼠的数量增加，油棕被吃得更多。一种生物的数量改变，会影响食物网中的其他生物。
…so rats and squirrels increase and more oil palm is eaten. A change in one population affects others in the web.

🏠 自己试试 Try it yourself
画一画你家附近（花园、稻田或池塘）的一条食物链，用箭头连起来。
Draw a food chain from near your home (garden, paddy field or pond) with arrows.
❓ 想一想 · Check
如果油棕园的蛇都被捉走，老鼠的数量会怎样？
If all the snakes in the oil palm estate are caught, the rats will…
少了天敌，老鼠会增加。
With fewer predators, rats increase.
🧠 为什么？ · Why?
食物网中麻雀突然消失，猫头鹰可能会怎样？为什么？
If the sparrows vanish from the food web, what might the owls do, and why?
猫头鹰缺少食物，可能会迁移到其他地方。
The owls lose a food source and may move elsewhere.
S6 p.64-80
步骤 3 Step 3

生态平衡 The balance of nature

每种生物都是食物网的重要成员。我们应保护栖息地，不支持野生动物的非法买卖（如象牙），以维持生态平衡。
Every organism matters in the web. We should protect habitats and refuse illegal wildlife trade (e.g. ivory) to keep the balance of nature.
❓ 想一想 · Check
哪一个行为有助于维持生态平衡？
Which action helps keep the balance of nature?
保护栖息地让食物网保持完整。
Protecting habitats keeps the food web whole.
S6 p.76
⭐ 大道理 Big idea
所有生物都通过食物链和食物网连在一起，能量从太阳开始一路传下去。
All living things are linked by food chains and webs, passing on energy that starts with the Sun.

🦔 3.1 动物的物种生存🎨 3.2 创造动物模型🔗 3.3 生物之间的食物联系

🦔3.1 动物的物种生存3.1 Survival of Animal SpeciesS1 p.18-22, J-3, J-4, 课本 Textbook S6 p.42-61

  • 动物必须确保物种的生存，让后代继续延续，避免绝种。
    Animals must make sure their species survives, so that offspring continue and the species does not become extinct.
  • 动物用外形特征或特殊本能来保护自己，以及适应寒冷或炎热的气候。
    Animals use physical characteristics or special instincts to protect themselves and to adapt to cold or hot climates.
  • 自卫方法：
    Ways animals defend themselves:
  • 尖刺（例：针鼹、豪猪）：让敌人不敢靠近。
    Spines (e.g. echidna, porcupine): stop enemies from coming near.
  • 坚硬的鳞片（例：穿山甲）：保护身体，避免被擦伤或咬伤。
    Hard scales (e.g. pangolin): protect the body from scratches and bites.
  • 难闻的气味（例：臭鼬）：让敌人感到不适。
    Foul smell (e.g. skunk): makes enemies uncomfortable.
  • 喷出黑墨（例：鱿鱼）：干扰敌人的视线，趁机逃跑。
    Squirting ink (e.g. squid): blocks the enemy's view so the animal can escape.
  • 自断身体部位（例：壁虎）：引开敌人的注意力，趁机逃跑。
    Shedding a body part (e.g. gecko): distracts the enemy so the animal can escape.
  • 伪装（例：变色龙）：身体颜色与环境融合，不容易被敌人发现。
    Camouflage (e.g. chameleon): body colour blends with the surroundings so enemies cannot easily see it.
  • 其他自卫方法：角（鹿、犀牛）、假眼睛（蝴蝶）、缩进壳里（乌龟、蜗牛）、体形膨胀（河豚）、毒刺（蝎子）、群居（大象）、强壮的腿（斑马）。
    Other defences: horns (deer, rhinoceros), eyespots (butterfly), pulling into the shell (turtle, snail), inflating the body (pufferfish), venomous sting (scorpion), living in groups (elephant), strong legs (zebra).
  • 适应恶劣气候：
    Adapting to harsh climates:
  • 寒冷的气候：厚毛、厚脂肪、小耳朵（北极狐、鲸、海象）→ 减少热量散失，保暖。
    Cold climate: thick fur, thick layer of fat, small ears (Arctic fox, whale, walrus) → reduce heat loss to keep warm.
  • 炎热的气候：大耳朵（耳廓狐）→ 帮助散热；驼峰储存脂肪（骆驼）。
    Hot climate: big ears (fennec fox) → help lose heat; the camel's hump stores fat.
  • 行为：躲在地洞、夜晚才出来活动（避开白天炽热的阳光）；冬眠（降低呼吸率和心率，减少能量消耗）；在泥沼里打滚（散热降温）。
    Behaviour: hiding in burrows and coming out at night (avoids the hot daytime sun); hibernating (lower breathing and heart rate, use less energy); wallowing in mud (loses heat and cools down).
  • 繁殖和保护后代：动物的繁殖方法有两种：胎生和卵生。
    Reproduction and protecting young: animals reproduce in two ways: viviparous (give birth to live young) and oviparous (lay eggs).
  • 卵生例子：蝴蝶把卵产在叶子背面；企鹅雌性产卵、雄性孵卵；蟒蛇用身体盘绕卵堆保温；小丑鱼在珊瑚礁上产卵。
    Egg-laying examples: butterfly lays eggs under a leaf; penguin — female lays, male incubates; python coils around its eggs to keep them warm; clownfish lays eggs on coral.
  • 胎生例子：猴子、老虎（用乳汁哺育幼儿）、树袋熊（幼儿在育儿袋里成长）、蝎子（幼儿趴在母亲背上）。
    Live-birth examples: monkey, tiger (feeds young with milk), koala (young grow in the pouch), scorpion (young ride on the mother's back).
  • 保护后代的方法：把卵产在隐蔽的地方、把卵含在嘴里（罗非鱼）、成群袭击敌人（蜜蜂）、喂养幼儿。
    Ways to protect young: lay eggs in hidden places, carry them in the mouth (tilapia), attack enemies as a group (bees), feed the young.
  • 物种的生存：动物为了避免绝种，确保物种能继续生存所具有的能力。 📗 课本 S6 p.77
    Survival of the species: the abilities animals have so that their species does not die out and continues to survive.
  • 课本列出的自卫方法共 14 种：角、尖刺、硬壳、群居、伪装、假眼睛、强壮的腿、蜷曲身体、喷出黑墨、体形膨胀、毒刺或毒牙、坚硬的鳞片、难闻的气味、自断身体部位。 📗 课本 S6 p.77
    The textbook lists 14 ways of defence: horns, spines, hard shell, living in groups, camouflage, false eyes, strong legs, curling up, squirting ink, puffing up, poisonous sting or fangs, hard scales, bad smell, and shedding a body part.
  • 角：鹿、羚羊、犀牛、水牛用头上的尖角与天敌斗争。强壮的腿：青蛙、兔子、斑马用发达的腿逃离，有些甚至能把天敌踢走。 📗 课本 S6 p.43
    Horns: deer, antelope, rhinoceros and buffalo fight enemies with sharp horns. Strong legs: frogs, rabbits and zebras escape on powerful legs; some can even kick enemies away.
  • 硬壳：乌龟遇到危险时把头和四肢缩进壳里。蜷曲身体：穿山甲把身体蜷成一团，用坚硬的鳞片保护自己。 📗 课本 S6 p.44
    Hard shell: a tortoise pulls its head and legs into its shell when in danger. Curling up: a pangolin rolls into a ball and its hard scales protect it.
  • 毒刺或毒牙：毒蛇、蜈蚣、蝎子、蜜蜂等用毒刺或毒牙攻击天敌。尖刺：针鼹、豪猪和刺河豚的尖刺让天敌难以靠近。 📗 课本 S6 p.45
    Poisonous sting or fangs: venomous snakes, centipedes, scorpions and bees attack enemies with stings or fangs. Spines: the spines of echidnas, porcupines and porcupine fish keep enemies away.
  • 自断身体部位：壁虎、章鱼、螃蟹等受到惊扰或攻击时会自断身体部位，引开天敌的注意力并趁机逃跑。群居：大象、斑马、羚羊等群居，能从各个方向及时发现天敌，减少被攻击的可能性。 📗 课本 S6 p.46
    Shedding a body part: geckos, octopuses and crabs drop a body part when attacked to distract the enemy and escape. Living in groups: elephants, zebras and antelopes live in groups so they can spot enemies from every direction and are attacked less.
  • 伪装：随着环境改变身体颜色，或身体的颜色和花纹与环境融合，例：竹节虫、竹叶青蛇、石头鱼。假眼睛：有些蛾和蝴蝶翅膀上有像眼睛的斑点，能吓跑天敌。 📗 课本 S6 p.47-48
    Camouflage: changing body colour with the surroundings, or having colours and patterns that blend in, e.g. stick insect, green pit viper, stonefish. False eyes: some moths and butterflies have eye-like spots on their wings that scare enemies.
  • 锋利的爪、坚硬的喙、有力的尾巴等，也是动物遇到危险时用来自卫的外形特征。 📗 课本 S6 p.44
    Sharp claws, a hard beak and a powerful tail are other body features animals use for defence.
  • 寒冷地区：麝牛、驯鹿、北极狐、北极熊有厚毛；北极熊毛下的黑色皮肤能吸收阳光的热量；企鹅有浓密的羽毛，能保暖和防水；冬天来临时，有些鸟和鲸会迁徙到温暖的地方；有些动物会冬眠几个月。 📗 课本 S6 p.50-51
    Cold places: musk oxen, reindeer, Arctic foxes and polar bears have thick fur; the polar bear's black skin under its fur absorbs the Sun's heat; penguins have dense feathers that keep them warm and waterproof; in winter some birds and whales migrate to warmer places; some animals hibernate for months.
  • 炎热的沙漠：骆驼的驼峰储存脂肪，能转化成水分和能量；许多动物从食物中获取水分，并减少排尿；耳廓狐、蝎子、蛇等躲在阴凉的地洞里，减少水分流失。 📗 课本 S6 p.52-53
    Hot deserts: the camel's hump stores fat that can be turned into water and energy; many animals get water from their food and urinate less; fennec foxes, scorpions and snakes hide in cool, shady burrows to lose less water.
  • 保护卵的方法：① 把卵藏起来（海龟、鳄鱼、蜥蜴、壁虎、蝗虫把卵埋在沙或土里）；② 孵卵（鸡、企鹅用体温孵卵）；③ 守护卵（蛇、鸵鸟、鳄鱼守着卵）；④ 包裹着黏液的卵（青蛙和某些鱼产大量包着黏液的卵，黏成团块，天敌难以吞食）。 📗 课本 S6 p.57-58
    Ways to protect eggs: ① hiding eggs (turtles, crocodiles, lizards, geckos and grasshoppers bury eggs in sand or soil); ② incubating (hens and penguins keep eggs warm with body heat); ③ guarding eggs (snakes, ostriches, crocodiles); ④ eggs covered in slime (frogs and some fish lay many slimy eggs that stick in clumps, hard for enemies to eat).
  • 确保幼儿生存的方法：哺乳（斑马、羊、长颈鹿、猫）；喂养（鸟、北极熊、狮子）；把幼儿放在育儿袋（袋鼠）；把幼儿含在嘴里（鳄鱼）；与威胁幼儿的天敌对抗（母鸡、猴子）；群居保护幼儿（狮子、猴子、鹿）；把幼儿背在背上（天鹅、蝎子）。 📗 课本 S6 p.59-61
    Ways to make sure the young survive: feeding milk (zebra, goat, giraffe, cat); bringing food (birds, polar bears, lions); keeping the young in a pouch (kangaroo); carrying them in the mouth (crocodile); fighting enemies that threaten the young (hen, monkey); protecting them in a group (lions, monkeys, deer); carrying them on the back (swan, scorpion).

🔗3.3 生物之间的食物联系3.3 Food Relationships Between Living ThingsS1 p.24-25, J-4, J-5, 课本 Textbook S6 p.64-80

  • 食物链表示生物之间“吃”与“被吃”的关系。箭头表示被吃（也表示能量传递的方向）。
    A food chain shows the “eating” and “being eaten” relationship between living things. The arrow means is eaten by (and shows the direction energy is passed on).
  • 例子：稻 → 蚱蜢 → 鸡 → 蛇
    Example: rice → grasshopper → chicken → snake
  • 太阳是食物链中主要的能源。
    The Sun is the main source of energy in a food chain.
  • 稻是绿色植物，通过光合作用自己制造食物，是生产者。
    Rice is a green plant that makes its own food by photosynthesis, so it is a producer.
  • 蚱蜢、鸡和蛇以植物或其他动物为食，是消费者。
    The grasshopper, chicken and snake eat plants or other animals, so they are consumers.
  • 能量沿着食物链传递：稻 → 蚱蜢 → 鸡 → 蛇。每个生物都把一部分能量用来进行生命过程，所以能量不会完全传给下一个生物。
    Energy is passed along the chain: rice → grasshopper → chicken → snake. Each living thing uses some energy for life processes, so not all energy passes to the next one.
  • 几条食物链连在一起，成为食物网。
    Several food chains joined together make a food web.
  • 如果食物网里某个生物的数量增加或减少，同一栖息地里其他生物的数量都会受到影响。
    If the number of one living thing in a food web increases or decreases, the numbers of the other living things in the same habitat are affected.
  • 例：蛇的数量锐减 → 它的猎物（如老鼠、松鼠）的数量将增加。
    e.g. if the number of snakes falls sharply → the number of their prey (e.g. rats, squirrels) will increase.
  • 例：油棕园的食物网：油棕 → 老鼠 → 蛇；油棕 → 松鼠 → 蛇。生产者 = 油棕；终极消费者 = 蛇。
    e.g. oil palm plantation food web: oil palm → rat → snake; oil palm → squirrel → snake. Producer = oil palm; final consumer = snake.
  • 课本例子：辣椒 → 蝴蝶 → 螳螂 → 麻雀 → 猫头鹰。辣椒是生产者，其余都是消费者。 📗 课本 S6 p.65
    Textbook example: chilli plant → butterfly → praying mantis → sparrow → owl. The chilli plant is the producer; the rest are consumers.
  • 不同的栖息地有不同的食物链，例如稻田、草原、池塘和森林。 📗 课本 S6 p.66-68
    Different habitats have different food chains, e.g. paddy fields, grasslands, ponds and forests.
  • 太阳是所有生物主要的能源。食物链让所有生物直接或间接地从太阳获得能量，以继续生存。 📗 课本 S6 p.70
    The Sun is the main energy source for all living things. Food chains let every organism get energy from the Sun directly or indirectly so it can survive.
  • 例：如果食物网中的麻雀突然消失，猫头鹰缺少食物会迁移到其他地方；蝴蝶和毛虫的数量会增加。 📗 课本 S6 p.75
    e.g. if the sparrows in a food web suddenly disappear, the owls lose food and move elsewhere, while butterflies and caterpillars increase.
  • 每种生物都是食物网中重要的成员。我们应保护自然环境和栖息地，不支持野生动物的非法买卖（如象牙），以维持生态平衡。 📗 课本 S6 p.76
    Every living thing is an important member of the food web. We should protect nature and habitats and not support illegal wildlife trade (such as ivory), to keep the balance of nature.
📌 记住！ Remember!
箭头表示被吃，也表示能量传递的方向。
The arrow means is eaten by and shows the direction energy flows.
食物链总是从生产者（绿色植物）开始。
A food chain always starts with a producer (a green plant).
💡 你知道吗？ Did you know? extra 补充
能量沿着食物链传递时，每一级只有一小部分（大约十分之一）传给下一级。
As energy passes along a food chain, only a small part (about one tenth) reaches the next level.
⚠️ 常见错误 Common mistakes
  1. 看图/读表错误 Diagram/table reading
    食物链的箭头从被吃者指向吃者，表示能量传递的方向。
    Food-chain arrows point from the eaten to the eater, showing the direction energy moves.
  2. 概念混淆 Concept mix-up
    食物链第一个一定是生产者（绿色植物），不是太阳。
    A food chain always starts with a producer (green plant), not the Sun.
  3. 概念混淆 Concept mix-up
    骆驼的驼峰储存的是脂肪，不是水。
    A camel's hump stores fat, not water.
  4. 概念混淆 Concept mix-up
    伪装是与环境融合；假眼睛是吓跑天敌——两者不同。
    Camouflage blends in; false eyes scare enemies away — they are different.
  5. 答题错误 Exam-answer error
    预测数量变化要说增加还是减少，并说明原因（天敌/食物变多或变少）。
    When predicting population change, say increase or decrease and why (more/fewer predators or food).
  6. 概念混淆 Concept mix-up
    鲸和海豚是胎生的哺乳动物；企鹅是卵生的鸟。
    Whales and dolphins are viviparous mammals; penguins are oviparous birds.
  7. 概念混淆 Concept mix-up
    ❌ 冬眠就是睡很久的觉。✅ 冬眠时呼吸率和心率降低，减少能量消耗，度过寒冬。
    ❌ Hibernation is just a long sleep. ✅ Breathing and heart rate drop to save energy through winter.
  8. 概念混淆 Concept mix-up
    ❌ 能量会全部传给下一个生物。✅ 每个生物把一部分能量用在生命过程，只有一部分传下去。
    ❌ All energy passes to the next organism. ✅ Each uses some energy for life processes; only part is passed on.
  9. 概念混淆 Concept mix-up
    ❌ 大耳朵帮助保暖。✅ 大耳朵帮助散热（耳廓狐）；寒冷地区的动物耳朵小，减少热量散失。
    ❌ Big ears keep animals warm. ✅ Big ears lose heat (fennec fox); cold-climate animals have small ears.
  10. 用词陷阱 Wording trap
    ❌ 自卫写“它很厉害”。✅ 写出特征 + 作用：“豪猪有尖刺，使天敌不敢靠近。”
    ❌ "It's very fierce." ✅ Feature + job: "The porcupine's spines keep predators away."
  11. 看图/读表错误 Diagram/table reading
    ❌ 在食物网中找“老鼠吃什么”时看错箭头。✅ 指向老鼠的箭头来自它吃的生物；从老鼠出发的箭头指向吃它的生物。
    ❌ Misreading arrows when asked what a rat eats. ✅ Arrows into the rat come from its food; arrows out of it point to its predators.
  12. 答题错误 Exam-answer error
    ❌ 设计动物模型只画特征，不写作用。✅ 每个特征都要标示作用，并配合栖息地。
    ❌ Drawing features without their jobs. ✅ Label each feature's job and match the habitat.
  13. 概念混淆 Concept mix-up
    ❌ 壁虎断了尾巴就会死。✅ 自断尾巴是为了引开天敌的注意力，趁机逃跑。
    ❌ A gecko dies if it drops its tail. ✅ It's a trick to distract the predator and escape.
  14. 用词陷阱 Wording trap
    ❌ 把“终极消费者”写成“第一个消费者”。✅ 终极消费者是食物链最后一个生物（不被其他生物吃）。
    ❌ Calling the first consumer the top consumer. ✅ The top consumer is the last in the chain (not eaten by others).
  15. 概念混淆 Concept mix-up
    ❌ 群居只是因为动物喜欢在一起。✅ 群居能从各个方向及时发现天敌，保护自己和幼儿。
    ❌ Animals live in groups just because they like company. ✅ A group can spot predators from all sides and protect the young.

预测 Prediction 示范 Example 1
食物网：油棕 → 老鼠 → 蛇；油棕 → 松鼠 → 蛇；老鼠 → 猫头鹰。
Food web: oil palm → rat → snake; oil palm → squirrel → snake; rat → owl.
❓
如果大量的蛇被捕捉，预测松鼠的数量会怎样？说明原因。
If many snakes are caught, predict what happens to the squirrels. Give a reason.
[2 分 marks]
❌ 弱答案 Weak answer · 1/2
增加。
Increase.
✅ 满分答案 Full-mark answer · 2/2
松鼠的数量会增加，因为它的天敌（蛇）减少了。
The squirrel population will increase, because its predator (the snake) has decreased.
💬 评分理由 Mark by mark
  • 弱答案只有预测，没有原因，只得 1 分。
    The weak answer predicts but gives no reason: 1 mark only.
  • 原因要指出食物网里的联系：谁吃它、它吃谁。
    The reason must name the link in the web: who eats it or what it eats.
🧩 答题句型 Answer frame
句型：“……会增加/减少，因为……的天敌/食物变少/变多了。”
Frame: "… will increase/decrease because its predators/food become fewer/more."
解释 Explaining 示范 Example 2
北极熊生活在寒冷的北极。
Polar bears live in the cold Arctic.
❓
说明北极熊的一种外形特征，并解释它怎样帮助北极熊生存。
State one body feature of the polar bear and explain how it helps it survive.

北极熊有厚毛和厚脂肪，能减少身体热量散失，帮助它在寒冷的北极保暖。
The polar bear has thick fur and thick fat, which reduce heat loss and keep it warm in the cold Arctic.
💬 评分理由 Mark by mark
  • “有毛”太笼统，要说“厚毛”；“因为很冷”没有说出作用。
    "Has fur" is too vague — say "thick fur"; "because it is cold" does not give the function.
  • 特征 1 分 + 作用（减少热量散失）1 分。
    Feature 1 mark + function (reduces heat loss) 1 mark.

句型：“特征：……；作用：……，帮助它在……生存。”
Frame: "Feature: …; it … , helping it survive in …."

动物：穿山甲、鱿鱼、变色龙、乌龟、臭鼬、竹节虫。
Animals: pangolin, squid, chameleon, tortoise, skunk, stick insect.
