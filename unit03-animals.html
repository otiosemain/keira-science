单元 3：动物Unit 3: Animals

这一单元学习动物怎样保护自己、适应恶劣气候、繁殖后代，以及生物之间的食物链和食物网。
This unit covers how animals protect themselves, adapt to harsh climates, reproduce, and the food chains and food webs between living things.

五年级科学 Year 5 Science · KSSR Semakan · 来源 Source: S1 p.18-27 (Tunas Pelangi 科学 5A)

3.1 动物的物种生存3.1 Survival of Animal SpeciesS1 p.18-22, J-3, J-4, 课本 Textbook S6 p.42-61

  • 动物必须确保物种的生存，让后代继续延续，避免绝种。
    Animals must make sure their species survives, so that offspring continue and the species does not become extinct.
  • 动物用外形特征或特殊本能来保护自己，以及适应寒冷或炎热的气候。
    Animals use physical characteristics or special instincts to protect themselves and to adapt to cold or hot climates.
  • 自卫方法：
    Ways animals defend themselves:
  • 尖刺（例：针鼹、豪猪）：让敌人不敢靠近。
    Spines (e.g. echidna, porcupine): stop enemies from coming near.
  • 坚硬的鳞片（例：穿山甲）：保护身体，避免被擦伤或咬伤。
    Hard scales (e.g. pangolin): protect the body from scratches and bites.
  • 难闻的气味（例：臭鼬）：让敌人感到不适。
    Foul smell (e.g. skunk): makes enemies uncomfortable.
  • 喷出黑墨（例：鱿鱼）：干扰敌人的视线，趁机逃跑。
    Squirting ink (e.g. squid): blocks the enemy's view so the animal can escape.
  • 自断身体部位（例：壁虎）：引开敌人的注意力，趁机逃跑。
    Shedding a body part (e.g. gecko): distracts the enemy so the animal can escape.
  • 伪装（例：变色龙）：身体颜色与环境融合，不容易被敌人发现。
    Camouflage (e.g. chameleon): body colour blends with the surroundings so enemies cannot easily see it.
  • 其他自卫方法：角（鹿、犀牛）、假眼睛（蝴蝶）、缩进壳里（乌龟、蜗牛）、体形膨胀（河豚）、毒刺（蝎子）、群居（大象）、强壮的腿（斑马）。
    Other defences: horns (deer, rhinoceros), eyespots (butterfly), pulling into the shell (turtle, snail), inflating the body (pufferfish), venomous sting (scorpion), living in groups (elephant), strong legs (zebra).
  • 适应恶劣气候：
    Adapting to harsh climates:
  • 寒冷的气候：厚毛、厚脂肪、小耳朵（北极狐、鲸、海象）→ 减少热量散失，保暖。
    Cold climate: thick fur, thick layer of fat, small ears (Arctic fox, whale, walrus) → reduce heat loss to keep warm.
  • 炎热的气候：大耳朵（耳廓狐）→ 帮助散热；驼峰储存脂肪（骆驼）。
    Hot climate: big ears (fennec fox) → help lose heat; the camel's hump stores fat.
  • 行为：躲在地洞、夜晚才出来活动（避开白天炽热的阳光）；冬眠（降低呼吸率和心率，减少能量消耗）；在泥沼里打滚（散热降温）。
    Behaviour: hiding in burrows and coming out at night (avoids the hot daytime sun); hibernating (lower breathing and heart rate, use less energy); wallowing in mud (loses heat and cools down).
  • 繁殖和保护后代：动物的繁殖方法有两种：胎生和卵生。
    Reproduction and protecting young: animals reproduce in two ways: viviparous (give birth to live young) and oviparous (lay eggs).
  • 卵生例子：蝴蝶把卵产在叶子背面；企鹅雌性产卵、雄性孵卵；蟒蛇用身体盘绕卵堆保温；小丑鱼在珊瑚礁上产卵。
    Egg-laying examples: butterfly lays eggs under a leaf; penguin — female lays, male incubates; python coils around its eggs to keep them warm; clownfish lays eggs on coral.
  • 胎生例子：猴子、老虎（用乳汁哺育幼儿）、树袋熊（幼儿在育儿袋里成长）、蝎子（幼儿趴在母亲背上）。
    Live-birth examples: monkey, tiger (feeds young with milk), koala (young grow in the pouch), scorpion (young ride on the mother's back).
  • 保护后代的方法：把卵产在隐蔽的地方、把卵含在嘴里（罗非鱼）、成群袭击敌人（蜜蜂）、喂养幼儿。
    Ways to protect young: lay eggs in hidden places, carry them in the mouth (tilapia), attack enemies as a group (bees), feed the young.

3.2 创造动物模型3.2 Creating an Animal ModelS1 p.23, J-4, 课本 Textbook S6 p.62-63

  • 设计动物模型时，每个特征都要有作用：
    When designing an animal model, each feature must have a purpose:
  • 尖刺：使敌人不敢靠近。
    Spines: stop enemies coming near.
  • 尾巴：使身体保持稳定。
    Tail: keeps the body steady.
  • 坚硬的鳞片：保护身体。
    Hard scales: protect the body.
  • 含剧毒的平滑表皮：让敌人中毒。
    Smooth, highly poisonous skin: poisons enemies.
  • 练习：设计一只住在炎热干燥陆地的动物，要有保护自己的外形特征，还有适应炎热气候的特殊特征，并画出来、标示重点。
    Practice: design an animal that lives on hot, dry land, with a physical feature to protect itself and a special feature for the hot climate. Draw it and label the key features.

3.3 生物之间的食物联系3.3 Food Relationships Between Living ThingsS1 p.24-25, J-4, J-5, 课本 Textbook S6 p.64-80

  • 食物链表示生物之间“吃”与“被吃”的关系。箭头表示被吃（也表示能量传递的方向）。
    A food chain shows the “eating” and “being eaten” relationship between living things. The arrow means is eaten by (and shows the direction energy is passed on).
  • 例子：稻 → 蚱蜢 → 鸡 → 蛇
    Example: rice → grasshopper → chicken → snake
  • 太阳是食物链中主要的能源。
    The Sun is the main source of energy in a food chain.
  • 稻是绿色植物，通过光合作用自己制造食物，是生产者。
    Rice is a green plant that makes its own food by photosynthesis, so it is a producer.
  • 蚱蜢、鸡和蛇以植物或其他动物为食，是消费者。
    The grasshopper, chicken and snake eat plants or other animals, so they are consumers.
  • 能量沿着食物链传递：稻 → 蚱蜢 → 鸡 → 蛇。每个生物都把一部分能量用来进行生命过程，所以能量不会完全传给下一个生物。
    Energy is passed along the chain: rice → grasshopper → chicken → snake. Each living thing uses some energy for life processes, so not all energy passes to the next one.
  • 几条食物链连在一起，成为食物网。
    Several food chains joined together make a food web.
  • 如果食物网里某个生物的数量增加或减少，同一栖息地里其他生物的数量都会受到影响。
    If the number of one living thing in a food web increases or decreases, the numbers of the other living things in the same habitat are affected.
  • 例：蛇的数量锐减 → 它的猎物（如老鼠、松鼠）的数量将增加。
    e.g. if the number of snakes falls sharply → the number of their prey (e.g. rats, squirrels) will increase.
  • 例：油棕园的食物网：油棕 → 老鼠 → 蛇；油棕 → 松鼠 → 蛇。生产者 = 油棕；终极消费者 = 蛇。
    e.g. oil palm plantation food web: oil palm → rat → snake; oil palm → squirrel → snake. Producer = oil palm; final consumer = snake.
