单元 2：人类Unit 2: Humans

这一单元认识人体的骨骼系统和血液循环系统，以及身体各系统怎样互相配合。
This unit covers the human skeletal system and circulatory system, and how body systems work together.

五年级科学 Year 5 Science · KSSR Semakan · 来源 Source: S1 p.10-17 (Tunas Pelangi 科学 5A)

2.1 人体的骨骼系统2.1 The Human Skeletal SystemS1 p.10-12, J-2, 课本 Textbook S6 p.12-19

  • 骨骼系统由许多骨头组成，决定人的体型。
    The skeletal system is made of many bones and determines a person's body shape.
  • 头骨：像一顶头盔，保护脑，并形成脸部支架。
    Skull: like a helmet, it protects the brain and forms the frame of the face.
  • 肋骨：像一个坚固的笼子，保护心脏、肺、肝等内脏。
    Rib cage: like a strong cage, it protects organs such as the heart, lungs and liver.
  • 脊柱：位于身体正中央，支撑头部和身体，让身体挺直。
    Vertebral column (backbone): in the middle of the body, it supports the head and body and keeps the body upright.
  • 手部骨骼：进行各种伸展和抬、举、抓、握等动作。
    Arm and hand bones: allow stretching, lifting, grasping and holding.
  • 足部骨骼：支撑整个人体的重量。
    Leg and foot bones: support the weight of the whole body.
  • 关节是骨头和骨头之间连结的地方，使我们能够活动自如。
    A joint is where one bone joins another, so we can move freely.
  • 身体的关节有：颈椎骨关节、肩关节、肘关节、腕关节、指关节、腰椎骨关节、髋关节、膝关节、踝关节、趾关节。 extra 补充
    Joints in the body: neck (cervical) vertebrae joint, shoulder, elbow, wrist, finger, lower-back (lumbar) vertebrae joint, hip, knee, ankle and toe joints.
  • 肘关节只能让手臂伸直或弯曲，不能转圈。
    The elbow joint only lets the arm straighten or bend; it cannot rotate in a circle.
  • 骑滑板车、玩滑板时要戴护膝等防护装备，保护骨头和关节，避免受伤。
    Wear protective gear such as knee pads when scooting or skateboarding, to protect bones and joints from injury.

2.2 人体的血液循环系统2.2 The Human Circulatory SystemS1 p.13-14, J-2, J-3, 课本 Textbook S6 p.20-29

  • 血液循环系统由心脏、血管和血液组成。
    The circulatory system is made up of the heart, blood vessels and blood.
  • 心脏：把血液推送到肺和全身。
    Heart: pumps blood to the lungs and the whole body.
  • 血管：输送血液到全身。
    Blood vessels: carry blood around the body.
  • 血液：把氧气和营养素输送到全身各部位，同时把二氧化碳和其他废物带走。
    Blood: carries oxygen and nutrients to all parts of the body and takes away carbon dioxide and other waste.
  • 肺：氧气和二氧化碳在这里进行交换。
    Lungs: where oxygen and carbon dioxide are exchanged.
  • 从身体各部位回到心脏和肺的血液，含有较多的二氧化碳；从心脏推送到全身的血液，含有较多的氧气。
    Blood returning from the body to the heart and lungs has more carbon dioxide; blood pumped from the heart to the body has more oxygen.
  • 营养素来自消化系统，由血液输送到全身。
    Nutrients come from the digestive system and are carried by the blood to the whole body.
  • 正常人的心脏每分钟跳动约 60 至 80 次。
    A normal person's heart beats about 60 to 80 times per minute.
  • 如果血液循环系统不能正常运作，身体得不到足够的氧气和营养素，也不能把废物排出体外，最终会导致器官衰竭，甚至死亡。
    If the circulatory system cannot work properly, the body does not get enough oxygen and nutrients and cannot remove waste, which may lead to organ failure and even death.
  • 运动越剧烈，呼吸率和心跳越快。例：跳绳比走路呼吸更快。 extra 补充
    The more strenuous the activity, the faster the breathing rate and heartbeat. e.g. skipping rope makes you breathe faster than walking.
  • 长期骑自行车等运动，可以让静息心率下降，说明肺和心脏更强健。 extra 补充
    Regular exercise such as cycling can lower the resting heart rate, showing a stronger heart and lungs.

2.3 人体各系统之间的关系2.3 Relationship Between Body SystemsS1 p.15, J-3, 课本 Textbook S6 p.30-40

  • 身体的各个系统不是单独工作的，而是互相配合。
    The body systems do not work alone; they work together.
  • 血液循环系统和呼吸系统合作：把氧气输送到身体各部分，同时把二氧化碳排出体外。
    The circulatory system and respiratory system work together: they carry oxygen to all body parts and remove carbon dioxide from the body.
  • 消化系统吸收营养素，通过血液循环带到全身；骨骼系统保护体内的重要器官。
    The digestive system absorbs nutrients, which the circulatory system carries to the whole body; the skeletal system protects important organs inside the body.
  • 吃饭时被噎着：影响呼吸系统和消化系统。
    Choking on food: affects the respiratory and digestive systems.
  • 骨折并肿胀：影响骨骼系统和血液循环系统（周围的血管受损，影响血液流动）。
    A broken bone with swelling: affects the skeletal and circulatory systems (nearby blood vessels are damaged, which affects blood flow).
  • 建筑工人戴安全帽：保护骨骼系统（头骨），避免头部被硬物撞击。
    A construction worker wears a safety helmet: protects the skeletal system (skull) from being hit by hard objects.
  • 在巴士站戴口罩：保护呼吸系统，避免吸入废气。
    Wearing a mask at the bus stop: protects the respiratory system from breathing in exhaust fumes.
